## Rebel Hideout
The group arrives to the rebel hideout. Violet had lost an arm. Form, Feris, and Tarren are also there, so are six other tieflings and two gnome children: Brian and Rebecca. Kaldrake tells them everything except the Morrigan vision, the stained glass, and killing the elf in Fort Bosco.

Violet is getting her arm patched up by Tarren. Feris is gathering the others. Gorm is moving boxes. They seem to be trying to escape. The city is shaking around every minute.

Branwyn talks to Feris, asks what the easiest escape from the city is. He says it would be the front gate, but there might be some resistance. Kaldrake mentions it should just be guards since everyone else is inside. He introduces himself to Feris. Branwyn and Brewster heal.

Brewster tells Tarren about Gwyneth looking for Daphne. Tarren says they’re going up to Domnall Keep. Brewster convinces him to change their plans from getting Isaac elected to going to Whitehall and freeing the slaves. Branwyn asks Violet if she interjects, but she just groans, which isn’t an interjection, so Brewster assumes they’re on board. He goes and grabs the gears from his ydril machine.

Branwyn runs out from the surface, afraid of Mora coming after her. Moiloc and Kaldrake follow her. Moiloc hears Shurima’s voice in her head, telling her to get the right eye of the Advisor of Strength, Corrin the Impaler, who is right outside his house, the manor for the Advisor of Secrets. He is going to take back the castle. Moiloc suggests to the others that doing this may put them in Shurima’s good graces, allowing them to leave through magical means. Kaldrake points out that since they’re elves there are two enemies out there. Branwyn tells them about Mora speaking to her, and tries to express that this is very dangerous, and that Mora likes destroying the things she loves. Kaldrake mentions that this means there are three enemies out there. Moiloc says Shurima might be able to hide her. They realize they forgot Brewster downstairs, Kaldrake volunteers to get him while they think of a plan to kill Corrin. Moiloc confidently suggests she can just fight him.

Brewster agrees to join, since even though the plan involves helping Shurima, it does mean they can kill the Minister of Strength. Kaldrake tells them where the advisors live in Hilltop Manor. Moiloc suggests they check what’s on the map the prisoner gave her on the way. They decide to take the sewers to help clear a way for the Resisance to get out.

On their way down the sewers, they realize all is silent, and the water has stopped flowing. The refugees are following. The group makes it to an exit to the canals below the cathedral, and Moiloc pokes her head out. The sky is black, as if there are dark storm clouds over the whole city. Many people are running through the streets, pouring out of the market district, toward the cathedral, where a group of clerics, including Simon, creating a protective dome of light. Corpse eaters are hammering into the light, unable to get through.

Moiloc and a masked Brewster, which utterly shocks Kaldrake, go to the fountain to check the map. Moiloc kicks in the loose bring, finding a pouch of 300 gold and two 50 piece rubies. They continue down the sewers to the Hilltop Manor. The party emerges, leaving the rest of the Resistance in the sewers. Castle Blackguard is surrounded by a gray fog, with a swirl and a face occasionally appearing, realizing its Lexar.

They all run up the hill. Kaldrake and Brewster feel winded, like their lungs are being crushed. They see that across the ground, the fog pushed out from the castle, beginning to envelop them. Moiloc tries to reach out to Shurima, but something is stopping her from being able to commune with him. Behind them, the city is gone. They are on a hill enveloped by darkness. They pass the manor of the Advisor of Strength, whose door has been kicked in from the inside. Branwyn begins to feel winded at this point as well, as an inhuman cold seeps into her body. The Advisor of Coin’s house is untouched, and they notice magical warding runes on the doorframe.

Brewster tries opening the door to the Minister of Coin’s house, but it doesn’t budge. However, Kaldrake can open it. Bisco is impaled onto the wall with a knife. The house looks like Gwyneth’s house from the original timeline. Kaldrake takes the dagger. Inscribed on the hilt is a symbol of the royal blacksmith of the Kodlak Islands, Donatello Angelo (D. Angelo). There is none of Olidamuru’s smoke in this manor.

Moiloc begins hitting along the wall for a secret passage, and finds something. She hits the wall, which disappears, and finds a box for a ring containing a ring that looks like the Ring of Rot that took Brewster’s finger. However, Brewster identifies that this ring is imbued with a different magic.

Kaldrake enters the drawing room to look for the djinn, and is able to find the same secret area behind the painting, but no lamp.

Moiloc tries to use her hand axe to break through a locked door, but it bounces off, causing her to hit herself. Brewster identifies a glyph on the door that could be deactivated with fire, and Moiloc uses her fire longsword to open the door.

Inside is a library. There is a table with a map, and a dagger is stuck in just north of Whitehall. Brewster finds a rare first edition book of the history of Whitehall. Kaldrake finds a book on the history of the Sootspot family. Branwyn finds a book on herbalism. Moiloc finds a locked jewelry box, and slams it on the ground to break it open. Inside is a pendant which Brewster can identify as magical.

Moiloc finds a completely empty room. Brewster uses Detect Magic and finds a transmutation spell, but doesn’t think he can break it. He also grabs a Detect Magic and Pass Without Trace scroll. Without any way to pass to the next manor without going outside, they head back out to the hill.

They pass the manor for the Advisor of Secrets. No light is coming from the windows. Moiloc gets the feeling they do not want to be near this building in 15 minutes. As they approach the castle, Branwyn feels the effects of the smoke again. The fog shifts into a kaleidoscope of colors. The gate had been smashed in, with four Inquisitors, none of which are Corrin, dead inside.

The door leads into a skinny, winding hallway. They send in Puck first, and he floats through the first hallway before the turn. Moiloc follows, triggering a pressure plate on the floor. A ballista shoots Puck from the end of the hall, bouncing off of him. Puck rounds the corner, and sees a body stuck in spikes which are sticking half-out of the wall. Puck squeezes past, and Moiloc follows, activating another two spike traps from the wall, dodging them both.

Kaldrake finally has room to get off the pressure played and as it raises, the ballista fires again, piercing his armor.

Back in the third hall, Puck begins to walk on the ground to potentially trigger traps. He finds spikes behind where the spikes in the previous hall were, and Moiloc jumps over the same spots the spikes were in the previous hall. She sees Puck at the end of the hall be engulfed in flames, and turn into a gem. Moiloc jumps over the plates to get to the end of the hallway, but triggers a spike trap at the end. She is able to retrieve the gem. Brewster triggers a trap while advancing, almost downing him.

The group decides they probably won’t be able to get through the traps and backtrack to the entrance. Moiloc triggers another two spike traps while she backtracks, and Brewster triggers one.

Kaldrake falls flat on his stomach onto the ballista trap, and the ballista flies over him. The others pass by as he weighs it down, and he army crawls off it, the second ballista flying over them.

The group decides to use Rope Trick to climb up to a third floor window. Inside this room are deep grooves in rows on the floor. Branwyn throws a backpack over the deep groove, and it lands, not triggering anything. She uses Misty Step to pass the other three divots. Kaldrake makes it past the first divot, but falls onto the second divot. A buzzsaw flies across the room and imbeds itself in his side. The mechanism clicks and starts smoking, not able to make it through his armor. He then triggers the next buzzsaw as well, this one deeply gashing into his leg, the buzzsaw remaining operational. On the final buzzsaw, he slaps his backpack on the ground to trigger the mechanism, and jumps through after it passes. As he lands, he bumps Branwyn through the door at the end of the room, which clicks shut and locks. Brewster sees a small wire between the pressure plates, and cuts it. The pressure plate becomes deactivated.

Branwyn sees writing by each of three doors in the room she is shoved into. One reads Dreams, one reads War, one reads You. She feels better, somehow becoming healed, as she enters the Dreams room. As she enters, she falls asleep. The door back to Kaldrake opens, and Kaldrake sees Branwyn is no longer inside. They decide to try and not split the party, and choose the door they believe Branwyn would have chosen. Brewster disarms the other traps.

Kaldrake enters next, seeing the options of Time, Death, and Dog. He chooses dog, knowing it is what Branwyn would have chosen. Inside is a field with a dog. Brewster heals himself and enters next. He sees Hand, Beach, and Door. He thinks Branwyn would have chosen Hand. Moiloc sees Town, Devil, and Moon. Moiloc disregards what Branwyn would have wanted, and enters Moon. Inside she sees a room in the Kodlak Islands, and her mother, giving birth, and a midwife preparing water.

Kaldrake is in a beautiful field, with the smell of oranges, like what used to grow in Martin’s Isle. A large fence surrounds the field, and a young dog is in the center. He holds out a hand to the dog, which the dog sniffs. It begins snarling, and grows larger, and larger, becoming taller than Kaldrake. Looking out, he sees a path leading away from the fence, and somehow knows it was the path he took to get here. As he takes a step back, the dog snaps at him. Kaldrake puts his hand out again, asking what he wants, and the dog bites his wrist. Kaldrake strikes back, and the dog disappears, an elf taking its place. Kaldrake recognizes him as Balthazar. He tell him it’s all his fault. Kaldrake reappears in a circular room, realizing that he doesn’t know if his father was dead before the first reality reset. He wonders if his father’s death really was his fault.

Branwyn is three years old, wandering through a forest with a picnic basket. The trees appear gnarled and twisted. She calls out to her papa, and hears the crunching on leaves, who calls out to her. She follows the voice, and the wind picks up. It gets colder as the sky darkens, and it begins to snow. She walks for some time, not finding her father. She drops the basket and begins running after her father’s direction. All of the sudden, there is an old woman in front of her, with a hooked nose and a coat of furs, saying she will help her find her father. Branwyn gets confused, saying she was older when this happened. The woman reaches out, brushing her hair, saying she also wasn’t blonde. She puts a finger on her forehead, and her bones turn to liquid, and she collapses. She wakes up, in her childhood bed, smelling breakfast her father is cooking, before appearing in the room with Kaldrake.

Brewster sees a carved throne in the shape of a palmed hand, and sits in it. It turns below him, and begins to grip and squeeze him. The air is forced out of his lungs. All of a sudden he is back in Whitehall, tied in a torture chair. The same room from after his first failed escape attempt. His hands are bound behind his back. He can see deep gouges, his left eye is swollen shut. He begins to panic, trying to get out,  breaking his wrists, falling onto the cold stone floor. Someone opens the door and mocks him for falling over, laughing at him, offering to nail his feet to the floor. He begins to cry, before reappearing with Kaldrake and Branwyn.

Moiloc is in a hut with her mother, giving birth. Outside, she hears wet slapping. She pushes open the flap to the tent, and sees two fish men sneaking up to the hut. She draws her sword to attack, but her sword phases through them. They enter, and Moiloc hears sounds of battle. The midwife is almost cut in half, but her mother has a sword planted in the head of one of the Sahaguin. Moiloc sees her take out the sword from him, kills the other. She falls onto the ground, and gives birth. She picks up the baby and cradles her. A devil appears behind her in a puff of smoke, reaches down, takes her hand, and she goes limp. The devil disappears as her father rushes into the room.

Kaldrake reads the room and asks if everyone is okay, especially Brewster. Brewster says he was pepper sprayed. Kaldrake realizes this isn’t true but doesn’t say anything. The group proceeds through one of the two doors in the room, and descend the stairwell. The next room is large and barren. Steam is rising off of four sunken pools, filled with pile of bones. Inside each pit are two levers under the steaming water. One pool has the left lever flipped.

Moiloc tells everyone to hear her out. She raises the dead, causing the bones to turn into a skeleton. Brewster notices bones coalesced around the left lever of one of the pools, and Infernal scribbles around each pool. The group also sees four torches on the wall, one by each pool, and the one by the pool with the pulled lever is lit. Moiloc has the skeleton go to the pool with bones nested around the left lever, and has it pull the right, triggering three of the torches, turning two on, and one off. They undo this, and pull a lever in the pool adjacent to all three unlit torches, lighting them all, and opening the door.

The next room has a stage and eight pillars. The eight pillars have the same images inscribed on them as in the Cathedral, but everyone has Olidamuru’s face. On the stage is a blindfolded human woman, kneeling with her hands bound. In front of her is a dagger. Kaldrake and Brewster climb onto the stage, and under the dagger is a note that says “STAB HER.” The woman doesn’t seem to respond to their questions, but Brewster confirms she’s alive. Beside the stage are staircases that lead to the stage, and a side door each. Brewster notices another difference on the pillar, instead of 16 figures coalescing into two, it’s just 8 figures, then 16, then 9. The final one is not arranged around a globe, but four on the left, five on the right. Brewster pinches her, but she doesn’t react. He cuts her hand free with the dagger. They take the woman with them and return to the circular room, going the other direction this time.

They enter into a room splitting into five hallways. One hallway has scorch marks. They go back and raise three more skeletons. They send the skeletons down one at a time. One hallway crumbles beneath the skeleton. One is crushed by a stone wall that juts from the wall. The skeletons make it through the other two. Moiloc goes through the middle one, and makes it through, so the others follow her.

The next room is full of swinging pendulums. Moiloc casts Fireball into the center of the room immediately, destroying the mechanism, as pendulums and gears fall from the ceiling.

They enter into a large throne room. The throne is atop a long staircase, with two Inquisitors staring them down. The bodies of corpse eaters lie on the staircase, and the Inquisitors look beaten up. Kaldrake takes the wand of Fireball from Moiloc, hiding it in his armor. He introduces himself, saying he’s looking for his sister, and begins walking up the stairs. The Inquisitors begin walking down the stairs. They place their hands on her and begin to restrain her. Branwyn demands them to unhand her, and they spit at her.

Branwyn shoots at one of the Inquisitors, clipping him in the knee, causing him to drop, and release Kaldrake. She shoots him again, hitting him in the eye, killing him. She shoots the other one between the gaps in his armor, causing him to let go of Kaldrake. Kaldrake Uses the Greater Invisibility spell on himself, and climbs the stairs, to see Corrin the Impaler, a huge figure, on the throne. His left eye is missing, and his right eye is glowing yellow. Kaldrake’s bones chill in fear when meeting his eye. There are two additional Inquisitors by him.

The other Inquisitor from the stairs hits one of Moiloc’s skeletons. The Inquisitors from by Corrin descend the stairs to the others, as does Corrin. He raises his arm, and a tube is visible from his wrist, and casts Fireball. The Inquisitors all burn away, as do Moiloc’s skeletons, and Brewster collapses. Moiloc uses Lay On Hands to revive Brewster. She also casts Bow of Enmity. Brewster motions for Moiloc to get out of the way, and casts Melf’s Acid Arrow on Brewster. Corrin impales Brewster, rushing down the stairs and stabbing him with his sword.

Branwyn backs up and shoots twice at him, and he deflects both arrows with his sword. Kaldrake, still invisible, runs down the stairs and lunges at Corrin, piercing him. Corrin growls at him. Corrin drinks a bottle from his belt, healing himself. Corrin emits a yellow fog, paralyzing everyone, and attacks Moiloc. Moiloc drinks a healing potion, and ignites her fire long sword, slicing back, causing him to howl in pain, yelling slurs, and enraging Moiloc. Kaldrake swings his battle axe at Corrin, slicing him as well. In a flurry of moves, he downs both Moiloc and Brewster.

Branwyn shoots Corrin, causing him to sag to the ground, but her second arrow misses. Kaldrake readies himself as Corrin is on his knees, and decapitates Corrin with his battle axe. Branwyn uses Cure Wounds on Brewster and Moiloc. Moiloc uses Second Wind.

Moiloc plucks the eye out of Corrin’s head. Brewster roots through his body, taking 75 gold and an enchanted great sword that increases the wielder’s size to large. They split the gold, including the gold from the Inquisitor’s stash, among each other.

Moiloc feels Shurima on top of the staircase, and ascends. She sees Shurima, but just the bandages, hollow. He raises a hand, takes the eye, and puts it into his own head. He beckons toward stairs behind the throne. The left stairs will take them to Lothric, the right will take them to the Queen. Shurima disappears. They decide to avoid the Queen, since she is likely in the castle, and leave down the other staircase, appearing in the fields outside Lothric. The dome around the Cathedral has collapsed. The forest outside the front gate is on fire.

As the group leaves the woods, a lone flamingo approaches the party.
