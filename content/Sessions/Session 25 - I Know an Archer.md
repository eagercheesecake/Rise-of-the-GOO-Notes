### Logline
*Moiloc and Brewster drink coffee. Branwyn fends off assassins.*
### Synopsis
Jessie disappears. Brewster gains a third eye with True Sight, and Moiloc begins to float, thanks to Schmebulock's stew. Branwyn finds Monty in town, under a new alias. Branwyn is attacked by triton assassins sent to kidnap Moiloc, but fends them off. Ulysses invites Branwyn to a meeting. Branwyn reunites with the group. She gives Moiloc the pendant from Mora, and Moiloc has a vision of Gwyneth, Mora, Daphne, and Sister Marie in Whitehall.
# Day 56
### Silver Wreath Inn
[[Lothar (Brewster) Nash|Brewster]] wakes up to find [[Jessie Harkness|Jessie]] gone. The bed seems unslept on. Burnt into the sheets is the eye symbol. There is nothing on Brewster’s bed. It seems to have only been there for a few hours. He proceeds down to the bar. The mess has been cleaned up. He asks [[Schmebulock]], who is at the bar, if he saw Jessie leave. He says he just woke up an hour ago, and was thrown out the window last night. [[Martin Sootspot|Martin]] also wakes up, and, leaving a note for [[Helen Berestova|Helen]], goes to get breakfast.

Brewster takes the sheets and leaves the inn. He transforms into the same child he was in Whitehall, and asks Shmebulock if he’s seen the eye symbol or knows anyone who has. He has not. Shmebulock generously gives him a viscous sludge, and Brewster sees Martin at a nearby booth. He joins him, and tries to convince him to eat the sludge. Martin unwaveringly refuses, so Brewster tries it on his own. He is racked with pain, as a bulging third eye sprouts from his forehead. It gives him True Sight, and he ties a bandana over it.

[[Moiloc Kodlaksdottër|Moiloc]] wakes up, and finds [[Goro Hattori|Goro]] is gone, but his stuff is still in the room. She proceeds downstairs, and Brewster convinces her to try the sludge. She begins to levitate an inch off the ground. She tries to skip around and do a backflip, but Martin had a divination that she was going to fall flat, and she does.

Brewster asks Moiloc where Goro is, and fills the others in on Jessie’s disappearance and the symbol. The pair go to check Goro’s bedsheets, but there is no symbol. Martin sees Goro outside the window, and stops him to tell him Moiloc and Brewster are investigating his bedsheets. He asks why, but she doesn’t know. Goro says he’s shopping and will be back soon. Martin asks if there are any good shops nearby, and gets a long talk from Goro about a nearby blacksmith.

Martin reunites with the others in the inn, telling them about Goro. Brewster catches up with him, asking about the sheets, but Goro doesn’t know anything. Goro says he’s sitting around and shopping, and has full faith that the rest of the group can handle this, and he will rejoin them when they’re done. Brewster comes back into the inn, blushing, causing Martin and Moiloc to wonder about Brewster’s sexuality. They decide to explore town, and Martin checks in on Helen to ask if she wants to come. She doesn’t, and Martin gives her ten gold to shop for herself. She asks for more, and Martin gives her ten more.

The trio explore the city. They find a market, the blacksmith, [[Bartholomew|Bart's]] Apothecary, [[Monty MacMasters|Mark Milkman's]] Traveling Shoppe, and the Silver Wreath General Store. They each buy enough rations to get two people through the journey to Whitehall.

[[Branwyn of Coryphea|Branwyn]] wakes up, and proceeds to the main area. She sees Shmebulock, and recognizes him as the man who was thrown out the window the night before. She also notices the same [[Ulysses|man]] in a wide-brimmed hat, brooding in the corner. She asks Shmebulock to point her to the market.

She tries to size up the man in the hat. He seems dangerous. He’s a middle-aged, clean-shaven human, covered in pouches, fitted with many blades. She guesses that he’s a bounty hunter. She locks eyes with him, and feels a compulsion to go talk to him. She thinks he tried to charm her, somehow, without moving. She leaves the inn, and the man’s gaze follows her as she leaves. As she proceeds through the town, she notices the man following her.
### Domnhall Keep
Branwyn stops by a stand selling grilled thirstwater, and buys some, gaining a sense of poetic justice as she eats it. She finds a regal carriage, with a sign that says Mark Milkman’s traveling shop. She gets in line, but sees the man pretending to shop a few stalls down. She sees a hairless tabaxi man, with a turban covering his ears. He welcomes her, asking what she needs. He magically summons a map for her, for 15 gold. She recognizes him, but can’t quite place from where. Suddenly, she clocks him, asking if he has a deal for her. His smile breaks for a moment, but he insists he has no idea what she’s talking about. She tries to haggle with him, and gets it down to 8 gold. He asks if she needs anything else, and she says she wants to impart a message to someone. He creates a parchment and quill to take it. She says if she ever makes a deal with him again, and he screws her like he did the last time, then they’re going to have a problem. She walks away.

She unfurls the map, and sees it’s a map of the Orkus Badlands. She sees that she is in Domnhall Keep, and it seems like it would be a good place for her party members to stop on the way to Whitehall. Branwyn sees the man again, and proceeds into the nearby general goods store. As she enters, she feels a human child run past her. She feels for her coin pouch, and finds it’s still there. She feels around again, and finds [[Mora|Mora’s]] amulet is gone. Branwyn chases after him as he weaves through the crowd. He proceeds to the church, and as Branwyn follows, the crowds begin to thin. The child darts down an alleyway. As Branwyn turns the corner, she doesn’t see the child. She continues down the alley, but trips. As she turns, she sees the child, dead, on the ground, seeming to be what tripped her. In his hand is the pouch that once held the amulet, empty. Feeling like this may be a trap, she turns to leave the alley. Crouched upon nearby rooftops, she sees two humanoids, armed, watching her, seemingly waiting for her to leave the alley.

Branwyn uses Rope Trick to climb up to the roof. The two figures draw their swords and rush at her.

> [!danger] Fight
> Branwyn goes to the back of the roof, and tries to cast Spike Growth on one of the enemies, but they cast Counterspell and the spell fizzles. The assassin dashes to Branwyn’s side. The other assassin jumps over the gap between the roofs, attempting to corner her. Branwyn Misty Steps away, and tries to shoot one of the assassins. One shot hits, the other doesn’t. That assassin runs up to her, but is unable to land a hit. The unscathed assassin also runs up to her, heavily slashing her.
> 
> Branwyn uses Hunters Mark on the hurt assassin, and swings at him with her hand axe, biting into him. The unharmed assassin is unable to hit, but the bloodied one gets a swipe in. Branwyn slices into his side, cutting deeply into him, pushing his corpse off of her axe with her foot before turning to the other assassin, who turns to run away. Branwyn takes the opportunity to slice into his leg, causing him to fall off of the roof, and continue running away. Branwyn tries to shoot him, missing the first one, but the other arrow sinks into his back, causing him to drop onto the ground, dead.

Branwyn searches the assassins. On the assassin on the roof, she finds the amulet, as well as gold. She finds that their curved long swords are magical; but she can’t discern the type of magic. She takes the sword, but sees a crowd forming around the other assassin, and drops off the roof in the other direction and runs off. Branwyn sneaks to the bathhouse.

Meanwhile, at the blacksmith, they meet blacksmith Steve. Moiloc shows him her fire longsword. He thinks it looks cool, and asks where she got it. For some reason, she refuses to tell him. She asks to get her longsword enhanced, but it’s too pricey for her, so she offers her services instead. He says people have been stopping his caravans from arriving. Brewster asks for a little money to buy armor, but is shut down.

As they walk off, they see a body fall from the rooftop. Martin and Brewster run to it to grab it first. They put their hands on it at the same, and Martin says he’ll give Brewster the gold he wanted if he lets him take the sword. He asks for more, and eventually wears him down. As the rest of the crowd, Brewster Identifies the sword as Sentinel, a magic +2 katana. Once per short rest, it can cast Counterspell.

Moiloc casts Invisibility, and roots around in the assassin’s pockets. She takes it, and returns to the others. Brewster’s True Sight is able to read the paper, and it says “Moiloc Moona Kodlaksdötter.” He tells Moiloc, and asks if all adventurers get hits taken out on them. Martin says good adventurer’s don’t get hits taken out, and Moiloc says people have been trying to kill her family for a while. On the back it says “bring back alive.” Investigating the body, they see they have blue skin. Moiloc lifts the mask, and recognizes it as a triton, a race which had been at war with the Kodlak Islands for a while. However, they had been allies for decades.

Moiloc announces that she only knows one person who uses a bow: Branwyn. Brewster, confused, pulls out his crossbow. Moiloc explains to Martin who Branwyn is. Martin recognizes the description, and says he saw her be taken away by a monster. Brewster give the description of Mora’s monster form, and she confirms it was her. Martin says Mora charmed him, and Brewster offers to check if there are any spells currently on him, but he declines. They proceed to the general store.

Branwyn leaves the bathhouse, having washed the blood off, and notices the man in the hat again, at a table by a tea stand. Branwyn joins him, sitting across from him. He doesn’t say anything. Branwyn asks for his name, but he just takes a sip of his tea, and shakes his head. He reaches into his jacket, and hands her a piece of paper, reading “10 PM,” with a picture of the church. He walks away. Branwyn proceeds back to the general store. Upon arriving, she recognizes Moiloc and Brewster.

The three are arguing over what winter clothing to wear, with Martin complaining that he just gave a lot of money to Brewster. Moiloc asks the shopkeeper what furs are available. They all buy coats for themselves, and Moiloc buys an extra for Branwyn. After that, she realizes Branwyn is somehow back in the shop. Brewster introduces Martin, and they tell her about Kaldrake. Branwyn tells Brewster about her upcoming meeting with the man in the hat. Branwyn says she is going to have Moiloc buy her an ale, and Brewster mentions the sludge, revealing his eye, and activating his True Sight.

Around Branwyn’s neck, Brewster sees ethereal thorns. He recognizes them from one of [[Daphne|Daphne’s]] books. It was an ancient book that he only managed to see a couple of times. It’s an undetectable enchantment magic that possesses the user, and is undetectable if the person who the magic is affecting has consumed the flesh of whatever cast the spell on them. It hasn’t been used in millennia.
### Silver Wreath Inn
They proceed to the inn, and Branwyn secretly tells Brewster about her meeting with the bounty hunter. They pay for another night. They fill each other in. Branwyn tosses the pendant to Moiloc. Brewster attempts to Identify it, but gets nothing. As Moiloc puts it on, her eyes glaze over. Martin casts Detect Thoughts on Moiloc as he notices this.

Moiloc sees a flash of images: mountains, snow, trees, caves, tunnels, gnomes, shackles, stone, a large pit, nothingness, a purple crystal floating over a city. She hears [[Shurima the Corpsemaker|Shurima]] whisper that he needs it. She sees the town of Whitehall, an elvish woman in white ([[Gwyneth Sootspot|Gwyneth]]), a woman who looks like an older Branwyn (Mora), a gnome woman ([[Daphne]]), and a human woman with a shaved head and a scar over her eye ([[Sister Marie the Confessor]]) - then she sees those final images repeat twice. Martin feels pain rack his head as he tries to read Moiloc’s thoughts, but is able to see them.

Moiloc comes back from the vision to see Branwyn ranting about Shurima not rescuing [[Charlotte]]. Branwyn lays out the whole story. Moiloc describes the vision, and they are able to identify the people in the vision as Gwyneth, Mora, Daphne, and Sister Marie the Confessor. Martin adds in the bit about Shurima saying he needs it. Brewster fills Branwyn in about the thorns visible via True Sight. Branwyn and Brewster also mention [[Maokai]] and [[Eru]].

Brewster goes and gets food while Branwyn fills them in about the bounty hunter. Brewster brings back the food, and channels Identify through [[Puck Nash|Puck]]. Martin has the same magical signature as Kaldrake.
