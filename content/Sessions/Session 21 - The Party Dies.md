### Logline
*The party tries to uncover the reason for mysterious circumstances in Col Man'Dar.*
### Synopsis
The group enters Col Man'Dar. They meet an old woman who offers them housing in exchange for picking up medicine. They proceed to the apothecary, where they meet a woman who claims her husband, John, has gone missing, and didn't show up for work at the mill. While looking for John, the group occasionally sees through some sort of illusion, seeing the town covered in blood. After being pointed to a clearing in the forest that John frequents, the group finds a hydra, and the entire party is downe while trying to escape.d while trying to escape.
# Day 42
### Col Man'Dar
As the group approaches Col Man’Dar, around 7pm, [[Moiloc Kodlaksdottër|Moiloc]] hears a song, but as she tunes in to try to listen to it, it’s gone. She notices the nearby crops as browning, as if dehydrated. The ground is starting to dry up. However, the island across one fork of the Velka River looks fine. Moiloc knocks on the door of a nearby farmhouse. A half-elf with forcibly rounded ears answers. Moiloc asks where the inn is, and he says they stay at the farm or across the river. The half-elf says he can ask his mistress if she’s taking visitors, and leaves them on the porch. [[Lothar (Brewster) Nash|Brewster]] sees various non-humans being enslaved. He says he feels suspicious of the location, but the others say it would be suspicious if they just left. After about five minutes, the half-elf returns, saying they can come in, but their slaves should stay outside. Moiloc and Brewster enter.

They are led through the main hall. Moiloc takes a step forward and hears a squelch, but only sees wooden floors. She feels something rub off on her foot, like she’s tracking mud, very similar to the cave full of viscera from Dunn. Meanwhile, outside, [[Kaldrake Sootspot|Kaldrake]] and [[Branwyn of Coryphea|Branwyn]] start to feel like someone is watching them. Branwyn notices one of the slaves are planting seeds, which is strange for this late in the season. Brewster and Moiloc enter a lavish bedroom on the third floor. A human is lying, still on the bed. A very thin old woman is in a chair nearby. Moiloc says she is a representative, heading to Whitehall, and the woman begins coughing. She apologizes, saying she and her husband are very sick. Brewster looks to the bed to confirm the husband is breathing. Moiloc apologizes, saying she shouldn’t lodge with someone who is so sick, the woman says if they get some medicine from the apothecary, she’ll give them some gold and let them stay there. Moiloc accepted and they leave the house, collect the others, and proceed to the nearby bridge.

Kaldrake mentions that he feels like they’re being watched. Moiloc quietly suggests that they proceed to the apothecary, but try to get out of there as fast as possible. As they proceed, they see [[Skalazar]] on board a fishing vessel, hauling some fish. They fill Brewster in on who he is, saying he might know what happened to [[Goro Hattori|Goro]], who could be an asset to the team. They walk to the ship, and some fish stands yell at them on the way to buy from their stalls. Moiloc buys some fish for the group, asking a fish seller about the town. She says not to go near the farms with their non-human companions. She also mentions someone at the lumber mill trying to sell some horses, but she drives a hard bargain.

Moiloc goes up the head of the ship, asking about Skalazar. He says he’s a free man, working for them. There is a house near the dock where the dock workers stay, and they seem to only be here seasonally. They approach Skalazar, who recognizes Kaldrake and skittles over to him. He shows Kaldrake all three gold he’s made. Skalazar explains that Goro took them there after Lothric went down. [[Belnades]] kept going north to join the Rebellion. Skalazar says he’s hoping to get promoted to the trout ship.

As they pass the church, they notice an armed Inquisitor in front of the church. Brewster notices it’s odd that the Inquisitor is armed with a sword. They enter the apothecary, and the woman inside looks disheveled, as if she had been crying. She says she’s looking for her husband, James, who never arrived for work at the lumber mill this morning. Moiloc offers to help, and the woman gives a description of James, with a scar on his elbow.

Moiloc asks for some healing before they go. She leads them into the next room, and there is an empty crib. Brewster notices she is making the potions with pretty good ingredients. Branwyn notices all the ingredients in the room are good, and she seems either well-off, some being imported from the Orkus Badlands. She also says she delivers babies for people. Branwyn realizes she seems to forget what she did for the traveling traders next door. Branwyn notices the window is open, but when she brings it up, she turns and it is somehow closed. Brewster casts Detect Magic, noticing an enchantment on everyone in the room. She gives everyone a greater healing potion.

The group enters the nearby church to look for James. As they enter, Branwyn, for a moment, sees everything splattered with blood, and a pair of black eyes. When she blinks, it’s back to normal, with no blood, and no eyes. Kaldrake prays to [[Eru]] for safe travels, and feels at ease. Moiloc prays to [[Shurima the Corpsemaker|Shurima]], saying she doesn’t know what’s happening in the town. She feels a breeze blow past her, and feels like she should check the fields by the farm. Branwyn taps Brewster on the shoulder, and they leave the church. Branwyn tells Brewster was she say, and they consider something luring people here to kill them. Brewster knows very few creatures that could do a mass enchantment like this. Branwyn tries to convince Brewster to help free the people from the enchantment, asking if he would want someone to help him if he were in this situation. Brewster says the humans wouldn’t do the same, pointing out the slaves they saw. Brewster uses Identify, but can’t find any spells affecting him.

The group proceeds to the lumber mill. Goro is hauling a cart in the distance. Branwyn and Kaldrake, for a split second, see a long, pale-white serpent-like being in the trees, with a human face, square teeth, and fish scales. They also hear soft music. Kaldrake waves Goro over, and they reunite. Brewster mentions that Goro looks familiar, and they are able to piece together that Brewster knows Goro’s sister, [[Haru Hattori|Haru]]. Branwyn plugs her ears, trying to block out the music. Brewster uses Magical Tinkering to make an object emit music. Neither seems to dispel the magic. They mention that they are looking for horses, and he points them inside the mill. They enter the mill, not noticing anything out of the ordinary.

They ask the woman in the mill, Olga, for horses, and she says she’ll sell them for 200 gold each. They ask if they can do something for her instead, and she says if they bring her John, then she’ll give them the horses. Brewster asks if she sells lumber, and she says that she does, seeming to imply someone else is the salesman, but not saying his name. Brewster buys a plank for 15 gold. The woman makes him sign a contract saying he won’t buy wood for construction purposes at any other mill. Brewster signs “Brewster,” since it isn’t his legal name. She says to pick up the wood from Vermin, the nearby gnome. Brewster asks if that’s his real name, and if she named him, and she just says “what do you think?” Kaldrake laughs at this, and Brewster says his name is Stinky, high-fiving the woman.

They proceed to Vermin, who gives them a plank, and says John is sometimes at a nearby clearing. They camp out about a mile north of the village, noticing that it’s a bit noisier, with the sounds of birds.
# Day 43
### Col Man'Dar
The sound of birds are gone. Brewster hears a recording he left: “They’re watching us in the trees, I see them, I see them, it’s in the trees.” He is whispering, hoarse, and terrified. Branwyn searches the forest, but doesn’t see anything in the trees. As Kaldrake is putting away his sleeping bag, he sees a film on the bottom of the sleeping bag. Kaldrake thinks it looks like something out it in its mouth. Kaldrake screams and throws up. Branwyn smells the saliva, and feels a bit faint. Brewster asks if Branwyn remembers her name, trying to see if Mora made her forget. Branwyn doesn’t remember her original last name, but it seems to be by choice.

The group proceeds to the clearing in the woods. It’s perfectly circular. In the center of the clearing is a small crater. As Brewster approaches it, [[Helen Berestova]] stops him. Brewster gives her paper and she writes that they’re watching them, in the trees. Moiloc sees the fish-like creature encircling the clearing, looking at them with its long neck over the treetops, smiling at them. Branwyn approaches the crater, and inside, sees a cracked-open egg, looking like a chicken egg, and she gets a feeling it’s from another plane. She tries to pick up the egg, and as soon as she touches the egg. Everyone sees the creature appear, with a body that starts human, but is stretched to the shape of a serpent, covered in fishy scales. Two small hands poke out of its sides midway, and at the top is a head, teeth sharpened into blades, biting Branwyn, putting her to sleep.

> [!danger] Fight
> Moiloc casts Inflict Wounds on the abhorrent hydra. The attack seems to be somewhat repelled. Brewster uses Melf’s Acid Arrow. Puck moves by Helen to protect her. The hydra hoists Branwyn into the air, slamming her back into the ground. Kaldrake hits with a Distracting Strike, and hits again, and it seems to be bleeding white. Branwyn gets up, and stabs it. Moiloc heals Branwyn with Lay on Hands. Brewster uses Flaming Sphere, ramming it into the hydra. Branwyn uses Misty Step to get away, and shoots it twice.
> 
> The hydra unhinges its jaw, spitting at Brewster, Branwyn, Puck, and Helen, but Puck flies above it. The hydra takes more acid damage from Melf’s Acid Arrow. Kaldrake swings at the hydra a couple more times. Moiloc uses her fire long sword and cuts at the hydra, cutting through it like butter, its guts pooling out onto everyone. Puck and Branwyn hop away. The hydra is reduced to nothing but a sack of skin, but then starts to inflate. Everyone runs away as it fills itself with air, its wound sewing itself up, and its head splitting, multiple times. Wings sprout out of it. Moiloc and Branwyn run back, and Moiloc uses Eldritch Blast on it, but it bounces off.

Brewster recognizes the creature as the abhorrent hydra. The creature they can see is more of a limb, controlled by a brain, which must be fed at regular intervals with humanoid creatures. The hydra cannot be killed, the more damage you do, the bigger it gets. The heart can be anywhere, but is likely within the town. It is from the astral plane. The heart requires a lot of nutrients, draining the life essence of anything nearby. Brewster yells at the rest of the party that they need to leave. As they run, they feel themselves sinking into the ground. The hydra attacks all of them as they run, and black out.
