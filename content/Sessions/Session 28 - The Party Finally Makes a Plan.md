| [[Session 27 - The Party Sins]] |              |
### Logline
*Branwyn wakes up in Mora's cabin, with Mora's newest victim. Brewster, Moiloc, Martin, and the others make their way to Domnhall Keep.*
### Synopsis
The group returns to the church. Ulysses is still inside, taking a nap. Martin wakes him up. They ask if he wants to come, and he says he will, but it will take him two days to finish the ritual he is working on. Unable to get another divine artifact, Brewster gives him the mask to make into bullets, but asks to watch as he makes it. He manages to stay awake all night and take good notes on how to get a god-killing bullet, shedding a single tear as the mask is melted down.

Moiloc gets Goro to agree to join them on their journey, and Martin does the same with Helen, giving her a gemstone. Martin has a restless night full of terrible dreams.

On their way back to the Keep, a courier give Martin a letter from Maelon Switt saying he has one more day until Maelon sends his assassin. William meets them, saying Billiam could not join them, since their mom said he had to watch over the farm. He asks who the leader is, and Moiloc volunteers herself. When she realizes he will be following her, she passes him to Branwyn, saying that since she’s an archer he can better learn from her. Martin gets another coin from under someone’s pillow in the barracks, seeing Donnell keep on the map. They pick up Tarren. Brewster gets some disguise kits and a wagon covering to replace the torn one. Branwyn goes to a fletcher and finds some Sylvan arrows of returning and some fire arrows. She also buys a +1 crossbow. Moiloc buys horse armor using the gems from the chapel, and goes to the wagon to pray to Shurima.

Martin finds a locked chest in the stables, and pries it open, breaking the lock. Inside is clothing, but also a bag of coins, including another copper coin with a portion of map. He continues to a chapel, where he finds a priest with the last coin in his hands, slipping it into his robe. Martin uses Mage Hand to pull out the coin. He goes to a table in the library to assemble the coins, which give him a vision of a set of stairs in the desert, gaining the knowledge on how to navigate to it.

Tarren and Moiloc ride the horses, while Brewster takes a nap in the wagon with the others. On their way to the town, they see a bandit on a wolf, remembering they were warned about a bandit camp nearby. They roll up the flaps of the cover for Martin to keep a watch out, and William gets excited about the prospects of a battle. They are soon surrounded by bandits, and come to a stop.

The group creates a 7-step plan. They tie up the bandit and leave him.
## Step 1: Get In
The group approaches the camp, parking the wagon nearby. They wait for the party within the camp to die down, and Brewster and Moiloc carry the two zombies on the ruined wagon cover to the gate. Martin sneaks in, under Invisibility, while Moiloc attempts to convince drunken guards that the zombies are immensely injured bandits. Inside, they see a massive bonfire with drunken bandits around it. There are a few people in the mess hall, and a guard by the warehouse. They also see about five captives in the cages.
## Step 2: Check the Gate
The gate is guarded, so the group decides not to close it. They have a plan B to block that entrance. Brewster casts Flaming Sphere on the gate, blocking it.
## Step 3: Fire
The guards near the gate drop, aflame, and the gate and nearby walls begin to spread. Martin casts Fireball on the nearby mess hall and the surrounding tents. Some bandits sluggishly begin going to the fire to investigate. The zombies walk onto the bonfire, and walk, flaming, onto the nearby tents. Moiloc uses her fire longsword to light tents on fire. Brewster notices some bandits putting out the flames, and uses Control Flames to make the flames brighter, and green.
## Step 4: Damage
Branwyn, Tarren, William, and Goro enter through the gate near the boss. Most get in unnoticed, but a guard notices Branwyn, and grabs her. She attempts to stab him, but he stops her, slapping her onto the ground. He stabs her once, but she blocks the second attack, getting up, and casting Hunter’s Mark on him. He attempts to stab her again with his longsword, again landing one of his two hits. Branwyn stabs him with her dagger twice. The guard is heavily wounded, and angry. He slashes at her legs, and her knees buckle, but she stays standing, although weakly. Branwyn slips her dagger into his chest, killing him.
## Step 5: Break-In
Goro breaks into the warehouse, and he, Tarren, Branwyn, and William, continue into the boss’s room. Brewster weaves his way to the captive’s cage. He sees a large, one-eyed bandit in the boss’s area, stuffing treasures into a bag. He also sees a lizard folk, chained and muzzled. Brewster tries to pick the lock of the captives’ cage, but the locks break. Martin decided he doesn’t need to cast his other Fireballs as originally planned due to how quickly the fire is spreading, and runs, but trips and falls into a bandit camp. They pull their swords, but Martin casts Invisibility immediately. They look confused for a moment, before noticing the fire, and running off. Moiloc continues to spread the fires.

## Step 6: The Boss
Branwyn, Tarren, and Goro quietly sneak into the boss’s room, but William prepares his bow, screams “For the People!” He then whiffs a shot on the boss, who orders his guards to kill the intruders.

## Step 7: The Captives
Brewster manages to pick the lock from the captives’ cages, leading them out the door by the boss. Martin and Moiloc continue out the back entrance, seeing charred corpses. They manage to escape, but meet three bandits who draw their swords. Moiloc tells them it’s not worth it, drawing her own fire longsword. They nod to each other, draw their weapons, and run off.
## Step 8: The Warehouse
Brewster uses Control Flame to stave off the fire a bit. Branwyn runs around the walls and opens up some of the cages for the wolves. She opens the kennels that haven’t caught fire, saving about eight wolves. Three of them run off, the other five sniff around her expectantly.

They return to the wagon and count their riches. They have five crates of spices worth 250g each from the warehouse, and an extra 1000g from the boss’s haul. They give 200g to the captives, who head back to the Keep. Helen asks Martin for some more gold.

They load back up into the wagon, with Brewster hopping on a horse. Branwyn tells William off for being loud and blowing their cousin in the camp. William apologizes, embarrassed.
