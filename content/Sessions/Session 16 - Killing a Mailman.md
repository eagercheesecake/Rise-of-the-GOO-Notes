### Logline
*Brewster begins construction on the ydril machine. Branwyn and Moiloc get some mail. Kaldrake suffers the consequences of jumping into Gwyneth's pit.*
### Synopsis
Branwyn and Moiloc go shopping for the Resistance. They come across a mailman, and Branwyn disguises herself as an Inquisitor and steals an invitation to the Queen's tournament. Brewster builds the ydril machine. Branwyn and Moiloc interrogate the Inquisitor prisoners. Kaldrake has a vision of Gulvayas, who with the aid of Morrigan, took over the role of Martin and killed the High Mages, including Morrigan, absorbing their power. After the vision, Kaldrake appears in the ethereal plane, and after wandering for some time, finds a pair of portals. Going through one, he appears in Sabra, which is close to Lothric, and follows a pilgrim who is also heading to Lothric.
# Day 33
## Rebel Hideout
[[Branwyn of Coryphea|Branwyn]] and [[Moiloc Kodlaksdottër|Moiloc]] wake up first. They hear [[Dr. Nubgrub]] working on something in his lab, and proceed into the meeting room and see [[Feris Weal|Feris]] awake and working as well. Moiloc asks if there’s anything they can help with, and Feris mentions the two Inquisitor prisoners, and the need for someone to buy food for the day from the market. Moiloc agrees to help with both items, and Feris gives them some gold to buy the rations. He also asks for a new pair of boots, since his current pair has a hole. Branwyn mentions to Moiloc that she has a knack for getting people to talk about themselves, and they decide to try to interrogate the Inquisitors first.

Moiloc wakes up the Inquisitors, who do not turn to look at her. She gives them each a ration, which gets their attention. One Inquisitor is a male teenager, the other is a woman in her mid-40s. Moiloc approaches the teenager, and says she knows [[Kaldrake Sootspot|Kaldrake]], trying to establish trust with a connection to an Inquisitor, but he doesn’t recognize the name. The teenager says his name is Haddock. Moiloc asks what he’s in for, he gives her a look and doesn't respond. Moiloc notices the other woman listening, and asks her if she knows Kaldrake, to no response. Branwyn threatens that the longer they’re silent the more the Resistance will think they’re hiding. The woman chuckles at this. Moiloc pretends to lament that the prisoners won’t be able to help with her super secret Inquisitor plan, but they don't believe her and maintain their silence.

Meanwhile, [[Lothar (Brewster) Nash|Brewster]] begins working on the machine. By the time he arrives to the lab, Nubgrub is no longer there. Moiloc and Branwyn stop by Brewster's lab to see if he needs anything from the market, and he asks for some pre-made cogs to speed up his building process.
## Market
After arriving at the market, Moiloc looks for sweet deals on rations, and haggles at a street stall, getting a pretty low price. They proceed to look for boots, and Moiloc asks the leather worker how much a pair of boots would be for a pretty lady. He gives her a 50% discount. They start digging into their personal funds to buy cogs and arrows from the blacksmith. With all items bought, they decide to return as the market gets crowded.

As they go to leave, Branwyn notices a mail carrier with an Inquisitor’s seal on his chest run past them. They decide to follow him. He runs up to an Inquisitor, a dark-haired woman, and hands her a letter, then heads south. They continue to follow him to the docks, and he hands a letter to a rugged, beefy man. Branwyn and Moiloc decide to try and catch him in an alley. However, he soon notices the clanking of Moiloc’s armor, and he asks why they’re following him.

Moiloc uses Command to have him halt, but the spell fails and he books it. Branwyn casts Hunters Mark on him and she and Moiloc chase after him. Moiloc chases after the mailman while Branwyn splits off, using side roads to try and get ahead of him. The mailman is still looking over his shoulder at Moiloc as Branwyn jumps out in front of him. She Disguises Self as an Inquisitor and offers help the mailman. He tells her someone is chasing him, and she tells him to hide in an alley. As Moiloc gets to Branwyn, she slows down immediately as to not look suspicious in front of an Inquisitor and apologizes, turning to leave. Branwyn returns to the exhausted mailman, who thanks her for saving him. Branwyn knocks him out with her hand axe and takes the letters from his satchel. She un-disguises herself, reunites with Moiloc, and they start heading back to the hideout.
## Rebel Hideout
Moiloc and Branwyn give the boots to Feris and put the rations in the store room. Feris gives them a magic dagger. They give the cogs to Brewster, and Branwyn takes the dagger from Moiloc. On their way to put the rations ways, they pass by the cells, and find that the younger Inquisitor has been bound to the wall.

They return to Brewster to read the letters. One is a letter to a gold broker from his family. The next is a letter to the baker Brewster is disguised as, from his wife. The next is addressed to an Inquisitor, Dirigible Morningstar, inviting them to the tournament. The last one won’t open, and it seems to be sealed with immensely powerful magic.

Moiloc and Branwyn proceed to the meeting room and tell Feris and [[Violet]] that they have an invitation. Feris gives them gold to buy horses, and suggests they take the Inquisitors’ tabards. They ask Feris for more details on the tournament, but he doesn’t have any.

In the cells, Branwyn asks for the tabard, and the woman refuses. Moiloc uses Command and she snootily complies. They are able to just take the teen’s tabard since he is tied up.

## Dream
Kaldrake awakes as [[Gulvayas Hakon|Gulvayas]] in a large office. Someone knocks, and he places the mask on his face, disguising himself as [[Characters/Characters/Martin Sootspot|Martin]]. An elven woman, [[Mora|Morrigan]], enters who looks a lot like Branwyn, wearing a tree pendant, a religious symbol. She says her preparations are ready, nobody will see it coming. The pair have crazy sexual tension. Their goal is to gain control of the Aetherium. Gulvayas takes a vial of blue liquid, telling Morrigan to drink it to prevent [[Baedos]]’s control. A gnome enters, saying the other High Mages have been gathered.

A pluck of the red string attached to Kaldrake sends him to Gulvayas at a meeting with the High Mages. Gulvayas announces his plans to become Archwizard and Master of the Aetherium. The High Mages rise in protest, but Morrigan entangles them with black vines, and drinks the vial. The Mages coalesce into Gulvayas, dragged toward him with the same red string Kaldrake uses to interact with his visions. Morrigan collapses, confused, saying she loved Martin. Gulvayas removes his disguise as she dies, revealing his lie.
# Day 33
## Village
Kaldrake appears at a foggy village. Humanoid, smokey forms shift in and out of reality. He proceeds to the inn, but still doesn’t see tangible people. He sees a sign at the crossroads and heads that way. The fog seems to coalesce around him as he moves. He realizes he is in the ethereal plane, between the material and astral planes. He recalls that only a wizard's planar portal would allow escape, but there should be a god that lays dominion over this plane who could help.

The arrows on the crossroad signs are blank. One side points to grass and one to woods. He follows the grass path. He realizes after some time that he has been walking for a long time. He sees sand off on the distance, off the road. A red string shoots out of his chest, trailing off of the path into the sand. He follows the string for a long time. The sun rises and sets in ten minutes. The string goes solid, and his feet go sore. Suddenly, he is standing in front of a statue of [[Eru]] in the desert. There is an impression on the peristalsis that seems to be worn from someone sitting on it. The placard is worn off. He blinks and appears in the astral plane. Bridges of light are breaking away from the statue, leading to one door each. He opens the left door, and it shows a birds-eye view of a city: a small village. The right door shows fire twisting into various forms: the primordial plane. He prays to Eru and gets a feeling that if he jumps into the village he’ll be fine. However, instead of falling he just immediately appears on the ground in the center of the village.

He locates himself as being in the village of Sabra in the darkness of night, just south of Lothric, but the town seems empty. There is a [[Styx the Pathfinder|pilgrim]] heading north. Kaldrake approaches the pilgrim and asks if he is also heading to Lothric. The pilgrim turns, his mouth is stitched closed. Kaldrake asks if he wants company, and he seems to agree, handing him a cracker.

On their walk, Kaldrake notices that animals seem to dodge the light of the pilgrim’s lantern. He asks if the lamp helps avoid dangers, he nods, and Kaldrake asks why he can enter the light, and he shrugs.

Kaldrake realizes his heartbeat is gone again, and he does not feel temperature. The pilgrim’s entire body is scarred in strange runes, including the Celestial word for Eru. Kaldrake asks if he knows what’s happening to him, he looks at him pitifully and points at his chest. He motions to come closer, places a hand on Kaldrake’s forehead, presenting him with one of his memories. The pilgrim is kneeling subserviently in front of Gwyneth, who has a blade leveled to his neck. He gives Kaldrake a letter, identical to the one Moiloc, Branwyn, and Brewster couldn’t open. The seal looks like a wagon wheel. Kaldrake opens it, but it is written in Celestial, and he can’t read it.

A vision also appears of a the Michael's in Lothric, and of [[Monty MacMasters|Monty]]. Kaldrake shows the pilgrim the white crystal. The man looks at Kaldrake like he’s an idiot, takes the crystal, and chucks it into the woods. Kaldrake confirms with him that [[Gwyneth Sootspot|Gwyneth]] is still the grand vizier.
