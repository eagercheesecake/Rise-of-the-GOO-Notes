### Logline
*Branwyn, Moiloc, and Brewster have visions while under the effects of the hydra. Martin meets his new adventuring party.*
### Synopsis
Branwyn meets with Maokai and declines his offer to free him from the purple haze trapping him. Brewster enters the Ringed City and meets with its residents, the hadozee, learning about all the gods. He finds Eru's skull in the center. Moiloc learns about her mother from a demon, but refuses his offer to follow him. He gives her a ring, asking her to put it on a Sootspot, in exchange for one favor at a future time. Moiloc and Brewster wake up in Col Man'Dar, and meet up with Martin, Goro, Helen, and Jessie.
# Day 48
### Maokai's Prison
[[Branwyn of Coryphea|Branwyn]] asks [[Maokai]] who he is, and he introduces himself as the god of nature and decay, betrayed by [[Mora]]. They were supposed to make a deal where she would become his avatar and return the world to nature, destroying buildings and civilizations. But now he’s been dispelled to this plane. He says Mora looks just like Branwyn. Branwyn suggests he may have been more successful as a god if he focused more on helping his worshippers.

Maokai asks Branwyn to help him, but she says he isn’t in the position to make demands. She says she’s a believer in balance, and Mora is hungrier than she has ever been. Mortals are at the whim of the gods throwing the world into chaos, and she refuses to help a god which contributed to that. This interests Maokai, who realizes she is not like Mora, and realizes that perhaps she is right. He asks her to free him from the purple haze outside, but Branwyn says she will only comply if he takes his place as a protector of nature, including mortals, taking justice on people who disrupt the balance, and helping creatures both large and small, saving those who are lost in the wood. He agrees to do so, in a cycle of 100 years of helping, and 100 years of decay. Branwyn disagrees, not wanting to bring more destruction on the world. Maokai says it’s a choice between him and Mora, the lesser of two gods. Branwyn says she will defeat the witch, and Maokai says greater than her have tried.
### Ringed City
[[Lothar (Brewster) Nash|Brewster]] enters the first layer of the Ringed City. Its majestically sunlit, with streets covered in sand. Footprints lead to three ape-like beings, the hadozee, wearing red tunics with an emblem of a boar’s head. Many other hadozee are further ahead. A few are holding pottery, and others are bowing to a statue in - mausoleum. Brewster attempts to sneak behind them, but notices they’ve seen him, and just aren’t acknowledging him. They’re approaching the mausoleum with the same boar’s head symbol. Brewster walks next to one of them, and they welcome him to the Ringed City. Brewster recognizes this as relating to Eru. The hadozee says it’s [[Eru|Eru’s]] former domain, but she’s been gone for a long time. Brewster asks what they are doing, and they say they are custodians of the mausoleum of [[Sha’Sime]], who died a long time ago.

Brewster asks why he might have been taken to the city. He says usually people who come by have an aura of divinity, but Brewster doesn’t seem to have that. He asks what he might do next, and the hadozee says to follow the road to the kiln of life, where Eru created the minor gods and the first life forms. Brewster presses him for more information, but he says he hasn’t been there in some time.

Brewster proceeds to the next ring. He walks past several mausoleums on the way. Hadozee in green tunics have a chalice symbol. This is the symbol of [[Celica the Good]], the goddess of healing, often worshipped by healing clerics. Black tunics have a tower and sunset on them. This is the symbol of [[Gal Troxic]], the god of Order, popular in Maedros. The current ruler of Maedros used to be a paladin of Gal Troxic. Pink tunics have a heart, belonging to [[Dario the Twin]], the god of love and camaraderie. He is the twin of Celica, made at the same time. He’s not as popular, usually worshiped by newlyweds and lovers, with very few actual clerics. Blue tunics have a boat, relating to [[Caspian]], the god of sea and commerce, the patron god of traders and sailors. He’s worshiped a lot on the Kodlak Islands. Brown tunics have a fleur-de-lis, for [[Disgaia]], the goddess of the earth, and the goddess of nature, worshiped by some druids, rangers, and apothecaries.

The next mausoleum has corpses of hadozee with grey tunics which have the symbol of chaos on them, which Brewster doesn’t recognize. Looking inside, Brewster sees a few more corpses, with a pedestal with no statue on it. The next area is a small, wooden house with two hadozee, with a sigil of a lantern on it. This is for [[Styx the Pathfinder|Styx]], the god of the dead, the ferryman of souls, and the patron god of travelers. He asks about Kaldrake’s meeting with him and being taken from the plane of death to the living, but they hadn’t heard of a situation like this before. Brewster asks about the mausoleum and the corpses, and they say [[Unknown God of Chaos|their god]] is no more. A god that is dead still has a source of divinity that could be channeled to bring a new god or successor, but a god that is no more cannot be brought back. They have forgotten who this god is, but Brewster thinks [[Gwyneth Sootspot|Gwyneth]] would know.

Brewster proceeds to the next layer and sees a tall obelisk, and the corpses of a few hadozee, almost magnetically stuck to the side of the obelisk. The obelisk has a the chaos symbol with four of its arrows inverted. He feels like he should not get close to it, it’s made of crystal he has never seen. He feels like it might absorb his soul. It reminds him of a crystal native to the underdark below Whitehall. He makes a wide circle around it to pass.

Next, he arrives at a garden of mushrooms, with hadozee wearing brown tunics with a decaying tree, for Maokai, the god of nature and decay. Brewster brings up that there’s another god of nature, asking if he’s a successor. They say they can’t divulge the source of his power, but it’s something like that.

Brewster continues to the next level, and sees hadozee around a fishing village. They are wearing light blue tunics with an image of a fish skewered by a sword, for [[Sekollah]], the god of war and the hunt, worshiped by the sahaguin.

Next is [[Blipdopol]], the goddess of fish, shellfish, and disease, with a sickly green tunic, worshipped by the sahaguin and other underwater creatures. Then there is yellow tunics with the grin of a shark, for [[Laogzed]], the god of famine, but the hadozee refer to him as Laogzed, the eater of souls.

The next area has crimson tunics with a lute, for [[Olidamuru]], the god of chaos and revelry, with an opulent mansion. Inside are drunken hadozee. Brewster asks if the viscous black liquid that has made them drunk would be safe to drink, and they say no.

Next is a graveyard with just fragments of a statue. There is a symbol of scrolls, but the hadozee wear no clothes. They worship the god of secrets, but they don’t remember his name. Brewster asks if it’s [[Shurima the Corpsemaker|Shurima]], but they’re not sure. They seem aimless, with no memories, confused that the statue broke but they didn’t die.

Finally, there’s another mausoleum with no statue, with a symbol for a star. Brewster remembers reading about this god in an old book, thinking it might be [[Hortensia]], the goddess of magic. She is a pagan god who was worshipped a long time ago, before Martin’s Isle. Nobody worships it anymore.

Brewster arrives at a colossal staircase going to what appears to be a coliseum, with a large double door of marble. He pushes them open, and sees a large throne room. He can see the sky above him. In the center is a massive throne. Surrounding are 16 smaller thrones. Behind those are 8 massive items looking like large pedestals or beds, which are unmarked. Inside the large throne is a massive skull.
### Gahenna
[[Moiloc Kodlaksdottër|Moiloc]] is sitting at a very long table in a lavish dining room, with an immaculate feast, better than anything she’s seen, even as a princess. The demon is now dressed in velvet finery, with hair neatly pushed back, brightly smiling, offering her the food. She tries to be polite while eating the food, immensely enjoying it. She asks where they are, and he says they’re in Gahenna, and introduces himself as [[Mephistopheles the Cold|Mephistopheles]], ruler of two-fifths of Gahenna. He asks how she is, saying he was intimately familiar with her mother, who was his favorite warlock. She asks if [[Moona|her mother]] is here, and he confirms that she’s dead. She asks what her mom was like, and he says she was dedicated and thorough, and always loved her family - one of her weaknesses. She did his killing, hunting, sleuthing, and anything else he needed. He says she died of stress in childbirth, and he was there to collect when it happened. She does believe this is true, but with her vision from Olidamuru, she asks for the full story. He says Kodlak Islands was at war with the sahaguin under the command of Sekkolah and Blipdolpol, who like to work together. While she was giving birth, some sahaguin broke in. She was able to fend off a few, but the stress of the battle, added to the stress of childbirth, killed her. Moiloc asks how she arrived, but he admits that he doesn’t know. Moiloc continues questioning, and he says she independently killed the previous ruler of his portion of Gahenna. He also pulls out a vial with a writhing black mass inside of it, implying it is her soul, which he collected due to her contract with him: her soul, in exchange for finding her husband, and getting a family. He offers her to follow him, but she says she already signed with a god. When he asks who, she feels compelled to say, and states it’s Shurima, which seems to scare him.

He asks if she’s seen any Sootspots, and asks to give him a golden ring inlaid with a black gemstone, which he says will help him find his prey: a wizard who has betrayed him, stealing from him, implying it’s Gwyneth. She says she’ll need something in exchange. He says he can’t give her mother’s soul back. However, at her request, he says he’ll owe her a favor, to be exchanged at any time. A contact appears, saying she’ll put a ring on the finger of any Sootspot, in exchange for a favor of Mephistopheles the Cold. She signs it.

# Day 52
### Edoval
Brewster and Moiloc wake up with a start after four days. Brewster immediately wakes up, screaming in anger at his discovery, before realizing he’s no longer in the woods, but in a makeshift sick room in an inn. Across the room he sees Goro, who has woken up from Brewster’s screaming. Brewster asks Goro to fill him in, and he says the hydra defeated and enthralled them, and he and Helen followed after the hydra. A group of adventurers destroyed the hydra. Goro tells Brewster about [[Ouroborus Tikktal|Ouroborus]] flying off, and Branwyn being taken away by a terrifying monster. He says Kaldrake is dead, and Martin Sootspot has been helping. Brewster tells Goro that Eru is dead, and asks for food. Goro gives them some food, and Brewster and Moiloc fight over Shurima.

They proceed to Martin to talk to him. Brewster admits that he’s a big fan, and always wanted to go to the Ethereum. He mentions slavery, and overloads him with his life story, eventually saying the god of magic, and Eru, are dead. Moiloc tries to give Martin the ring, and Brewster tries to identify on it, but can’t. Swearing he can do magic, he tries to make [[Puck Nash|Puck]] appear, but panics when he can’t find the stone. Martin gives him the Sootspot signet ring. Brewster is curious as to why he woke up at exactly the same time as Moiloc, and asks if she went to the Ringed City, too. She says no, and says she thinks she was in hell. Martin asks if she got the ring from hell, and she says she isn’t sure. Martin admits she’s suspicious, and Brewster commends Martin for his Sootspot insight in picking that up. Moiloc asks is Brewster is okay, saying his lips are pretty close to Martin’s ass. Brewster punches Moiloc, forgetting her armor is invisible, and hurting his hand. Moiloc casts Lay on Hands on Brewster. Martin asks how they know each other, and if they know Goro. Moiloc says she spent a couple weeks with him on a ship, but she was dealing with the loss of her armor. Brewster comments that she obviously got armor back, and that he was enslaved, so she should get over it. Martin tries to get Goro to come over and split them up, but he refuses, giving him a thumbs up. Martin suggests they heal up before continuing toward Whitehall to find Branwyn.

While the others rest, Martin looks for a book in the local library on how magic could be completely hidden from detection. He finds that this can only be done by very powerful magic users.
