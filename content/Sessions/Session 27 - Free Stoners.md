### Logline
*Moiloc gets kidnapped. The party meets with Ulysses and Isaac.*
### Synopsis
After a fight with the group, Moiloc splits off and gets kidnapped by tritons. She fends them off, and kidnaps one of them. After questioning him, Shurima tells her to enslave him, but she says she killed and buried him. Meanwhile, Ulysses tells Martin, Branwyn, and Brewster about guns, and how he may be able to kill Mora. The group meet back up and talk to Isaac. They recruit the Archer brothers to join them on their adventure in Whitehall.
# Day 57
### Silver Wreath Inn
The group returns to the inn. [[Moiloc Kodlaksdottër|Moiloc]] feels a tug at her amulet pulling her toward Domnhall Keep. Moiloc takes note to look for [[Shurima the Corpsemaker|Shurima]] when they head there tomorrow. [[Lothar (Brewster) Nash|Brewster]] tells [[Branwyn of Coryphea|Branwyn]] about the meeting with the [[Ulysses|cowboy]], and asks if she wants to come to ask about the hairs. He invites [[Martin Sootspot|Martin]] as well. Brewster goes to the bathhouse to clean off the fish.
# Day 58
### Silver Wreath Inn
In the morning, Brewster gets some [[Schmebulock]] coffee to go. He sits with Martin and they talk. Moiloc comes down and asks how everyone slept. Martin says he doesn’t sleep, Moiloc asks if that’s and elf thing, and Martin says to watch how she words things. Branwyn asks if it’s time to go, and Brewster nudges her to be quiet. Moiloc asks about her head wound, stating she doesn’t remember what happened last night. Brewster tries to convince Moiloc to rest some more, but Branwyn is worried about leaving Moiloc alone, and reminds Brewster about their conversation on trust.
### Domnhall Keep
Brewster asks to see the ring Moiloc got from hell, and threatens to put it on to see if she’ll react. He sees the Sootspot seal on the ring. Moiloc admits to pretty much everything about hell. Branwyn tells Moiloc to be more honest to the group. Brewster and Moiloc fight about Moiloc’s mom working for demons. Branwyn and Martin leave while they argue. Once Brewster realizes they have left, he decides he doesn’t want them to beat him to the chapel, and tells Branwyn to follow him there, and begins booking it. Moiloc, sad that her friends don’t trust her, dejectedly and slowly walks to the chapel, not paying attention to her surroundings. Moiloc is knocked unconscious.

Moiloc wakes up blindfolded and bound on the ground. By the movement of the floor she can tell she’s in a wagon, and can hear a couple other people in the wagon. She tries to rub the blindfold off of her head using the ground, but is unable. A guard kicks her to get her to stop. She is able to slide her gauntlets off. The wrist constraints fall off with the gauntlets, and the rest of her armor materializes. She removes her blindfold, and sees two tritons preparing to attack.

> [!danger] Fight
> Moiloc Eldritch Blasts the tritan in front of her, and kicks him out of the wagon. The other tritan hits her, but can’t get through her armor. Moiloc gets up and uses Dissonant Whispers, but the tritan avoids it. She then pushes the tritan through the canvas of the wagon, who is then run over.

Moiloc finds her items in the wagon, and gets to the reins and turns the wagon around. The wagon was taking her to Samble, a village to the East, where they were going to send her on a boat to the Kodlak Islands. As she goes back, she sees the man who was run over is definitely dead, and the other is running towards her. She stops the wagon and checks the mangled body. Moiloc finds 20g.

> [!danger] Fight
> She Eldritch Blasts the approaching tritan. He attacks her, and she uses her fire longsword to attack him. He ripostes her, but can’t get through her armor.

She hits him with the pommel of her blade, knocks him out, and ties him up, putting him in the wagon. She finds another assassination letter on these assassins. She puts salted fish just outside of reach of the tritan. Moiloc heals herself and heads back to Domnhall Keep.
### Domnhall Keep Chapel
Brewster gets to the chapel first. There’s no candlelight coming inside the chapel. On the door, is a paper that says “closed for renovations.” Brewster opens the door and sees a werewolf corpse strung up on a statue of Eru. Kneeling below it is the cowboy, doing something with metal casings and black powder. Brewster asks if that’s how he used the weapon, and he said he’s modifying it to kill the God. Brewster asks if Branwyn’s hair can kill a god, and he says it can kill a specific one: [[Mora|the witch]]. At this moment, Martin and Branwyn come in. Branwyn says since he has her hair, he should give them his name, which he says is Ulysses.

Brewster offers an exchange of information about Mora, saying they would also like to kill her. Ulysses ask Branwyn where Mora is, and Branwyn explains about the cabin moving, but can’t track down an exact location. Branwyn tells him about [[Charlotte]], and he seems afraid, saying the need to hurry, and urging Brewster to give him the mask. Martin steps in, saying it’s a Sootspot heirloom. Ulysses says it’s a mask from his people, and points out that there are no illusionist in the Sootspot family.

Brewster asks if he’ll disappear once getting the mask, urging him to keep them in the loop. He says they can help. Branwyn tells him Mora is almost at her full power, and Ulysses says he saw her at this level before, 140 years ago, showing them the half of his face that has disappeared. He mentions his daughter was taken, and that nobody has ever escaped before, which is why Branwyn intrigued him so much. He explains he needs the mask because it has divinity in it, which is needed to kill another divine being, and a vessel to absorb the divinity. He raises a white crystal, which looks like the ones Kaldrake saw in [[Gwyneth Sootspot|Gwyneth’s]] room. Brewster asks when he needs the mask, and he says he’s using Branwyn’s hair to track Mora, which will take a couple of days.

Martin tells Ulysses about Moiloc’s vision of Mora in Whitehall and invites him to join them. Brewster confirms he doesn’t buy into religion, and he killed [[Laogzed]], the god of famine. Brewster tells Ulysses that Laogzed seems to be alive, and [[Eru]] is dead. Ulysses seems to have thought Brewster was lying about this when they last spoke.

Martin and Branwyn go to the library. Martin sees a book mentioning a copper sword from [[Sha-Sĭme]] which is yet to be found. It is written that it was discarded at the end of the Great Dragon Strife in the country of Shurima. Branwyn finds a book mentioning [[Maokai]], the god of the wood. There’s also a page describing a god of secrets, but the name is missing. Branwyn writes Shurima in the blank. She takes the book and adds some knowledge she has in it. Martin uses Knock on the chest in the bedroom, and finds 700g worth of gemstones, and a small button. He pockets 100g worth.

Brewster asks Ulysses about the gun. He got it from a group of warriors in Rere Hua in the Orkus Badlands. A group of uwantee gave it to him, and it’s called a thunder spear. Brewster asks what Ulysses is going to do with the divine essence of Mora, and he says he’s not sure. He might ask the wizards of the Etherium. Brewster asks how he feels about the Etherium, and he says he’s hates Sootspots, not wizards.

Martin has Mage Hand push the button in the chest, to reveal the hidden room that the trapdoor led to. It’s a rather comfortable bedroom, but there are chains attached to the floor by the bed. Martin reads through a journal in the room, which tells of a young boy who was excited to become an adventurer, until he learned that he and his father were werewolves. They were looking for a cure, but after the son got out, they had to start chaining him up. The trio exits the chapel to find Moiloc and proceed to Domnhall Keep.
### Domnhall Keep
Moiloc proceeds to the inn to store the wagon. Vola points her to the Domnhall Stables outside of town. She gets a stable there to keep the wagon and horses, and talks to her captive. Moiloc tells him to tell her what she knows or he’ll join his friend. He spits at her. Moiloc uses Charm Person and tries again. He says he’s not authorized to share. He says he’s from the Kodlak Islands, from a small village under the water called Sebastian’s Wake. He’s part of the Free Stoners, who are fighting against the tyrannical Kodlak. The group was started by their king, Tribolt. Moiloc knows Tribolt, he trained her to fight and was a father figure to her. Kodlak Islands has been overtaking the tritan’s villages for fishing, and food has been sparse. Moiloc was to be brought to Tribolt. He confirms Kodlak is alive, for now.

Moiloc asks Shurima what she should do with him. He suggests to heat her medallion and brand it onto his flesh, making him hers. Moiloc considers it for a while.
### Silver Wreath Inn
As Brewster, Martin, and Branwyn head back to the inn, they see Moiloc, with a man in desert garb. Martin asks if she is safe, and she Messages back saying she’s safe and will talk to them at the inn when they’re alone. He introduces himself as Eltoth Evergrande. Moiloc says he’s going to be staying with them for a while, and that they should meet in private. She buys Eltoth a room, and sends him upstairs. Moiloc asks Brewster to send [[Puck Nash|Puck]] outside to make sure he stays in his room. Helen is in her room, so they bring her into the room as well.

Moiloc tells them about her fight, claiming she bravely fought five tritons, and saying she brought a wagon and horses. She says he’s under Charm Person. She explains everything he said, and they ask what her plans are. They philosophize about what to do with him, and decide the best thing to do would be to kill him before Charm Person ends. Moiloc takes him out, and claims to kill him, hiding his body.
### Domnhall Keep
They proceed to Domnhall Keep, and are met with a closed gate. Martin introduces himself and requests an audience with Isaac Domnhall. The guard confirms his signet ring is real and lets them in.

In the center of the courtyard is a statue of a burly man with burn scars, holding a spear. The plaque says it is a statue of [[Isaac Domhnall]]. Martin and Branwyn notice there is a crack on the belt. Martin uses Mage Hand, and an ancient copper coin slips out of the crack, with a boar’s head on one side, and a map on the other. They proceed to Isaac’s office, with the guards telling them where to go, but the guard by the office says he’s not in, and to go to the war room. They hear [[Violet]] and [[Feris Weal|Feris]] inside.

Inside the room is Isaac, Violet, Feris, and [[Tarren Hammerfist|Tarren]]. Tarren happily welcomes Brewster. Isaac is older, bald, with burn scars across the entire right side of his body. The group introduces themselves, and Brewster asks what their goals are now that the [[Pontiff Sulyvahn|Pontiff]] is dead. The Resistance seems to know this, but they haven’t heard anything from Lothric. They are now planning to take back Whitehall. They reveal that Isaac had been working with them the whole time. Isaac explains that they are trying to track down the current leader of Wallachia: the [[Queen Cypha|Queen’s]] closest advisor, Gwyneth Sootspot. They know she’s in Whitehall, but their spies aren’t reporting back, and they worry they were found out. She was seen with a witch. Martin immediately suggests that they’ll have to kill her.

They have two options. Firstly, they could wait and gather forces, since Isaac has asked the largest warlord in the badlands to help, but it would be difficult to enter via the gate. This might take six or seven days. Or, they could send a small group into Whitehall as a scouting force, stop Gwyneth and her allies, and clear the way to free the slaves. For the small group, Tarren has volunteered, Brewster, Martin, Branwyn, and Moiloc would join, and Ulysses would likely join as well. Feris says he might be able to get a mercenary group to join if they stop by Velras. A cleric in Isaac’s town has gone missing recently, who could have helped [[Helen]]. Brewster tells them [[Jessie Harkness|Jessie]] went missing, but Violet says Jessie was killed by a flying monster, who also took some children. They also point out Daphne and the Sister Marie the Confessor’s presence in Moiloc’s vision. Isaac tells them to do what they need to do to prepare.

The group proceeds to the armory. Martin sees another boar’s head coin nestled in a pile of ingots. Brewster and Branwyn get a +1 set of scale mail. Moiloc gets a +1 handaxe. Martin finds a scroll of revivify in the library.

They proceed to the archery range and see two young twin boys practicing. Martin sees a coin peeking out of the pocket of one of the archers. They are taking turns splitting each other’s arrows. Martin takes the coin using Mage Hand. They introduce themselves as the Archer Twins, [[William Archer|William]] and [[Billiam Archer]]. Branwyn offers them to join in on the mission. They lay out the difficulty of the plan. They say they’re not afraid to die, and are honored that they were selected. Branwyn says they should, in fact, be ready to die.
