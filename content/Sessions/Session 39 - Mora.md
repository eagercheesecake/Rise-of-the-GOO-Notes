### Logline
*Mora acts.*
### Synopsis
Brewster, Martin, Branwyn, and their group find Daphne and the Clotho Lachesis. After a hostile reunion, they realize from the book that Ishtar and Moiloc are nearby. They are attacked by an angel of Gal Troxic, then by Mora and her captured children, and Tobias's daughter dies in the battle. One of the children is revealed to be Brewster and Daphne's daughter, Callia, who dies but is revived. Martin takes the Clotho Lachesis and destroys it, being teleported to the moon with Gwyneth. Martin kills Gwyneth, and is reborn as Abaddon.
# Day 63
### The Arena
The group proceeds on the boat, following [[Daphne]], as two more beings appear in the sky, one going to the cathedral, and one going to the arena, where Daphne lands. They row quickly to catch up. [[Martin Sootspot|Martin]] gets a message from [[Gwyneth Sootspot|Gwyneth]] saying not to fuck up the book. Martin hangs up. [[Branwyn of Coryphea|Branwyn]] casts Foresight, and thinks a curse on [[Mora]]. She also casts Commune with Nature, and gets a projection in her mind of the arena. It’s half-submerged in quicksilver. There is a magical book, surrounded by a magical aura. She also sees a projection of the cathedral, including the statue of [[Eru]] with a face. [[Lothar (Brewster) Nash|Brewster]] says he has never seen Eru with a face, even throughout history. Branwyn also senses the influence of the celestial, infernal, death, the undead, and the fae realm. This implies gods, demons, [[Styx the Pathfinder|Styx]], corpse eaters, and perhaps Mora. There is minor influence from the other planes as well.

Brewster enters the arena first. Inside, he sees the people who Daphne was with smeared across the walls. One has a javelin through his heart. In the center of the room is an angelic-looking elven woman holding Daphne over a quicksilver pit. Brewster casts Levitation on Daphne. The angel turns back to the others, saying they are enemies of the Lord of Order, [[Gal Troxic]].

### The Moon
Martin reappears on the moon, seeing an elven man inside the dome. On the other side of the dome is Gwyneth, who screams at Martin for destroying the book. Gwyneth says she’s decided to kill Martin. He jumps over the dome to attack Gwyneth with his other sword, plunging it into her chest. She drops to the ground, calling Martin a moron. A bright light from her chest goes into the dome, and Martin feels himself pulled in.

On the surface of Phaeros, citizens look up as the sky goes black, and the moon explodes. Martin opens his eyes, realizing he has become the fell harbinger of fate, [[Abaddon]].
