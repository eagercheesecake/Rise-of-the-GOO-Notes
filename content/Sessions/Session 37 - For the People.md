# Day 63
### Whitehall Mines
[[Lothar (Brewster) Nash|Brewster]] barricades the door to the mine so Inquisitors can’t follow them. [[Martin Sootspot|Martin]] finds a body with a different-colored outfit, seeming a bit older and higher-ranked. The use [[William Archer|William]], who he didn’t see, to ask questions via [[Lucretia|Lucretia’s]] Speak With Dead. They learn that the elevator only goes down to level 5. They also find that there will likely be guards everywhere, especially at lower levels, where guards, locked doors, traps, and the cartographer are all defenses they need to look out for.

Brewster explains that he last saw [[Daphne]] a decade ago, and knew her for another decade. He says that he thinks if anyone finds Daphne and tells her that they are with Lothar, that she would help.

They send puck to check out the storage room, and he finds four guards and one taller, bulkier guard with glowing blue veins. While thinking of a plan, [[Branwyn of Coryphea|Branwyn]] detects someone trying to track them. Martin feels that control over his [[Tobias Grande|Tobias]] was lost, and that the Tobias was dead.

Martin makes Brewster Invisible, and he tries to block off the other exit to storage. As he passes the larger man, he seems to notice something is amiss. William calls for someone to help him move a dead hook horror, and two of the guards move to help. They immediately see the dead bodies and notice something is wrong. They draw their weapons. Notably: Brewster seems to hear none of this.

### Capella Sinistra
[[Moiloc Kodlaksdottër|Moiloc]] and [[Ishtar]] continue down to the lower chambers of the chapel. They get to a circular chamber, with a two-level altar in the center. At the top of the altar is a stone slab offering table, with the broken, bloodied body of [[Ulysses]] on top. Standing above him is [[Sister Marie the Confessor]]. She welcomes them, saying Ulysses doesn’t have enough blood for her, and Ishtar’s pendant would do wonders for her sacrifice. Moiloc sees carvings on the altar, with drains connecting to the braziers. There’s a sealed door with a snake emblem on it, but Moiloc doesn’t recognize it.

> [!danger] Fight
> Moiloc uses Divine Favor, and slashes at Marie with her fire longsword twice. Ishtar uses Twinblade Tornado to slash into her from below. Marie cackles, and throws her whip around Moiloc’s ankle. Moiloc’s blood vessels burst in her eyes and Marie heals from the blood. Marie then slashes at Moiloc with her sacrificial dagger.
> 
> Ishtar again uses her Twinblade Tornado. Moiloc, angered, violently cuts into Marie twice. Marie again tries to drain Moiloc’s blood, but Moiloc fortifies herself, and the power of Shurima protects her.
> 
> Ishtar slices at Marie. Marie looks panicked and screams for help. The Death Knight suddenly appears at the door, and he then explodes in a blast of light. Standing behind him is a very drained Tobias. Marie reaches out to stab Moiloc, but Moiloc parries her, killing her.

[[Shurima the Corpsemaker|Shurima]] appears behind Moiloc, saying they need to sacrifice two people to open the door and reach the Underdark, recommending they sacrifice Tobias and Marie. Moiloc suggests they look for the rest of their party. Tobias stops her, saying she needs to act quick, as he can’t keep the [[Death Knight]] banished forever. Moiloc tells Tobias to take Ishtar and leave, meeting up with the others.

Moiloc has less ease convincing Ishtar. Ishtar heals Tobias, Moiloc, Ulysses, and herself. Moiloc eventually convinces her to leave, saying the door is calling her. Moiloc takes the sacrificial dagger and drives it into Tobias and Marie. The braziers glow hot, and the door fades away, revealing a hallway. She enters the hallway, and exits onto a balcony overlooking a city half-submerged in quicksilver. Floating above it is a large, purple crystal.
### The Lost City of Shurima
Shurima materializes next to her, saying it’s good to be home. He says this is the city of Shurima, and that they must brave the Cathedral of the Gods, quickly, before they have competition. They proceed into the city hand in hand, as Shurima lifts her into the sky, floating above the quicksilver ocean. The buildings flit in and out of existence, and Moiloc notices that the large crystal is heavily cracked, and there’s a draconian skeleton in the quicksilver. There are petrified, stone people in the buildings and streets of the town. They land at the entrance to the cathedral, seeing corpses. Moiloc notices some petrified elves, commenting that she’s never seen them before. A vast cloud of green smoke enters the cathedral, and Shurima comments that their first competitor has arrived. The smoke solidifies into a monster, looking like a frog, but growing tusks, pustules, and other extremities, so thin that they can see his ribs. Shurima says this is [[Laogzed]].

Laogzed and Shurima have a spat, both coming for the Clotho Lochesis. Laogzed comments that he had already made an agreement today, which had made him more powerful, and he creates a cloud of viscous green smoke, which is easily waved off. Shurima comments that the god of death cannot help him, and Laogzed admits that he, Gwyneth, and Mora had made an alliance against Shurima and killed Maokai. Shurima says he will help Moiloc, and puts a hand on her, blessing her with Haste.

Shurima comments that he believes in Moiloc, giving her Bardin Inspiration.

> 
> Moiloc’s sword grows with divine energy as she plunges it into Laogzed’s chest. He explodes, spraying Moiloc with poison.

Shurima grabs into the center of the cloud of poison as he dissipates, grabbing Laogzed’s heart. He heals Moiloc, asking if she wants to know why Laogzed followed her. She comments that she doesn’t really care.

Shurima says the gods are all fighting to take their place as the new [[Eru]], as she has been gone for many years. Shurima says he should take the throne, but it means he has to absorb the power of every god. The Clotho Lachesis is a book that can track down all of the divine artifacts so they can fully take every aspect of the gods, including their artifacts. Shurima questions Laogzed, asking what he meant about killing [[Maokai]], and he says he had to make the alliance before Shurima grew too strong. Laogzed got the power over death, Mora the power of the wilds, and [[Gwyneth Sootspot|Gwyneth]]… he cannot say. Shurima tries to give Laogzed’s heart to Moiloc, but she refuses, saying it’s gross. Moiloc asks Laogzed how to kill [[Mora]], and he says she is afraid of the bullet that can kill her, and it’s currently in Mora’s possession. Moiloc proclaims that she is done with Laogzed, and Shurima bursts the heart in his hand. Shurima says they need to go deeper into the cathedral to get the book.
