| [[Session 19 - The Party Turns Around]] |              |
### Logline
*The party continues to Col Man'Dar, traveling through planes along the journey. An encounter with an enemy reveals danger from within the party.*
### Synopsis
The party continues through the forest to Col Man'Dar, fighting trolls and other creatures. While traveling, they start inexplicably going through various planes, but forgetting their travels through the planes. Brewster makes a recording of this so they can remember. One night, Mora finds the group, who manages to hide from her as she magically determines they are going to Whitehall. The group continues to train Helen, but find a sigil on her back allowing Olidamuru to track them. Kaldrake stabs through the sigil, breaking the spell. The group is able to make it to Col Man'Dar's gates.
# Day 37
## Forest
After the storm elementals leave, the group continues down the forest path. After walking, [[Branwyn of Coryphea|Branwyn]] hears loud thudding in the forest, like something big is quickly approaching. They hide [[Helen]] behind a tree as a large troll comes through the tree line. [[Lothar (Brewster) Nash|Brewster]] recognizes that trolls are not native to Wallachia.

> [!danger] Fight
> [[Kaldrake Sootspot|Kaldrake]] cleaves into the troll deeply, causing it to heavily bleed, but its wound knits itself closed again. The troll misses Kaldrake with his club twice, then misses a bite. Branwyn shoots the troll in the shoulder, but misses her second shot. Brewster uses Melf’s Acid Arrow, shooting the troll in the chest, causing it to shriek. It doesn’t seem to heal. [[Moiloc Kodlaksdottër|Moiloc]] uses Bless, helping the team, and Kaldrake gets another hit off. The troll bites at Kaldrake, taking hold of him and slamming him twice with his club. Branwyn shoots at the troll twice more. Brewster misses an Eldritch Blast with his multitool. Moiloc uses her fire long sword, lobbing off the troll’s hand, which also doesn’t seem to regenerate. Kaldrake, still held by the troll, lightly attacks him. When the troll attacks back, Kaldrake is able to riposte, break free, and stab him in the throat, causing him to fall down with a giant thud.

The troll’s body dissipates and goes off into the ether. Helen returns, shaking, and Kaldrake helps her calm down, bloodied. Kaldrake helped teach Helen how to use a sword, and seems to be getting pretty close to understanding how to use it.

Later, Branwyn hears the snapping of branches around her. Poking out of the woods are three gnome-shaped creatures waiting to ambush them. Branwyn stops Kaldrake, pointing out the ambush. Kaldrake gestures for Branwyn to rush at one, saying he’ll go at another, and they rush to attack them, hoping to surprise them. The one Kaldrake approaches shrieks as he realizes that is happening.

> [!danger] Fight
> Kaldrake brings his weapon down on the creature, biting into its arm, but the creature deflects the second blow with its sickle. Branwyn shoots at her creature twice, hitting both times. The final creature jumps out and surrounds Kaldrake from the back, attacking with its sickle, but Kaldrake is ready for this and ripostes, which is deflected by the sickle again. The creature then misses twice. The other one attacking Kaldrake slashes him with his sickle. The final creature goes back to grab Branwyn, jumping onto her, repeatedly stabbing her. Moiloc steps to the side, telling Helen to run, and surprises everyone by casting Eldritch Blast, missing. Brewster also casts Eldritch Blast at the creature attached to Branwyn, but misses, also barely missing Branwyn. [[Puck Nash|Puck]] comes along and rams into the creature, but it doesn’t let go.
> 
> Kaldrake swings at the creature he had already hit, but it ducks under the blow. He’s able to hit the creature as it comes back up. Branwyn pushes the creature off of her. The weakened creature by Kaldrake, hitting him twice, as does the creature behind Kaldrake. Branwyn’s creature jumps back up on her, stabbing her again. Moiloc ignites her fire long sword, attacking the creature behind Kaldrake. Brewster attacks the enemy on Branwyn and the one behind Kaldrake with Eldritch Blast, and Puck tries to ram into the creature on Branwyn again, but misses. Kaldrake tries to finish off the weaker creature, but misses, and then he uses Second Wind to come back a bit from his wounds. Branwyn pushes the creature off of her again.
> 
> The wounded creature misses Kaldrake, who ripostes him. The creature then stabs at Kaldrake twice more, downing him. The creature behind Kaldrake turns to Moiloc, slashing at her. The creature by Branwyn downs Puck and slashes at Moiloc again. Moiloc misses Eldritch Blast again. Brewster uses Poison Spray on the creature attacking Branwyn, killing it, causing it to dissipate into a cloud, much like the troll. Branwyn heals herself and runs away into the forest. The remaining two creatures turn to surround Moiloc and dig into her with their shotels, but Moiloc is able to deflect the other attacks. Moiloc misses her next attack. Brewster uses Poison Spray on one of the creatures, and Healing word on Kaldrake, reviving him. Branwyn shoots the weaker creature, killing him and causing him to dissipate. She also shoots the remaining creature, who then turns to try and hit Moiloc, but is unable to get into her armor. Moiloc uses her fire longsword and slices into the creature. Brewster uses Poison Spray to kill the last one.

The group takes a short rest to heal up before proceeding. After some time walking, Branwyn and Kaldrake notice it is getting dark more quickly than it should be. The sun is still shining, but the forest itself is shrouded in darkness. Branwyn does not think this is connected to [[Mora]], but does notice that nature is shifting unnaturally. Brewster recognizes that this may imply they are an area between planes. Kaldrake confirms that seems like what’s happening, explaining his experience going between two planes. Brewster says it sounds like Kaldrake went through a portal in [[Gwyneth Sootspot|Gwyneth’s]] house that led to the plane of death and just happened to find his way out. She also describes the pilgrim, who Brewster and Branwyn recognize as [[Styx the Pathfinder]], who ferries souls from one plane to the next. They try to connect this to Mora, but Branwyn realizes she can’t really remember what Coryphea looked like, or even much outside of Coryphea from before she arrived at Montrissad.

Kaldrake notices it’s also dead quiet. He prays to [[Eru]] to get them out of this plane safely. Moiloc tries to pray to [[Shurima the Corpsemaker|Shurima]] but doesn’t hear a response. They decide to proceed. While walking, Branwyn realizes she has these lost memories and asks Kaldrake about it. Kaldrake doesn’t remember the trek through the desert or the statue, but of course, doesn’t realize this, and says the question is basically impossible to enter. They decide to use Brewster’s magical tinkering to record that they are in the plane of death, they don’t know how they got there, but they don’t think they’re dead. He says they’re worried they’re losing memories about traveling, and he’s keeping this note for records. He imbues this message into his alchemy tools so he’s sure he’ll trigger it in the morning. After some time it gets warmer and brighter, but they don’t realize, and they don’t remember their time in the plane of death. They have a long rest.

In the night, Moiloc and Branwyn take watch first. Moiloc doesn’t really keep watch, as she wants to ask Branwyn what it’s like to have a mom. Branwyn says her first mom left her on her father’s doorstep. Her dad said they had a fling, and the next spring her mom dropped a baby Branwyn off. She says that although she called Mora a mother, it was more like she was an apprentice to her, and at eighteen Mora left too. Moiloc confesses that on the night she was born, the battle of the Kodlak Islands occurred. She also tells Branwyn about her vision from [[Olidamuru|Olidamuru’s]] palace. She says she was a child of the village, so it was kind of like everyone was her parent, which also meant everyone knew her business. Moiloc asks what it was like after her mother and Mora left. Branwyn says she wants to tell everyone. They wake Kaldrake and Brewster up, and Branwyn tells them what she told Moiloc. Branwyn adds that her father was a solitary woodcutter who wasn’t prepared for a child. He wasn’t neglectful, but was taciturn. He would carve her toys and let her explore the woods, her only friend was the forest. In her childhood she learned the village didn’t really care for her. At the age of nine, her father wasn’t able to sell as much woodwork, and they were becoming impoverished, until one day her father gave her a honey cake, which made her so happy, and took her into the woods to work. Here, her father set her on a rock, and told her not to move. He said he’d come back for her, but he never did. She was near death from cold and hunger. After at least two days, just as she started to fade, Mora found her. She now realizes this was purposeful. She says she’s no longer as angry at him, and she can forgive him choosing his survival over her own as Branwyn, but as her daughter she isn’t sure if she’ll ever forgive him. She has no interest in seeing him again. The others console her and hug her as she cries.
# Day 38
## Forest
Kaldrake and Brewster take the next watch. Kaldrake asks if Brewster wants to have a heart to heart, too, and Brewster asks if he wants to know anything. She asks about [[Daphne]], and as Brewster is about to respond, he triggers his magical tools and hears about their time in the plane of death. They tell the others when they wake up, and make the same connections they did before.

Branwyn teaches Helen about strawberries. They make it through the day with no complications. Branwyn hunts for dinner, and finds a rabbit made of stone hopping around. It seems to be red hot at its joints. She then sees a deer made of water, and runs back to the party. Branwyn mentions the primordial rabbit and the deer, saying they’re in the primordial plane. She’s breathing too heavily to understand. She slows down and explains they went through the plane of death into the primordial plane, not back into the material plane. So next would be the plane of fiends. Kaldrake remembers her father talking to him about plane traversal, a newer field of magic which her mom pioneered.

The fiend plane is next, then the fey wild, then the celestial plane, and the plane of life, before looping back to the material plane. Moiloc and Brewster take the first watch. Moiloc asks where Brewster was born. He says Whitehall, and Moiloc says it’s like they’re going home for him. Brewster replies that he doesn’t consider that home anymore. Moiloc asks Brewster to share his story. He says he was born in Whitehall, enslaved in Whitehall, and escaped slavery with [[Tarren Hammerfist|Tarren]]. Then he met three danger magnets. Moiloc eventually realizes she’s she danger magnet, and comments that Danger Magnets would be a good bard group name. Moiloc says she’s sorry that he had to go through that, and says they’ll help him reclaim Whitehall. She accidentally calls him Branwyn.
# Day 39
## Forest
Branwyn and Kaldrake take the next watch. In the night sky, they see meteors falling, then reversing. In the morning, Brewster explains meteors constantly fall from the astral plane into the primordial plane, but he doesn’t know why they’d go backward. Kaldrake admits he’s been praying to Eru, who has been helping him. Brewster is upset about this, but says he’s glad Eru chose that Kaldrake was someone he cared about.

Branwyn notices the sound of branches falling from trees, and grass turning brown, like the forest is starting to die. She recognizes this as being similar to what happened in Coryphea, like the ground has been corrupted, and the forest no longer belongs here.

Kaldrake teaches Helen how to use the sword, and she actually manages to disarm him. Brewster gets her to learn how to put on medium armor. They don’t see anything else that day. Moiloc and Branwyn take the first watch. Moiloc expresses general confusion. In the distance, they see one of the dead trees walking. Branwyn douses the fire. Soon after, Moiloc sees birds flying toward them, and can tell they’re undead. Branwyn wakes Brewster and Kaldrake, and tell them about the treant. Branwyn recognizes the birds as ravens. One of them is white. Moiloc casts Protection from Evil and Good on Helen.

Branwyn explains that she told [[Charlotte]] to send a white raven if she needed her, and tells the others to hide. Brewster uses his Pass Without Trace scroll to hide the group in nearby brush, including Branwyn. The ravens land by the recently distinguished campfire. The white raven begins to snap and transform, into Mora. Branwyn realizes she told Charlotte to find Mora, and she has found them here. She looks different - she has six eyes and four arms, two of which have three fingered claws, she seems bigger, more hunched. She clicks at the campfire, and the raven jumps into her hands. She crushes it, and its blood falls into the campfire. An image of the Whitehall mountain appears, and she flies off to the north. Brewster recognizes that she didn’t seem to be from any realms or beings he had heard of. They talk about what happened, and decide Mora may have used the gold Branwyn gave Charlotte as an item of emotional value to track her. They ask how the raven would have found them otherwise, and Branwyn says she thought it was wood elf magic from her mother, since she named her Branwyn, after a white raven. Kaldrake asks if her mother really named her that. Branwyn bursts into tears.
# Day 40
## Forest
Brewster and Kaldrake are next on watch. Brewster cast Detect Magic, and finds no active magic they don’t know about. Helen is glowing, a wound is carved into her shoulder in the shape of an eyeball, and divination magic. He recognizes the symbol as belonging to Olidamuru’s cult. He tells Kaldrake about this, and they decide they can try to cut into the wound to disrupt the spell. In the morning, Brewster tells Branwyn about the divination spell, and that it may imply that Olidamuru is working with Mora. Branwyn takes this as good news, saying it may mean Charlotte is safe. Branwyn lies and says yes, that’s exactly what it means. Brewster updates his note to add the information about Mora.

They wake up Helen, and Kaldrake breaks the news to her. Helen seems surprised and goes to touch the wound. Brewster recognizes this as a genuine reaction. He tells her they would need to make a deep wound and break the symbol to dispel the magic. She drops at this, thinking. Brewster shakily hands her a piece of paper and pen, and she glares at him before writing something on the paper and giving it to Kaldrake. The paper says “Do it, Kaldrake.” They talk for a time on how to do it. Moiloc provides Guidance, and Kaldrake goes to cut into the wound. He surgically cuts across, and Branwyn goes to bandage it.

They confirm that they are still going to Whitehall. Branwyn teaches her about herbs to stave away infection. Moiloc teacher her to increase her stamina. They make it through the day uneventfully. Moiloc and Brewster take the first shift.
# Day 41
## Forest
Kaldrake and Branwyn take the second shift, and see more treants than the previous night. Branwyn teaches Helen about more medicinal herbs. Moiloc checks the wound but can’t tell if it’s infected. Neither can Branwyn, Kaldrake, or Brewster, but they think it’s probably fine. Moiloc does cardio with her, but the day passes fine. Moiloc and Branwyn go on watch first. Branwyn sees no treants, no birds - nothing.
# Day 42
## Forest
Kaldrake and Brewster get second watch. Kaldrake sees a star next to the North Star. It reminds him of his time in space, when he was glowing, and the light from when they were in his home castle. The star is moving toward Whitehall. He tells Brewster about all of this.

In the morning, Branwyn finishes teaching Helen about survival, with her showing Branwyn she knows some good knowledge. Moiloc has her do one-handed pushups, and ensures there’s no infection to her wound.

That afternoon, the group passes over a hill to see the village of Col Man’Dar. It is cut in half by a river, and the other side of the river is vibrant forest. Brewster mentions that Domnhall Keep has rule over this area, so it may be a bit kinder to non-humans, but they should be safe just in case. Helen doesn’t seem to know how safe it is, writing that she’s never been outside Lothric.
