# Day 54
### Wallachia Forests
In the morning, the group sets out. [[Moiloc Kodlaksdottër|Moiloc]] tries to teach [[Helen]] about armor but fails once again, with Moiloc falling out of the cart during a lesson, leaving a Moiloc-sized hole in the wall. She tries to mend the hole with thread, but doesn’t do it right, so it falls apart again.

As [[Lothar (Brewster) Nash|Brewster]], [[Characters/Players/Martin Sootspot|Martin]], Moiloc, Helen, [[Goro Hattori|Goro]] and [[Jessie Harkness|Jessie's]] caravan slows down to camp for the night, they see an explosion on a hill by the road. They see three robed figures on the hill pointing at them. Martin recognizes them as wizards from the academy.

> [!danger] Fight
> Martin casts Scorching Ray, hitting one of them. One of the wizards cast Fireball on the wagon, but Martin Counterspells it. The other wizards shoot Fire Bolts at Martin. Brewster casts Flaming Sphere near the wizards, ramming it into the hurt ones. Moiloc used Eldritch Blast on one of the wizards, and Martin finishes off the first wizard with a Magic Missile, hurting the same one Moiloc did.
>
> The hurt wizard used Fireball on the wagon again, and Martin is able to stave off most of the damage. The wizard then melts from Brewster’s Flaming Sphere. Goro, Jessie, and Helen, without their weapons, had been staying in the wagon, and are harmed. Brewster rams the Flaming Sphere into the remaining wizard, then casts Eldritch Blast. Moiloc also casts Eldritch Blast, bringing the wizard to very low health. Martin casts Magic Missile.

On the wizards, they find a pouch full of 110g, two scrolls of Mass Cure Wounds, and a letter. The letter is a commission to assassinate Martin, with a bounty of 5000g and a total scholarship, signed by High Mage [[Maelon Switt]]. Martin knows this name, he’s the only mage in line for succession of the academy who isn’t a Sootspot. Goro starts a fire, and the group sets up watch for the night. The group turns back to their burning wagon. 

Moiloc and Goro take the first watch. Goro asks how well Moiloc knows Martin, and she says she knows him well - or that she knew Kaldrake well. He asks if she thinks he would be receptive to hearing what [[Gwyneth Sootspot|Gwyneth]] really has planned. Goro says Gwyneth had been investigating the Great Old Ones. Goro admits that he was alive during the war between the gods and the dragons. Eru descended from the Ringed City and defeated the dragons, or so the stories say. Martin the Great was still alive at the end of the war. There was a country called [[Shurima the Corpsemaker|Shurima]], and its pharaoh was able to find his way to Eru in the Ringed City - and kill her. Somehow he had managed to gain enough power to kill the creator of the universe. The dragon lords were banished, the other Great Old Ones have been consumed, with their powers absorbed into the new gods who supported Shurima. The pharaoh never returned, so many say he died.

Goro traveled the world, trying to find his place, and atoning for what the dragonborn did during the dragon war. He told this story to Gwyneth before she left Montrissad. He believes she’s on her way to Whitehall. Moiloc agrees he should tell Martin about Gwyneth in the morning. Moiloc asks if there are more secrets, and Goro says there are eight dragon lords, each the child of one of the great old ones.

Martin and Brewster take the next shift. Brewster looks out over the hills to the nearby Orkus Badlands, and sees a large wolf on top of a nearby hill, who runs away as soon as he sees it. He recognizes this as the Desert Wolf Bandits’ scout, since he was harassed by them on his way back from Whitehall. They go from the Orkus Badlands to the nearby forests to steal and pillage. When Brewster was attacked, [[Tarren Hammerfist|Tarren]] fought five or so of them off. They decide to wake the group in the morning and leave.
# Day 55
### Mora's Cabin
[[Branwyn of Coryphea|Branwyn]] wakes up on cold, wooden floorboards; everything hurts. She opens her eyes, and recognized Mora’s cabin. There’s a chain nailed in the wall nearby, leading to a collar around her neck. She raises her hands to the collar, and her hands burn with magic. She’s in the bedroom, with a small child in the kitchen: [[Charlotte]], but different. Her ears are pointier, her hair is more blonde. She’s kneeling, and stringing together a wooden bow. Branwyn quietly calls out the Charlotte, then louder. Charlotte sees her, happy that she’s awake, and runs to her to hug her.

Branwyn tells her to listen carefully, asking who brought her here. Charlotte says Mora brought her there, and has been keeping her safe. Branwyn says it’s important that she listens to [[Mora]], and Charlotte says Mora said the same thing. As Charlotte pulls back from the hug, Branwyn sees that Charlotte’s eyes have become green. Branwyn says she wants to surprise Mora, asking if there’s a key. Charlotte says she was outside while Branwyn was being chained up, and that good girls don’t run away, so the collar was put on since she kept running away. Branwyn says if she sees the key, not to grab it, and to tell her about it when they’re alone.

They hear a shrill whistle, and Charlotte walks out of the cabin in a daze. Branwyn gets up and looks at the wall where the chain is welded to a nail in the wall. There’s enough leeway for her to walk around the room, but not to leave. Branwyn pulls at the circle in the wall, but it doesn’t budge. Branwyn hears whispering fill the house. She quickly checks if she’s placed anything under the table, which she hasn’t, then she sits on the bed, preparing for Mora. She hears a knock at the window. Outside the window is a tanned, muscular man, with a covered face, but a glowing eye. She opens the window, and he disappears in a puff of smoke, going behind her, and putting his hand on her shoulder. Branwyn tells him to be careful.

He introduces himself as Shurima, and says she is in danger, but he can get her out of it. Branwyn sees a snake-shaped scar on his chest. He says he will get her out if she can get back his amulet from Mora. Branwyn asks if he would do harm with it, and he says he wouldn’t, but she would need to deliver it to someone. Branwyn says she has learned not to make deals or promises, but she will do her best. Shurima says to look for a gold medallion and deliver it to his champion, then he will free her. He holds his hand out, as if asking her to put her hand in his. He takes a long fingernail, and scratches the symbol of the cult of Olidamuru, and that of the Inquisitors, into the back of her hand. He says it will keep her hidden from Mora once she leaves. The symbol inks into her hand, and Branwyn tears off a bit of fabric and wraps it around her hand.

As soon as he disappears, Branwyn sees the monstrous form of Mora enter the room. Her eyes flash yellow, and Branwyn’s body goes stiff as if she was being paralyzed, but she is able to stave it off. Branwyn pretends to be paralyzed as Mora’s long body coils around her, and she looks into Branwyn’s eyes, stroking her cheek. While Mora speaks to Branwyn, saying she missed her, Branwyn sees the amulet in her robe. She mentions Charlotte, and tells her to remember the price of disobedience. Mora tells her to stay put, and Branwyn secretly snatches the medallion from her.

She hears another tapping on the window, and realizes that Shurima can’t enter the cabin on his own. She opens the window, and Shurima tells her she did a good job. He pulls out [[Gulvayas Hakon|Gulvayas's]] blade, and cuts through Branwyn’s collar like butter. Branwyn holds tight on the medallion. He tells Branwyn to remember to give the medallion over, and that he’s going to transport her closer to Moiloc. Branwyn holds the medallion over the floorboards, and if he wants it, he needs to release the other one too. Shurima says Mora has her talons sunk too deeply into Charlotte, but if she gets the medallion to Moiloc, she may be able to get him enough power to save Charlotte. Branwyn appears in the desert, and hides the medallion. She sighs in resignation.
### Orkus Badlands
In the morning, Goro tells Martin what he told Moiloc the night prior. Brewster says this is suspicious, since a group of hadozee had forgotten their god, this may mean a god that’s alive could have been forgotten from consciousness, and that this is likely Shurima. Moiloc defends Shurima, and has another fight with Brewster about Shurima’s intentions.

As the group enter the Orkus Badlands, they see a bearded man with fine leathers. Brewster is suspicious, saying they can’t waste time on this, but Moiloc tells him not to worry and that she’ll do the talking. This worries Brewster, who hops out behind her. The man raises his hands, saying they’re getting robbed. Moiloc laughs, saying they don’t have much to steal. They hear paws on dirt.

One misses, but the other wolf bites into his arm. The bandit from the road runs up to Helen, slashing her with both of his swords. She falls on the ground, bleeding heavily.

>

The bandits that were attaching Martin moves on to Jessie and Brewster, downing Jessie, and throwing a net on Brewster. Martin uses Scorching Ray on the three bandits surrounding Brewster, hitting all three. The bandit that Brewster and Moiloc had been attacking dies as the wolf runs off whimpering. The bandits near Brewster move to attack Moiloc, slashing her with their sword. The bandit near Puck attacks him, but misses. Martin uses Flaming Bolt on the remaining bandits, hitting the one who threw the net on Brewster, and Blinks away. Moiloc uses her fire long sword on the same bandit, hitting him. Goro attacks their bandit with his warhammer, as does Helen.

Martin uses Chromatic Orb on one of the bandits near Brewster, and Blinks away again. Moiloc misses her fire longsword attack, and Goro manages to kill the bandit by them. Brewster uses Ray of Sickness on a bandit, killing him, but the wolf stays to fight. Puck rams into the remaining bandit. Looking through the bodies, they find 100g, and a poorly drawn map, showing the location of a bandit camp nearby. They continue, and are able to make it to Domhnall Keep. The village outside seems more populated will all types of humanoid species.
### Domhnall Keep
The group proceeds to the Silver Wreath Inn. There are patrons wearing clothes similar to the bandits, being rowdy in a corner. There’s an innkeeper behind the nearby bar. Moiloc asks how much the rooms cost, and they get 3 rooms for a total for 15g a night total. She says Shmebulock will walk them to their rooms. They tip him 2g, then Moiloc tips him another 1g. They decide who will room with who, as Brewster eyes Martin pleadingly. Moiloc asks if Goro wants to room with her, and they walk off, and Helen asks if Martin wants to room with her, and he accepts. Jessie and Brewster are left to room together as well.

Moiloc asks Goro if he has any more secrets. Goro tells Moiloc the story of how he met [[King Aslan]], who was also around at the end of the great dragon war, and when the dragonborn were hunted down after. Moiloc does not share the rest of this information with the others. Brewster asks Jessie what their plan is, and she says she’s going into the keep to meet up with [[Violet]] and [[Feris Weal|Feris]] in the morning.
### Orkus Badlands
Branwyn uses Primeval Awareness and finds that there are elementals within a mile of her. She starts walking toward the Keep. She goes over a nearby dune, seeing a small oasis, with a dehydrated wolf next to it. She approaches the water to help the wolf, but the water reaches up and grabs her. It’s a thirstwater, and begins to drain Branwyn.

Branwyn attempts to slice at it with her dagger, and cuts it off like gelatin. It shrinks back down into the water, and the bit on her arm droops off. She drags the wolf away from the oasis, and gives some of the water from the water skin into her hands, offering it to the wolf. A sheen of blue surrounds the wolf, and it transforms into a lanky, male orc, wearing furs. Branwyn hands him the water skin and he drinks the rest of it. He says he’s been running for days in the form of a wolf. He reaches into his furs, and gives her a leather-bound tome. She asks what it is, in orkish, which he seems to appreciate. He says it’s a tome that will imbue her with strength, turns into a wolf, then runs off. She reads the book, permanently gaining strength.

Branwyn is able to make it to Domhnall Keep, and finds the Silver Wreath Inn. She hears shouting and a crash as a large orc is thrown out of a window. She enter the bar fight, where a bunch of patrons seem to be fighting a human man with a wide-brimmed hat. The innkeeper is cleaning the bar, wiping down the mess as it is formed, and nursing a patron back to health. Branwyn approaches, and buys the last room, in the basement. Branwyn asks for a drink, and gets one on the house. She proceeds to her room in the cellar.
