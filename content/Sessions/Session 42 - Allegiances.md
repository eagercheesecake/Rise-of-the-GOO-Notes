### Synopsis

# Day 65
## Velbras
A day after leaving the mountain pass, [[Galen Laborde|Galen]] gets frostbite, but heals herself quickly. [[Moiloc Kodlaksdottër|Moiloc]]purchases a room at the Rusty Spoon and takes an extensive rest. The group arrives at 8am the next morning, entering as the bazaar opens. Moiloc wakes up and decides to enter the market as well.
## Cat's Paw
The group proceeds to the Cat’s Paw to see if Hadrian will let them take refuge, but Galen catches that they are being followed by a pickpocket. Galen decides to approach him, asking where he thinks he’ll go when he dies. He turns around and runs. The group enters the Cat’s Paw, which looks untouched since they had last been there. They take their old room, and begin a rest. Branwyn casts Alarm on the door to their room, and Galen begins deciphering Gwyneth’s spell book.

That evening, [[Janos Lawrence]] arrives, silhouetted by the sunset. He sees a knight in full armor with a large helmet. He asks the guard if he had seen Olidamuru or Galen, describing them visually, and he remarks that he hasn’t. Janos asks to be pointed to an inn, and heads to the Rusty Spoon. She is past tipsy on cactus wine. Janos hears her and decides to join. They have a lively conversation, where Moiloc tells him about Shurima, Olidamuru, and some details of the fates of the other Roosevelts. Eventually, Janos learns that the others would be likely to go to the Cat’s Paw. They are able to enter, and Moiloc passes out drunk. Jalen tries to get her to bed, but they set off Branwyn’s alarm while trying to break in.

Moiloc breaks down the door with her fire longsword to see an alerted Branwyn pointing her bow at Moiloc. Branwyn tells her to reach her hand through the hole on the door and open it, but she is too drunk to, so Janos asks if they can do it instead. Janos recognizes Branwyn and Galen, and Galen and Branwyn recognize the Maedrosi armor, causing tensions to rise. Janos suggests a game where he flips a coin- if it lands on heads, he can ask a question, and if it lands on tails, they can. Moiloc flips a coin, which Janos takes and flips repeatedly, showing that more heads arise than tails, claiming that he has a resistance to chaos. During the game, Janos admits that he has beaten Olidamuru in the past, and is looking to fight the corpse eaters. The others say [[Lexar Bellos|Lexar]] disappeared, and say Olidamuru let them go after capturing Moiloc because he thought they would be more entertaining to him alive, leading Janos to believe that Olidamuru knows them personally.

Janos proposes they team up, and Branwyn hesitantly agrees, under the condition that she can talk to Moiloc first. She tells Moiloc off again for constantly trusting Shurima over them. Meanwhile, Janos pulls Galen aside to ask about her allegiance with Maedros, although neither of them will say anything too incriminating. Branwyn makes it clear to Moiloc that she will have no part in helping Moiloc help Shurima. Moiloc confirms with Janos that the emerald in the auction brochure is, in fact, likely Olidamuru’s artifact. Galen trusts Janos to look at Gwyneth’s journal, although he doesn’t find anything she hasn’t already seen.
## Market
The group proceeds to the night market. Moiloc gets an endless water decanter, and the group gets various upgrades on their weapons and armor. They try to buy candles, and are able to haggle with the camel salesman, saying they will retrieve the ring he lost while gambling in exchange for four camels. They challenge the jeweler who won it from him to another game, and win it back with Janos’s gambling abilities. They are ambushed on their way out, but Janos is able to scare them off. As they exit the alley, Janos tries to ask Galen if her allegiance to Maedros is true, hinting that his isn't, but the risk of being exposed is too high for either to outright admit it. They hand it in to the camel merchant, and begin to head out to find Vulkan’s people. Galen reports back to Maedros with a letter, and Janos reports the fates of the Roosevelts to King David.
