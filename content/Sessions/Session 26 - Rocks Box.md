### Logline

### Synopsis
The group discusses plans for the nights, and decides to all go to the chapel. Martin and Branwyn will go first, and Moiloc and Brewster will leave five minutes later. They take a short rest, and upon waking up, Shmebulock knocks on the door and gives Moiloc a letter. She opens it, and realizes it’s for Martin. It’s from Maelon Switt, threatening to get Martin if he stays in the Keep for three days. Martin doesn’t seem worried.

Martin and Branwyn leave first, with Brewster and Moiloc trailing close behind. Martin and Moiloc cast Invisibility on themselves. The streets are completely empty. They arrive at the church, but don’t see anyone around. It’s a church for Eru. Branwyn sneaks around to the back, and sees a back door, and one open grave in the back as if they were preparing a burial. Moiloc recognizes this church used to be for Sha’Shime. Martin and Branwyn try the back and front doors, but all doors are locked.

Martin and Brewster take post in front of the church. Branwyn casts Rope Trick, and she and Martin climb through an open window into a loft in the attic. They see a trapdoor, and proceed down to the church’s kitchen, where there is a man chopping vegetables. They proceed back into the attic, and push through some boxes in the back. They see the outline of another trapdoor under some crates. Martin pushes the top two crates aside, but the bottom is extra heavy. They open it, to find it is full of rocks.

Brewster sends Puck into the window. Puck finds them removing rocks from a crate. Martin tells Puck to tell Brewster to knock on the door to get the man out of the kitchen. He knocks too quietly, and asks Moiloc to knock instead. Branwyn sneaks out behind the man as he leaves the kitchen. An older man answers the door. Brewster says he’s a scholar with an interest in churches, and has heard that this church has a rich history. The friar is suspicious, puts his hand behind his back, and welcomes him in for a tour. Brewster comes in, and Moiloc remains standing guard outside. Puck joins Martin in the attic. Moiloc begins to feel very cold as the wind picks up.

Branwyn enters a closed door in the hall that the friar left through to get to the door, to find a pantry. She listens down the hall and hears Brewster asking for a tour and being led inside. The pastor asks how much Brewster knows about Eru, and he says it’s a lot, asking about modern details about Eru’s statue. The friar imparts some information, before telling Brewster to proceed to the library. As he does so, Branwyn and Brewster feel a splitting headache. Kaldrake is able to remove the last crate to reveal the trapdoor. There are two chains holding it down. Martin casts Message to tell Branwyn this, and she comes back up the ladder.

Moiloc hears a buzzing in her ears, and sees a little insect around her head. She realizes this is the first insect she’s seen in a bit. She goes to smash it, but it dodges and stops moving. Something pierces the back of her neck, and she goes unconscious.

Brewster finds the back door, also with a Protection Against Good and Evil sigil on it. He unlocks the multiple locks, opens the door, and gets Puck inside. He learns about the trapdoor in the kitchen. While proceeding to the kitchen, he opens a door to find the friar’s bedroom. He scampers into the attic. Martin sits on the trapdoor. Branwyn tells Brewster to pick the locks, but he expressed worry that it’s keeping something in rather than keeping them out. Martin realizes the spell is different than a normal Protect Against Good and Evil spell, and asks Brewster to recreate the circle. He is able to, and Martin realizes it also protects from lycanthropes. They connect the dots, realize it’s a full moon, and realize everyone had locked themselves indoors. After remembering that Moiloc is outside alone, they run to where they left her, and realize she is gone. Brewster falls out the window onto drag marks. They begin to head in that direction to find Moiloc, down a nearby alley.

They hear buzzing and see bugs flying around them. Brewster realizes it looks to be a desert mosquito. He casts Flaming Sphere to act as a bug zapper. Martin suddenly drops to the ground. Branwyn goes to check Martin’s body, and sees her slump over as well. Brewster panics, jumping into the alley to hide away. He tries to see what happened, but can’t see any signs. Brewster casts Sanctuary, and drags Martin into the alley. He sees a dart in the back of Martin’s neck. He takes the gauntlets from Martin, and begins dragging him down the alley. He hears a scream from the church. They proceed down the alley, and sees Moiloc’s body, unconscious behind the barrel. He opens the barrel to find rotten fish, and slips into the barrel, which is full of rotten fish.

A werewolf approaches, sniffing Brewster’s allies. His sniffing leads him to the barrel, which he slaps over. Brewster manages to stay inside the barrel as it falls over. The werewolf eats the rotten fish, and once he finishes, and turns the barrel to face him. When he looks in the barrel, he is greeted by Brewster, magic crossbow in hand. He shoots the werewolf in the snout, causing it to stagger back. He summons Puck between them, and brandishes a torch, crawling out of the barrel. As the werewolf leaves the alley, Brewster sees a metal tube come out from the side of the alley, and hears three loud pops, as the werewolf’s dead explodes. The cowboy who invited Branwyn enters the alley, and takes two hairs from Branwyn’s head.

As he passes Brewster, Brewster calls on him to wait. He asks where he learned to use that gun from. The cowboy smiles, and asks what Brewster knows about gods. Brewster asks what he knows, asking if he’s been to the Ringed City. The cowboy chuckles, saying he’s never had the honor, and asks if Brewster has killed a god. Brewster says he hasn’t, but is hoping to. The cowboy asks who he is hoping to kill, and he says he wants to kill Shurima. The cowboy gives him some gold, and says to meet him at the chapel in the morning. Brewster agrees, asking how to wake his friends. He leaves, saying they’ll wake up in a couple hours.

Brewster explains what happened, saying he couldn’t stop the cowboy from taking the hairs because he was pooped from killing a werewolf. Martin seems confused, asking if Brewster pooped. Brewster gets upset, saying he saved Martin, and is owed an apology. He casts Friends, but Martin still doesn’t apologize.
