### Logline
*The party hires mercenaries for their mission in Whitehall.*
### Synopsis
Moiloc meets Reptile at the mercenary guild, and invites him to join their party if he can get his siblings into the Lyceum. Brewster and Martin explore an archaeological site. Martin meets with a friend at the Lyceum, recruiting her to join them at Whitehall. Moiloc and Brewster collaborate on killing an angry orc. Branwyn gets a tour of the Mausoleum of Dario the Twin, learning of Dario's birth and death. Brewster meets a cleric, recruiting her to join them at Whitehall. They also find someone who can heal Helen's tongue. Moiloc talks to Reptile, recruiting him under the condition that they find a way to enroll his siblings at the Lyceum. She also recruits another woman from the mercenary's guild.
# Day 60
### Velbrass
[[Moiloc Kodlaksdottër|Moiloc]], [[Martin Sootspot|Martin]], [[Helen Berestova]], and [[Tarren Hammerfist|Tarren]] follow the pickpocket. They enter the hovel to see a door slam closed. Inside the room is a raggedy sack. Martin comments that they are just breaking into a poor person’s house, and Martin says he could just call him a person. Martin sighs and leaves the hovel. Helen shows him her new ring, which frustrates him.

Moiloc checks the bag, and just sees rags. She hears two people talking in the next room, but can’t make out what they’re saying. She knocks on the door, and they immediately stop talking. She introduces herself through the door, saying she knows she broke into their house but doesn’t mean them any harm, she just knows there are children in need and wants to be nosy. She offers them a ruby, and the child she saw in the market immediately opens the door. He’s about 16, and has one arm. She shows them that her amulet is being pulled toward him, and he shows her that he has the same eye carved into his chest, saying [[Shurima the Corpsemaker|Shurima]] told him she might be coming. He asks where she’s staying, and she says she’s at the Cat’s Paw. He says his name is Reptile, and shakes her hand with a gauntlet that suddenly forms out of shadows. He says he is caring for a ten year old, and a baby less than one year old. He says he’ll meet her at the mercenary guild, Sellsword’s Sell-swords.

When Moiloc leaves, Martin asks what happened, as she was able to hear most of it. He casts Detect Thoughts and learns exactly what happened. Martin says Moiloc cannot invite this child to Whitehall with them, and when she doesn’t fully agree, Martin decides that Moiloc will not be going alone to meet with Reptile tomorrow.
### The Cat's Paw
Moiloc and Martin meet up at the Cat’s Paw and [[Branwyn of Coryphea|Branwyn]] brings them to the room. Two doors are locked, and two are open, with four beds in each room. They tell Branwyn about what happened, and Moiloc said she found a buddy. Martin is quick to correct her, saying he’s a follower of Shurima. They discuss their plans for the next day before going to bed.

Branwyn dreams that she is in a foggy wood in front of the tree that hosts [[Maokai|Maokai’s]] form. At the base of the tree is the shattered lamp. The dream pushes her into the tree, and instead of Maokai, she sees the upper half of a body, overgrown with moss and fungus, and with a black, burnt stone at its heart.
# Day 61
### The Cat's Paw
In the morning, [[Lothar (Brewster) Nash|Brewster]] finds he is unable to open his door, and unable to unlock it, since he had forgotten that he had cast Arcane Lock on the door the prior night. Martin casts Detect Magic on the door while Brewster tells them about [[the Bagworm]]. Once he learns that there’s a magic lock on the door, he realizes that he had been drinking and dispels his Arcane Lock. He looks a mess, and goes to clean himself up. They proceed up to the dining area to meet up with the rest of the party. Moiloc is excited to go see Reptile, but the group manages to get her to calm down until the mercenary guild opens. During Detect Magic, they also notice a magical presence on the whole room.
### Tower of Erebus
Martin, Brewster, [[Goro Hattori|Goro]], and Helen go to the Lyceum. On their way, they see signs for an archeological dig on a site believed to be the Tower of Erebus, a large black tower buried in the sand. Martin explains that he has a friend in town named Lucretia who might be able to guide them around the town. They pass through some ruins to the tower. Most of the tower is smooth, but on the door is a skeleton. A group of wizards are at the door with their hands raised, chanting. Suddenly, there’s an explosion, knocking the wizards back, turning one to paste. Martin recognizes one of them as [[Lucretia]], and runs to help her. Brewster goes to help up a gnome wizard. Brewster gives him a healing potion. He tells Brewster about the Lyceum, and returns to the door. Martin is pushed aside as a cleric heals Lucretia. The cleric says to keep an eye on her. Martin leaves her to meet up with another wizard, who says the door does this every time they try and open it. The wizard says the door is locked via an evocation magic, and the whole tower has a magical signature on it. The wizard introduces himself as Antonio, and Martin introduces himself as well. Antonio seems surprised that a Sootspot is here, and that he hadn’t heard of Martin as much.

Lucretia wakes up, and is very excited upon seeing Martin. They hug it out, and Martin introduces the others. Lucretia says she needs to rest after being knocked out, but says that not everyone will be able to fit. Brewster offers to take Helen to the cleric who revived Lucretia to try and get her tongue back. Goro joins Martin to Lucretia’s.

Brewster and Helen proceed to the cleric, [[Ishtar]]. Brewster says he can see that she’s busy but that he has a question for her when she’s free. He explains the situation, and she says it’s probably not something she can handle, but she can take him to someone who can: [[Hadrian]].
### Sellsword's Sell-Swords
Moiloc, Branwyn, [[Vulcan]], Tarren, and [[William Archer|William]] proceed to Sellsword’s. When they arrive, they find that it doesn’t open for another 30 minutes. Nearby is a silver mine and the royal palace. They also see a mausoleum with [[Dario the Twin|Dario’s]] symbol on it. The crypt is open, and leads under the palace.
### The Mausoleum of Dario the Twin
Branwyn, William, and Vulkan proceed into the crypt. Inside is a brazier lighting a circular room. There is a wooden door on the other side of the room, with two guards, wearing a pendant of a finger bone and ruby heart. Branwyn recognizes it of a pendant of Dario’s followers. The finger represents Dario himself, and the heart is representative of love. The guards let her enter, revealing a chapel behind the doors. The chapel has many pews in it, and a cleric on a pedestal. Branwyn recalls that Dario’s worshippers are called Valentines. They take a vow of poverty and are known to be very charitable. The cleric’s robes are clean, but very plain, and not embellished. They are zealous, and have a more rigorous prayer ritual. On the cleric’s neck is a pendant, emanating magical energy. He asks what she is there for, and she asks to learn more about Dario. The cleric asks them to follow them into the crypt, but Vulcan says he may be too big, and stays in the chapel with William.
### Sellsword's Sell-Swords
Moiloc enters the mercenary guild when it opens. Moiloc and Tarren sit down at a nearby table. An orc with a battle axe approaches her, saying she’s at his table. She asks if she could sit with him, and he says no, telling her to get up. She says she doesn’t like his attitude and refuses. He asks what she’s going to do about it, and she takes out her fire longsword. She asks if they should take it outside. Tarren asks if she can handle it, and when she says yes, he sits back down.

> [!danger] Fight
> The orc charges at her, and she parries his battle axe out of the way. He looks confused, and slices again, harder, cutting into her. Moiloc uses her longsword and slashes back at him, causing his armor to fall to tatters. He slashes at her twice, but can’t get through her armor. She cuts across his arm, angering him into a whirlwind of fierce blows. Moiloc goes low, slashing across his thigh. He looks at her, steeled, and slashes twice at her. She uses Vow of Enmity after missing. His pendant glows, causing a third eye to appear, and he attacks slowly enough for her to step to the side, allowing her to knock him out with her sword.

Moiloc uses Lay on Hands to bring him back. She says he was a formidable foe, and would love if he joined her. He spits at her and walks off, so Moiloc re-enters the guild and puts her boots on his table. She finds that Reptile has joined her table, and tries to introduce Tarren, but Reptile says that he knows Tarren from when they were enslaved at Whitehall. He was occasionally assigned to the same work details. Moiloc gets some grilled lizard for the table. He tells the story of escaping slavery about two weeks ago. His brother died, but Shurima appeared and saved him and his sisters. He said Shurima told him that if someone comes looking for help, that he should do what he can. Reptile says he will do anything to free the slaves of Whitehall. He’s been working on getting his sisters somewhere to live in the Lyceum. They’re working on getting the tuition for the Lyceum, but still need 500 gold. Moiloc asks if he can fight, and he says he’s better at sneaking. She also asks if there’s anyone else who would be willing to help. He says the Valentines tend to be charitable, and maybe the [[Sultan Anu|sultan]] would help, but he tries to keep a low profile and doesn’t know much. She gives him ten gold to get him through the next couple of days safely, and says she will see if he can come with them to Whitehall.

Ishtar talks to Brewster, who tells her he’s a traveling merchant from Brasov. She says that as a Valentine she can’t really leave her post, so she doesn’t travel much. The orc Moiloc attacked runs into Brewster, and he casts Ray of Sickness, killing him. This upsets Ishtar, making her realize that Brewster was lying about being a merchant, pushing him to tell her the truth. He says he won’t tell her in a public street. They proceed to the crypt and find Vulcan and William, who say Hadrian and Branwyn went into the crypt about 30 minutes ago. Helen joins them, and Brewster and Ishtar proceed down the crypt. He tells her about wanting to free the slaves of Whitehall, and she seems interested in joining, asking if he could ask Hadrian if she could join. Brewster stresses the danger, but she says she’s already sold.
### The Lyceum
Lucretia asks Martin how long he’s been in town. Martin notices the Lyceum is much more lavish than he was told. Inside are many wizards in different colored robes. They pass through a library with spinning bookshelves. She uses a pendant to unlock her dorm room, and they enter. Goro says he’s going to stay outside.

Martin tells Lucretia about her dad dying and the Ethereum being attacked by drow. Martin was recovering from a school-related injury at the time, and couldn’t fight. He saw some friends die. His father turned into a being of light on his hundredth birthday, and fell from the sky, dead. His mother was heartbroken, and hasn’t been seen since. Gwyneth was named the head wizard. They cremated his father. Martin explains that he’s joined a group who is trying to take down evil forces aspiring to become gods in Whitehall. Lucretia mentions that they mentioned Daphne in their history class, and says she’d love to join them to ask Daphne about the tower of Erebus. Lucretia says Erebus was unearthed a year ago, and they originally thought it was from Shurima, but found it was much older. Nobody seems to have ever heard of it, except for the skeleton, which was a common image of a long-forgotten god.

Lucretia says she is happy to join, but they’ll need to talk to the headmaster first. With all the commotion about King David, they shouldn’t need an appointment. They grab Goro and proceed to the top of the Lyceum, and find that the stairs don’t go anywhere. Lucretia raises her hands, and the staircase raises up into a greenhouse. They see a halfling gnome wearing green robes, who jumps in fear upon realizing he has guest. He jauntily asks what their business is, putting his hands behind his back and floating toward them. He introduces himself as Grand Wizard Diggle Switt, cousin of [[Maelon Switt|Maelon]]. Maelon tried to kill him once, so he threw him out of the window, thinking he had Levitate prepared. He didn’t. Diggle asks if Martin is hoping to join the student exchange student. He offers Martin a mango that he invented, which is sitting inside of a flytrap. Martin uses Mage Hand to take it, and finds it to be the most succulent fruit he had ever tasted.

Martin requests a release form for Lucretia. A quill flies in and signs Diggle’s name. Diggle again offers Martin a spot at their university, saying he could continue his divination studies, as they have a great program. He tells her to stop by the scroll room on their way out. He says he is late for a meeting, and teleports the pair out of the room.
### The Mausoleum of Dario the Twin
Branwyn and Hadrian reach the end of the tour, and Hadrian opens a portcullis, asking that she leaves her weapons outside of the room out of respect. Branwyn asks if he will join her in the room, and he says he will. She tells him to enter first. He agrees, understanding her suspicion. Beanwyn drops her items and they proceed in to find a tapestry of the sultan, who looks exactly like King Aslan. Branwyn asks if they are from the modern age, and he says they were commissioned by the current sultan, so they are fairly new. Some depict Dario’s birth with Celica, and his great battle in which Dario was slain. At the center of the room is a casket for an elven man, and Hadrian says it is the final resting place of Dario. Branwyn confirms that Dario and Celica were made into gods, not born. During the Great Dragon Strife, Dario was the first god to fall. Hadrian says they have not forgotten their god, since he is not fully dead. He shows Branwyn his pendant, the Everheart, containing part of Dario’s soul. Eventually they hope to find a way to bring him back. He says the ruler of Shurima had entered the Ringed City, and nobody knows what happened to him since. Branwyn mentions that she had met King Aslan, and the sultan, Anu, is his brother. The sultan’s rule was won militarily after he partnered with the Lyceum, and since the sultan doesn’t have any heirs, the next in line would be his niece.

As they leave, Brewster and Ishtar catch up. Brewster apologizes for interrupting, and asks if Ishtar can join them in Whitehall. Hadrian says since she is his most powerful cleric, she needs to have a good reason. She says she wants to help them eliminate slavery in Whitehall, a mission which should align with the values of the church. Hadrian allows her to go on the mission, and gives her his pendant, anointing her a full cleric. To become a full cleric, you must sacrifice something for another, and relinquishing her position as Hadrian’s right hand is a large sacrifice to save strangers. Brewster asks Ishtar about Helen, and she asks Hadrian if he can heal her. He agrees to help, and they lead him to Helen.
### The Lyceum
Martin, Goro, and Lucretia continue to the library of scrolls. The librarian screams to them, asking what they need, saying he is loud because a spell backfired. Martin can’t find any scrolls of Revivify, which makes sense, since the art of making Revivify scrolls. Martin takes a Shatter, Avard’s Black Tentacles, and Remove Curse scroll. As Martin leaves, he gets a letter carried to him by a raven. It’s from Maelon Switt, who is disappointed that he didn’t keep their appointment in Domnhall Keep, and asking him to stay in Velbras so he can meet up with him and kill him in two days.
### Sellsword's Sell-Swords
Moiloc approaches a nearby [[Holliday|woman]] who was listening in, asking her about the key players in the town. She says that would be the sultan, Diggle, and the Bagworm. The woman says she is a bounty hunter, sitting pretty after her last job: killing a Lyceum wizard. Moiloc asks what she knows about Whitehall, but she says she doesn’t go there that often due to the racists. Moiloc says she wants to make the racists pay, but doesn’t know their exact plan as to how. She asks if their plan is to free the slaves or kill someone. The woman seems interested, asking how much Moiloc is paying. Moiloc says she doesn’t know, and doesn’t even have the money.
