### Logline
*The group recuperates, and begins heading back to Velbrass.*
### Synopsis
Branwyn discusses the lasting effects of Mora's magic with Goro and Daphne, who decide to take the children until the magic can be dispelled. Brewster and Tarren decide to leave and help the remaining slaves in Whitehall. The others begin returning to Velbrass, being attacked by some trolls on the way. Shurima sends Moiloc to find the last artifact of Olidamuru, the Chaos Emerald, so he can merge with the last remnant of Olidamuru's being. Moiloc appears in Velbrass, and drinks alcohol for the first time while waiting for the others to arrive. She is able to break into the gambling den again, and find a listing for the Chaos Emerald, being sold at a nearby town. Another citizen is used as her scapegoat.
# Day 64
### Mountains
It’s 2am. The group has set up camp near a cliff in the mountains. [[Branwyn of Coryphea|Branwyn]] and [[Galen Laborde|Galen]] are on watch. [[Goro Hattori|Goro]] is up, watching over [[Azura]]. Branwyn goes up to him. He says there hasn’t been a Dragonborn baby in over a century, expressing surprise that one could even exist. He says he must protect her. Branwyn admits that some of [[Mora|Mora’s]] power is likely still dormant within Azura. Galen is listening in, crocheting funeral shrouds. The pair agree that they need to track down the other children who were under Mora’s watch. [[Vulcan]] says his people may be able to help contact the winds and track down the other children.

Branwyn approaches [[Tobias Grande|Tobias]], noticing he isn’t wearing a shirt, and is freezing. She tries to reach out, but shocks him, and he flinches away. Branwyn says she has some questions, and tries to get him to eat, but he refuses. She treads carefully, but Tobias ensures her that she doesn’t need to hedge her words. She asks what happened to his daughter, and he explained that his father took his daughter in order to gift her to Mora. He says that many slave children were given to Mora in this way. Branwyn confirms with Tobias that he was only able to track her via his regular tracking methods. He had tracked her to Grendel in the Badlands, but it turned out to just be a trap, and he was captured and given to Gwyneth. It seems that Mora can only claim children as a gift from people who have some form of ownership over the child, and Tobias believes that in exchange for his daughter, Mora helped his father gain power. Tobias eventually admits that he made a deal to give a part of himself away to [[Mephistopheles the Cold|Mephistopheles]]. He also tells of a daughter of Mora in Grendel, but he doesn’t know much. Tobias asks about her parents, and Branwyn lets slip that her father always referred to her mother as a “spirit of the woods,” dismissing it is just something he would say about her, but Tobias recognizes the phrase. He says he’s heard tales of nymphs and dryads, and not an actual wood elf, since the Sootspots were the only remaining elves.

As the sun rises, the others wake up. [[Ishtar]] is cooking giant eagle eggs. Branwyn and [[Lothar (Brewster) Nash|Brewster]] talk about next steps. Branwyn says she is following Vulcan. Brewster admits that this is probably incompatible with his goal for saving the slaves, which he is done putting off. He decided to leave the party. Branwyn refuses to let him do so without talking to [[Daphne]], explaining [[Callia|Callia’s]] predicament. They have a farewell, Brewster saying he hopes that Branwyn finds a better party next go around. Brewster gets Daphne, who clearly doesn’t want to talk, and leaves her with Branwyn to explain. Brewster returns to [[Tarren Hammerfist|Tarren]] to give him King Endrio’s cloak, and they agree to leave together and continue freeing the slaves. Brewster gives Branwyn the necklace of adaptation and the gun. Brewster and Tarren begin packing up to leave, and [[Nissa]] catches up to them, asking to join them in Whitehall. Tarren admits that their interests don’t align, but Nissa assures him they do, saying she just wants to fight the next battle. Brewster thinks she doesn’t care to rule Whitehall, remembering his deal with the Sultan, and accepts her offer.
### Shurima's Throne
[[Moiloc Kodlaksdottër|Moiloc]] clears her eyes and finds herself standing in a vast throne room, surrounded by sixteen small stone thrones, surrounding eight pedestals. In the center is a giant throne, and sitting atop it is [[Shurima the Corpsemaker|Shurima]], hunched over, twitching violently. [[Reptile]] is next to her, unconscious. Moiloc wakes Reptile up. They approach Shurima, who greets her between twitches, saying he needs her help. He says the ritual didn’t work, and he didn’t absorb all of Olidamuru. It seems [[Olidamuru]] made an artifact, a final piece of him that Shurima would have to absorb to finish the ritual. He says it’s in Grendel. Moiloc asks for another boon from Shurima, who says he is too weak, so she asks for a snack. Seeing his face, she agrees to leave with nothing. Shurima says he can use the last of his power to send her to Velbrass. She needs to collect the Chaos Emerald and bring it to him. She asks if Reptile will join her, and he says he has another task for her. He says her party is going to Velbrass as well, and she can rejoin them there. He will reward her with the power to save her home from [[Sekollah]].
### Velbrass
Moiloc appears in Velbrass, in the Lyceum. She finds a book on the history of the Orcus Badlands, although it is too big for her to locate any information on the chaos emerald. She gets her bearings and pulls a librarian aside, but he can’t get her any books on magical artifacts, as they are all in the restricted area, and she would need permission from Diggle Switt to get access. She begins to suspect that this library assistant put a sleeping spell on the head librarian to get a promotion. She decides to go to the Cat’s Paw to await her friends, to find it fully closed. She peeks through the window to see some Valentines cleaning up the place. She proceeds to the Rusty Spoon to get a room, to find some early-morning drunkards on the steps. She enters, to find the room hazy with smoke and scattered with various inebriated customers. Moiloc approaches the bar, hoping to get the barkeep’s attention. She raises a hand to order a drink. She gets a steaming, dark, drink, and takes a sip, realizing it is immensely strong. She prods the nearby man, asking if he is here by himself, which seems to insult him. She tries to cover by asking if the seat is taken. He says she is too young for his taste, and she is taken aback by the misunderstanding. She takes another sip, and begins to feel warmth from the drink. She says she’s passing, and he asks her business. She admits she is an emerald smuggler, or an intern for an emerald smuggler. The man begins pressing, asking where they are, but she says she doesn’t know, but they are getting them from Velbrass. He tells her they are in Velbrass, and she says they must have already stolen them. He says she must have given them to the den, and she doesn’t disagree. He begins to speed-walk out the door after saying goodbye.

Moiloc follows the man to the Den. Moiloc recognizes it as the Gambling Den she burnt down. Moiloc casts Invisibility as she follows the man inside. She sees a doorway once inside, guarded by two bugbears. The old man whispers something, and they both bow and gesture toward the door. Moiloc is able to sneak in behind him. Moiloc sees the man enter a side room, and goes to listen against the door. She hears some light whispering, then a loud slam, at which point she opens the door. She sees a nice office, with the man sitting at a desk, and a tiefling man, dead, whose head has just been slammed against the desk. The man begins looting through the desks. Moiloc is able to knock the man out, and begins rummaging through the desk herself. She finds a healing potion, and tries to rip open a locked drawer. She rifles through the tiefling’s pocket, getting blood on her hands, and finds a keyring. She is eventually able to get it open, but it is empty. She finds a false bottom, and tries to remove it with her dagger. She hears a loud, blaring noise, realizing she activated an alarm spell. The two bugbears smash the door down, and sees the scene, declaring the man killed their boss. They drag him away to the other room. She takes the keyring and enters the storage room. She opens a sack to find a bunch of red diamonds, which she loots. She unlocks a chest and opens it to find some gold. She returns to the desk, smashing the drawer open, times with the screams of the tortured man, and finds a letter for the Grendel auction house, outlining its menu for the next month: powerful magical artifacts, Nito’s spear of longevity, and the gemstone of heaven, which may be what she’s looking for. She leaves the Den, and decides to go shopping.

Suddenly, two very tall, fanged humanoids appear, with blue skin and stone clubs. They are covered in warts and pustules. Branwyn recognizes them as mountain trolls.
