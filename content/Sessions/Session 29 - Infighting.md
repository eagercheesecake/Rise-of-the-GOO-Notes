### Logline
*The party fights each other in a crypt.*
### Synopsis
The party enters Sha-Sĭme's Crypt and solve various puzzles. They are made to fight each other in a fake battle put on by the Crypt, which Martin wins. Martin is rewarded with the Bushido, Sha-Sĭme's sword. They learn of the history of Sha-Sĭme, Atalanta, and Rekk-To. In Velbrass, Moiloc and Kaldrake sell the spices from the bandit's camp and chase after a pickpocket. Branwyn and Brewster meet with the Bagworm.
# Day 59
### Domnhall Keep
The party reconvenes, and [[Branwyn of Coryphea|Branwyn]] introduces [[Vulcan]]. They move off of the main road and sleep. [[Tarren Hammerfist|Tarren]], [[Lothar (Brewster) Nash||Brewster]], and [[Martin Sootspot|Martin]] take first watch. [[Moiloc Kodlaksdottër||Moiloc]] and Branwyn talk to Vulcan. He says he was prisoner for seven months. He’s on his kadash, a pilgrimage of his people. He was taken by the bandits as a trophy. Branwyn warns him about Whitehall, and he promises to continue following them through it.
# Day 60
### Domnhall Keep
In the morning, Martin tells them about the coins, and suggest they stop to look for treasure, possibly including the sword of [[Sha-Sĭme]]. Branwyn suggests they not do it if it takes too much time, but Martin is able to convince her. They follow the map to find a dais depicting Sha’Sime’s boar’s head sigil, and a lantern sigil. There are six carvings in the dais, the size of Martin’s coins. Martin and Moiloc recognize that the lantern usually denotes a tomb, not directly referencing [[Styx the Pathfinder|Styx]]. He puts the coins in, and a crypt opens up. [[Goro Hattori|Goro]], Tarren, [[William Archer|William]] and [[Helen Berestova|Helen]] stay behind.
### Crypt of Sha-Sĭme
Martin summons an Arcane Eye. Martin enters first, and as soon as he gets in, the eye disappears, causing them to assume they’re on a different plane. He immediately re-summons it. The crypt is fully made of sandstone. At the end of the room are two torches, almost completely burned to the bottom. Brewster’s third eye sees writing in celestial, and is unable to copy it well enough for Martin to read, but manages to get it close enough after a few attempts. Martin recognizes it as a quote: “whosoever sheds man’s blood, by man shall his blood be shed, for in the image of [[Eru]] made he man.” Brewster tries to open the door, but it won’t budge. They realize one of them may need to shed blood for the door to open, so Moiloc asks Branwyn to slice her after Brewster seems to eager to help her bleed. The celestial appears and the door fades once Moiloc presses her bloody arm against the door.

They proceed into the next room to see a series of murals, mirrored on both sides of the hall. The mural is of a young girl and a boar. Brewster and Martin have seen this moral, recognizing it as Sha’Sime and her boar Adan. The next mural is a hunter pursuing Adan, then Sha’Sime mourning Adan’s death. Next is Sha’Sime leading an army, then Eru giving Sha’Sime an orb of light.

In the room at the end is a basin of sand with a dagger stuck in it, and seven skeletons, face-down, wearing worn red togas. Each skeleton is clutching a worn piece of parchment. Branwyn detects undead, but feels no presence within six miles except their party. When Martin sends his eye in, words appear over the basin asking what the basis of warfare is. Martin tries to use Mage Hand to take a parchment, causing the skeleton’s hand to crumble. The page says “OJOUZ.” They grab the others: “GRR” “OY” “HGYJK” “CGXLGXK” “UT” “JKIKVZOUT.” The Caesar Cipher gives them the solution: deception.

Their vision goes out for a moment, and they find themselves in the next room: an arena. They see stands of cheering audience members. On a balcony above the stands is a huge orc, wearing large jewels and furs, sitting in a throne. Branwyn and Brewster are placed across from each other in a sections off part of the arena. Around them are pillars with wooden stakes. Also sectioned off are Moiloc and Vulcan, and Martin and [[Puck Nash|Puck]]. Brewster casts Detect Magic, and feels a magic surrounding them: an extremely powerful illusion magic. Martin inspects the doors and finds that they are warded, and will retaliate if hit. He announces to the others that they shouldn’t fight the arena.

Branwyn extends her hand to Brewster to make a deal, but Brewster recognizes that she is planning to harm him. Brewster asks what exactly she’s suggesting they do. Branwyn gets on one knee, and says she only offers him good will and trust, dropping her hand axe and dagger on the ground. She sees Brewster hesitate, and drops her bow and arrows as well. Brewster picks it up, saying he hopes she knows he wouldn’t put her in harms name. He smirks, saying that he wants to see if they will call the fight off if the victor is clear, and summons Flaming Sphere by Branwyn. She gets on both knees and opens herself to it. The orc man throws a spear into her chest before she is hurt by the Sphere, killing her.

Moiloc uses Awaken Mind to talk to Vulcan and Martin, asking if they’re actually fighting. Martin uses Message to say it seems like they have to. Moiloc Eldritch Blasts Vulcan. Vulcan rages, and attacks with his greataxe, but can’t get through her armor. He lets out a menacing roar. Moiloc activates her fire longsword and attacks back, but isn’t able to hit. Vulcan isn’t able to land his next hit, but Moiloc is, slicing into him. Vulcan slices into her, still raging, digging deep into her. They go back and forth, with a very entertaining clash. Moiloc has to use Lay on Hands on herself, but ends up taking Vulcan down, eviscerating him with her fire longsword. She uses non-lethal damage, but the orc puts his thumb down, demanding Moiloc to kill him, and she plunges her sword into his chest.

Martin slices at Puck, hitting him. This angers Puck. Puck casts Sanctuary on himself, but Martin counterspells. Puck casts it again, but misses. Martin hits Puck again, taking him down. Martin notices that Puck doesn’t drop his gem. Moiloc tries to raise Vulcan, but can’t. The crowd starts chanting Rekk-To’s name, and the orc jumps into the arena, smiling at the three.

Rekk-To turns to Moiloc, lowers his horns, and charges. Moiloc is knocked back onto the stakes on the pillar, dying immediately. He then turns to Martin, grabbing him by the throat. Martin bashes his forearm, and he falls to the ground on one knee. Martin steps back, and casts Fireball on him. Brewster casts Melf’s Acid Arrow. Rekk-To stands, and rams toward Martin, but Martin dodges. Rekk-To ends up ramming into the pillar. Martin runs away, but fails to cast Blink. Brewster casts Flaming Sphere, and rams it into him. He tries to wrap his hand around Brewster, but Brewster escapes his grasp. Martin casts Fireball again, careful to hit Rekk-To and not Brewster. Martin Blinks away, and Rekk-To grabs Brewster. Brewster casts Sanctuary. Rekk-To charges him into the spikes, killing him. Martin casts Fire Bolts, killing Rekk-To. The arena disappears around him.

Meanwhile, Branwyn, Vulcan, Puck’s gem, Moiloc, and Brewster appear outside the wagon.

In the center of a room is a sarcophagus looking like Rekk-To. On the wall is more murals: Rekk-To leading an army of slaves, then kneeling before Sha’Sime, then conquering a land made of sandstone with a pyramid, then drinking of a goblet with the celestial word for “snake.” Martin vaguely remember Rekk-To was an avatar of Sha’Sime and a great warrior for the country of [[Shurima the Corpsemaker|Shurima]]. He died after being poisoned at a feast. Martin opens the sarcophagus and sees Rekk-To’s skeleton, and a goblet with the image of a cobra, along with a pile of gold, a diamond, and a Scroll of Revivify.

In the next room is six statues in a straight line: a soldier with a spear and shield, a soldier with a sword, a soldier with a hammer, a knight in plate armor, a soldier with a bow, and a boar. There are three circles on each side of the line to fit the statue. He sees an H in one circle and a boar’s head in another. He makes the bowman bear the swordsman, the one with the hammer beating the knight, and the spearman beat the boar (like the mural). The door opens.

Along the next hallway is more murals. A new, blonde woman is taming a boar. Then, older, she is seen kneeling to Sha’Sime. Finally, she is fighting a shark with human legs. The final mural is the woman lying on a coffin. In the room is a female corpse, missing an arm. Martin recognizes this as Atalanta, the last avatar of Sha’Sime. She was rewarded for taming Calydon, the great boar. She warred against Shurima during the dragon strife. Sha’Sime was defeated by [[Sekollah]]. It was thought Atalanta escaped. Martin removes the skeleton, and sees the same phrase as the door carved into the coffin. Martin slices himself, and drips blood onto the coffin. It opens, to reveal the skeletal remains of a young girl, holding a bronze sword. Martin thanks Sha’Sime, and takes the sword. The word “bushido” is etched in the hilt. Bushido’s voice echoes through Martin’s head, saying he has been found worthy of Sha’Sime’s grace. He can maintain this grace as long as he acts with honor.

Martin appears next to the wagon outside with the others. He changes the sword into a katana. Goro approaches them, and says he saw a caravan of adventurers leaving from Velbrass, heading south, at least 100 men. They could be heading east, but it seems they’re heading south. The party can’t understand why they would be heading to Donnell Keep, and decides to continue to Velbrass, arriving right at nightfall. Helen asks for more pay from Martin.
### Velbrass
The city has three towers at its border, with no vegetation between them. A wizard appears, asking them what their business is, and they gain entry under the guise of looking for trade. There are still people in the city, it seems like there was a celebration that day that is just dying down.

Martin, Helen, Tarren, and Moiloc go to sell the gems and goods at the market. They go to spice merchant, who inspects them. He offers 700g, but Tarren butts in, saying it’s worth more. The merchant offers 1,000g, since they’re not reputable merchants in the area. They agree, if he gives them in introduction to Velbrass. He says they’ve been celebrating because the bandits were defeated. The group leaving town is a group of mercenaries being sent to fight to assist [[David]] in Montrissad. Remaining mercenaries may be slim, but the Cat’s Paw might have more. They continue to a gemstone merchant to sell the gems from the chapel, but Martin keeps the diamond, recognizing it could be used for spells. Martin says he’s cashing in his inheritance, and the merchant seems to recognize him, but is unable to place it. The merchant correctly identifies them as being worth 500g, but since he hasn’t inspected them, he says he’ll give her 400g so they can leave now. Helen tries to guilt Martin into buying her a ring, but he refuses, so Helen buys it herself.

Martin sees a small, red lizardfolk [[Reptile|pickpocket]], who steals a gem from the jewel merchant's pocket. Martin lets him go, but keeps in mind to keep his items safe. Moiloc feels her pendant vibrate. Moiloc follows the lizard, and the group follows, seeing him scurry into a shack. She thinks he’s a teenager. Moiloc opens the door, and it squeaks.
### The Cat's Paw
William, Goro, Brewster, Vulcan, and Brewster enter the Cat’s Paw, which is a chapel that had been converted into a tavern. In the center is an amphitheater with a performing bard. There are gambling tables and private rooms, as well as a bar. A man bumps into Brewster and tells him to watch where he’s going. Brewster turns Invisible and trips him. As the group progresses, people seem to make way due to Vulcan’s size. Branwyn asks the staff if there is a room available, and buys a large room for everyone for two days. It’s the VIP room, so they get a meeting with [[The Bagworm]], the owner. Goro says he’s taking William to the room. Branwyn and Vulcan buys an ale, and Brewster buys a cactus sake. The bartender flirts with Brewster. Brewster says he has his eyes on someone else.

Brewster, Branwyn, and Vulcan go up to the bagworm in the private rooms. The guards let them in after Brewster shows them the VIP card. They enter into a lavish lounge room to see a suave tiefling man. He asks what he can do. Brewster says he just wanted to meet. He motions to them sit. He pours everyone a drink. Brewster says they’re in town to trade, to get some stuff off their hands, but the Bagworm realizes that they aren’t telling the whole story. He explains that the VIP room is for other privileges, implying it’s a brothel. For William’s sake, Brewster asks he calls off any visitors to the room. The Bagworm says that since he did them this favor, they can return it by telling him why they’re actually there. Brewster dodges the question, asking what he really wants. The Bagworm says there’s something he wants from the Lyceum. Brewster says he’ll think about it. Branwyn mockingly curtsies as they leave, and the Bagworm whistles back. Brewster asks why he’s called the Bagworm as they leave, and they say it’s because he is like a worm wrapped in a blanket. Brewster says he thought it was a dick joke, and the Bagworm says if it were a dick joke he would be called the Hog. Upon leaving, Brewster says he may not be a hog, but he’s definitely a pig, which Branwyn seems to enjoy.

Branwyn sits at an empty table near the stage. She hears someone drinking to forget an accident that their brother experienced due to his debts with the Bagworm, and some figures in robes entering and leaving the Bagworm’s den. Brewster turns Puck invisible to look at other peoples’ hands and chest at gambling, winning 200g. He returns to the room to find it more of a suite. There are four bedrooms, with four beds each. William is asleep on the couch, and the bedroom on the right is closed. Brewster enters the room on the left and closes the door.
