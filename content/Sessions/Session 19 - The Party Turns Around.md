### Logline
*The group gets to know the woman they rescued from Olidamuru's dungeon, and try to decide on where to go now that Lothric is destroyed.*
### Synopsis
The outskirts of Lothric are taken over by corpse eaters. Brewster, fearing deadly fights may become common in his future, begins looking for a ring to infuse with a new magic. Branwyn receives a note from Grima explaining what it means to be a green dreamer. The letter is abruptly cut off, telling her Mora is coming for her, and pointing her to a tree in his grove. Grima also provides Branwyn with a flamingo, Velma, who can hide them from being detected by Mora. The woman they rescued from Olidamuru's dungeon, Helen, points them to her former home to look for her husband, but everyone inside is already dead. The group invites Helen to join them. They are unable to find the group Kaldrake arrived at Lothric with, and begin going to Grima's Grove. After a day of travel, the forest begins to die and the air becomes thick, worrying Branwyn, and causing them to turn back and head directly to Whitehall. The group teaches Helen about survival and how to use shields and swords on the way so that she can defend herself.
# Day 34
## Lothric Fields
The golden dome around the Cathedral shatters. There’s a fire around where [[Kaldrake Sootspot|Kaldrake]] left his traveling companions. Corpse eaters are scattered across the fields, attacking farmhands. [[Velma|a flamingo]] lands next to [[Branwyn of Coryphea|Branwyn]], and raises its foot to hand her a letter. Branwyn bows to the flamingo and takes the letter, which is speckled with blood and abruptly cut-off halfway. It’s from [[Grima]], saying the answers to her questions are difficult. He says she’s a green dreamer, through whom wild magic flows, but he doesn’t know why. Green dreamers can see potential futures or pasts while asleep. [[Mora]] has a fascination with green dreamers, hoping to harness their powers. He admits he and Mora used to be friends. Quickly scrawled at the end, the letter tells her to run, saying Mora is coming for her. It says to take the bird, and points to a tree in the grove. The flamingo has a crown of twigs, and the letter notes her name is Velma. Brewster notices that Velma’s crown is the same magic as Branwyn’s ring, and prevents detection from Mora in a larger area.

Branwyn thinks Mora wouldn’t leave without what she came there for, so if Mora wanted something from the Grove, she either would have it, or still be there. They decide to start by looking for the group that Kaldrake left outside Lothric. The [[Helen|woman]] they rescued is hyperventilating. There is blood around her mouth, and they see her tongue is cut off. She is looking at a barn that has its door bashed in. [[Lothar (Brewster) Nash|Brewster]] identifies the ring from the [[Monty MacMasters|Minister of Coin’s]] house, and finds that it’s a ring that allows someone to track the wearer. Brewster asks if anyone has a ring that isn’t cursed so he can infuse it, since he had almost died multiple times since meeting the others and assumes that will only become more common. Branwyn mentions that there may be some corpses with wedding rings that he can take. When she says that, Brewster momentarily looks over to the woman.

They begin to approach the barn, silently. [[Moiloc Kodlaksdottër|Moiloc]] loudly crashes into the door, and they hear a loud crunch. She immediately does it again, and the door loudly crashes to the ground. Kaldrake doesn’t see anything coming after them. Moiloc casts Divine Sense and senses three undead cows, and one corpse tender. Branwyn sees the corpse tender taking something out of itself. They back out, and Moiloc tells the woman that the corpse eaters have taken over her farm, asking if she wants to come with or not. The woman points to her house, nearby the barn, and points to her wedding ring. Moiloc doesn’t sense anything in the first floor of the barn.

In the house, there is a corpse slumped over on the table, with some chunks taken out of his midsection. Brewster notices there’s an impression of a ring on his finger, but no ring. Kaldrake gets squeamish and throws up on the floor. Branwyn opens the door on the first floor and finds a storeroom. Branwyn tells the woman to stay in the storeroom. Moiloc opens the other door, finding a bedroom, with a teenage human in pieces around the room. Kaldrake is too squeamish to continue, and the other three go upstairs. A streak of blood goes into one of the rooms on this floor.

Branwyn finds a locked door. Brewster picks the lock, and finds a chest inside, with a beautiful white dress inside, and a ruby necklace. He takes them both. Moiloc opens the next door and finds an unrecognizable corpse. Same with the next door, which has two beds in it, and it covered in blood, but has no people in it. The last bedroom is empty. Branwyn sees a chest and jewelry box, and takes the jewelry box. Inside the chest is a male farmhand’s outfit. Brewster gives them the wedding dress and necklace.

They continue to the door with a blood streak entering it. Half of a corpse is on the bed, with a [[Tobias Grand|figure]] dressed in leathers shoveling it into its mouth. They sneak out of the room, and fill Kaldrake in, and he says he killed that guy in a prior timeline. Branwyn tells the woman there isn’t anything for her here anymore, and she tears up, but prepares herself to follow them.

They decide to meet up with where Kaldrake left the others. On their way through the fields, they are caught by three corpse eaters.

> [!danger] Fight
> Kaldrake immediately hits the corpse eater a few times, and it sews itself back up. Moiloc casts Bane on them. The corpse eater bites at Kaldrake. One of the others attempts to bit Branwyn and misses, but the last one carves into Moiloc with his nails. Brewster uses Melf’s Acid Arrow on the corpse eater attacking Branwyn, which heavily melts it. Branwyn makes another attack on it, but misses it twice. Kaldrake decapitates his corpse eater, and moves to help Moiloc with her corpse eater, cutting off its arm. Moiloc activates her fire longsword. Branwyn’s corpse eater swings at her, but misses, and finishes melting, dying. Brewster uses Poison Spray but the last one is immune. Branwyn shoots into its throat, and Kaldrake finishes it off by decapitating it.

They continue through the field and notice about a dozen corpse eaters following them. Brewster suggests they burn down the field, and Moiloc uses her fire longsword to ignite it. Branwyn navigates them through the forest, but since it’s been a long day, it takes them a while to get out of the forest, and they make it to the road about a mile away from where they’re heading. They see people running through the streets, and Brewster puts on the mask to appear human. They make it to the location of the camp Kaldrake left the others at, but the forest is ablaze, and they don’t see any sign of where the others went. They walk some way toward Grima’s Grove, and camp for the night. Brewster asks the woman her name, and she writes Helen.

They catch up for the night. Kaldrake and Brewster bicker about the mask. Kaldrake tells them that [[Gwyneth Sootspot|Gwyneth]] made a wish with a djinn for Kaldrake to come back, and he destroyed Brasov. Kaldrake tells them about the reality switching. Brewster flips over the “Helen” paper and takes vigorous notes on how Kaldrake says this works. He tells them about [[Jones]] taking him and the others to Fort Bosco. Branwyn asks why he would help and elf, and he says Gwyneth is the Minister of Magic, but in this reality she has been gone for two weeks. Brewster asks about the mask, and he says his great-grandfather is actually [[Gulvayas Hakon]] and he saw the mask in a vision. He also tells them about his [[Balthazar Sootspot|father]] and the curse to die at 100. Brewster tells him about the mask, and about killing the bakers. He also asks if push came to shove and Moiloc did something evil on Shurima’s behalf if they’d do something about that. They don’t give an outright answer. Brewster casts Identify on Kaldrake, but finds no actual spells affecting him. He doesn’t know about Kaldrake’s reality-altering abilities, but thinks answers might lie in the Aethereum. Brewster asks if anyone has a ring, showing his ~~Inspiration~~ Desperation. Moiloc and Branwyn take the first shift, and Kaldrake and Brewster take second.
# Day 35
## Forest
The group decides to go back the camp where Kaldrake left the others, and they find the charred ruins of a camp field, but no bodies, tents, horses, or tracks. The group convinces Brewster to go to Grima’s Grove real quick. They ask Helen if she wants to come, and she agrees. Kaldrake and Moiloc agree to teach her how to use a long sword. Moiloc gives her chainmail, and her wooden shield, but she struggles using the chainmail. Branwyn reads her book on herbalism. Kaldrake reads his book on the Sootspot family. Brewster Identifies the crystal dagger Moiloc is using and can’t find any spells on in, but believes it may have religious significance. They set up camp, and it’s oddly and suspiciously silent.
# Day 36
## Forest
Brewster casts Detect Magic and sees a sickly, unrecognizable aura from Kaldrake, but nothing magically off about the forest and its silence. Branwyn notices it doesn’t smell like they’re in a forest, but a cellar, reminding her of Gravewatch. Kaldrake also feels uneasy. Brewster notices similarities to when he went to Branwyn’s common, but the stale air is new. Brewster knows very little about the fall of Gravewatch, but unnatural stillness typically precedes massive magic activity. Kaldrake asks if this is what happens when Mora gets close, and Branwyn says it isn’t. Brewster asks if this could be Mora killing the forest after taking down Grima’s Grove.

Branwyn tells them about what happened when she left Coriphea as it turned into Gravewatch, saying that everything died. She mentions her conversation with Grima about protecting the forests, saying he only told her the [[Queen Cypha|Queen]] must die. Branwyn also mentions that Mora may be amassing power, since Brewster said this precedes magic events. She also questions if something like this may have happened when Amaris fell. Branwyn gets the feeling that they need to leave the forest as soon as possible, feeling terrified to go further. The group packs up, and begins heading to Whitehall.

Helen learns how to identify a few edible foods from Branwyn, and learns more about using a sword from Kaldrake. Brewster comes to Helen as someone who has been taken like she has, and asks if they can do anything to help, and she proudly holds up her great sword, seeming as good as can be expected.

That afternoon, they see a wrecked wagon, sunk into the mud. It seems to have swerved off the road and broken into splinters. Branwyn pops open a crate, and it seems to be full of food. They also find barrels that smell of wine, but leave them behind. They set up camp, and see the beginnings of storm clouds.
# Day 37
## Forest
While keeping watch, Branwyn sees some sort of twisting in the clouds, and points it out to Kaldrake, who recognizes them as storm elementals. They likely won’t attack, but it would be good to get underneath something to avoid their eyes. Branwyn sets something up to block their vision, and they stay under cover for the night. Kaldrake gets excited for a sleepover, and Branwyn says she’s sorry about his dad. He says their relationship was complicated, and they weren’t that close, but they haven’t had a chance to fully internalize it with everything else going on. Branwyn and Kaldrake have a heart-to-heart, and Branwyn says she’s proud of him, and he’s had some good wins. When the storm elementals leave in the morning, they set out again.
