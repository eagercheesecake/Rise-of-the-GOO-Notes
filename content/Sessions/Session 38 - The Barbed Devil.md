### Logline
*Moiloc faces her second trial. The others must decide between tracking Mora or Daphne.*
### Synopsis
Brewster and Lucretia spot Mora with various children, including Charlotte and Goro's daughters. Shurima confesses to Moiloc that he killed Eru and tried to take her power, but couldn't contain it, and was split into Olidamuru and Shurima. Moiloc has another trial in the Cathedral, calling a team to help her. When he appears, Tobias had killed his father, Vernan Grande. Meanwhile, the others proceed to follow Mora and search for Daphne, eventually having to decide which to prioritize. They descend further to find Daphne, and Martin sends a Scrying Eye after Mora. The Eye finds that all the children except Charlotte are enthralled, but soon disappears as the group enter the Underdark. They proceed to a boat in the Lost City of Shurima, after briefly being attacked by a Barbed Devil.
# Day 63
### Whitehall Mines
[[Lothar (Brewster) Nash|Brewster]] and [[Lucretia]] are checking the runes on the elevator to decode the password, as a feminine voice says GUPO and the elevator shifts up. The elevator shifts down, past the guard’s room, and Lucretia locks eyes with [[Mora]], holding a small Dragonborn child - Haru’s daughter, [[Azura]]. Three toddlers grasp at her shirt, [[Charlotte]], looking a little older, [[Callia|a gnome]], and a human.

Meanwhile, a figure materializes in front of [[William Archer|William’s]] corpse. [[Martin Sootspot|Martin]] recognizes this as [[Styx the Pathfinder|Styx]]. He pushes his hand into William’s chest, and pulls out a green orb. As he crushes it, it becomes green vapor and dissipates.

Brewster and Lucretia run in to tell everyone the news. Brewster finds it strange that Mora wasn’t near [[Gwyneth Sootspot|Gwyneth]]. They decide to follow Mora. Martin, [[Branwyn of Coryphea|Branwyn]], [[Vulcan]], and Brewster go down first, with Lucretia following. As they past the fourth floor, Branwyn smells blood.

There are inquisitors scattered across the room, all hurt or dead. Brewster notices a circular rock on the wall with a symbol of an ear, motioning for everyone to be quiet. Styx follows them, and the second group comes down as well. In a whisper, they begin asking questions, but don’t learn much. Branwyn sees a tripwire in front of a decline that has been severed. To the right is a gnome repeatedly stabbing another gnome.

The arrive at the sixth floor, and Vulcan stops them, saying there are inquisitors, and something is wrong with them. Their eyes are glowing purple. Martin recognizes this as the sign of a Dominate Person spell. They very slowly and carefully pass by the guards, and begin to smell decay. They realize that these guards are wasting away and have likely been here for a long time. In the room at the end of the hall, they hear squelching and flies, and see three corpses, with corpse tenders harvesting their organs. Brewster feels the sending stone vibrate, and picks it up to hear a guard say that monsters have taken over the city.

The group see two passages. One has fresh moss on it, likely the direction that Mora went, and another declines closer to the Underdark, likely toward [[Daphne]]. They decide to send an Arcane Eye to Mora and proceed down the path toward Daphne. It’s steep, and they try to use rope to connect themselves. Enough of them slip that everyone, tied together, begins to swiftly slide down the decline. Brewster levitates right before he hits the ground. They all manage to land safely, except for Lucretia and Martin. They land on the ninth floor.

The stench of sulfur overpowers the group. A small crevice in the wall is leaking quicksilver. Brewster recognizes it as a fresh crack, likely caused by an explosion. They proceed into the next room and see a man in Inquisitor armor, with a head wound, on the floor. He dies, and Brewster roots through a body. He finds another sending stone, and instructions to go after the cartographer. It’s stamped with the Sootspot seal. They cast Speak with Dead.

The inquisitor died due to a black powder explosion, caused by a slave uprising. Daphne is on the tenth floor or lower. Brewster uses Gaseous Form to slip through the cave-in, finding a hole that the slaves seem to have escaped through. Martin uses Shatter to burst through the crack that quicksilver was dripping through, getting them access to the hole. Brewster mentions that Daphne will likely attack on sight, and goes first, slowly descending in his gaseous form.

Meanwhile, the Arcane Eye catches sight of Mora and the children. The children, minus Charlotte, seem stiff and enthralled.

Brewster lands on the next level, and finds a gnome who was recently whipped to death. [[Puck Nash|Puck]] nods that it is safe for the others to descend. They follow a blood trail, avoiding some traps along the way. The passage begins to slope down and coil around itself like a spiral staircase. After descending for some time, the walls get smoother, becoming old brick. Eventually, the slope becomes stairs. Martin senses that he loses connection to his Arcane Eye, and realizes that they had entered the Underdark.
### The Lost City of Shurima
They enter a room with a sacrificial engraving, and see a blood-drained dwarf on the ground. Brewster uses his last bit of Gaseous Form to peek through the door, seeing [[Shurima the Corpsemaker|Shurima]] snake symbology. They decide to each sacrifice some blood, but Tarren stops them, offering to offer the blood himself, and having them heal him and let him rest. Branwyn bandages Tarren, and Vulkan carries him. They proceed onto a balcony overseeing the lost city of Shurima. Martin recognizes the purple crystal from [[Kaldrake Sootspot|Kaldrake’s]] vision. They see a boat tied to the railing, and another loose chain. Martin sees a boat in the distance with two gnomes, three dwarves, one half-elf, and one shorter humanoid.

A puff of smoke causes a demon to appear, the Barbed Devil. Martin notices his ring from Moiloc is gone. The devil says he has no more use for them. Brewster blinds him with his gem of brightness, and Vulkan throws him into the quicksilver ocean.

They proceed into the boat, and see a cloud of purple mist descending toward the cathedral. They see three humanoid creatures swoop down toward the cathedral.
### The Cathedral of the Gods
Meanwhile, [[Moiloc Kodlaksdottër|Moiloc]] proceeds through the cathedral, and sees a statue of [[Eru]]. However, in this statue, Eru has the face of a middle-aged elf. Around her neck is a noose of chains, connecting to the purple crystal, bigger than the cathedral itself. Shurima walks up to the statue, kneels, and begins to pray. Moiloc silently watches him pray for fifteen minutes, before the chain disconnects from Eru, hanging loose.

Shurima announces that they have arrived at his birthplace, saying he was a slave of the country of Shurima. His final act serving Eru was to carve this statue. He was still punished and beaten despite this, and has now returned for revenge, to claim the power he has deserved for thousands of years. He sought godhood during the Great Dragon Strife, and tried to take Eru’s power, but his mortal body couldn’t handle it. She broke him, carving him into two. His other half, [[Olidamuru]], took every evil impulse Shurima had. He believes if he unites these halves, they can control Eru’s power, and take over. He wants to merge the planes, the first thing that Moiloc objects to. He promises that this will help people, which tames Moiloc’s worries. He then says her final opponents are arriving - Moiloc saying she’s all in, and that he should have led with that.

Shurima says he needs to begin some rituals, so he cannot help Moiloc during her trials, but she can summon four of her allies. She summons [[Goro Hattori|Goro]], [[Nissa]], [[Holliday]], and [[Ishtar]]. They panic, confused, asking how they got there, saying they were just fighting corpse eaters. Moiloc says this will save everyone, and they agree to help. Three large devils and [[Mephistopheles the Bard]] appear before her. Moiloc warns him to get away from the devils, but Mephistopheles says he is under contract and she is in the way. Moiloc immediately says he has to die, and he seems shocked about how blunt she was with it.
