### Logline
*The groups begin their respective descents.*
### Synopsis
Brewster, Martin, and Branwyn proceed to the mines to find Daphne. They enter through an abandoned well, landing in a guard sleeping quarters. They are able to fight off the inquisitors and hook horror, learning Daphne, going by the moniker of "the cartographer," is in one of the lowest layers of the mines. Moiloc's group tries to enter the catacombs in the cathedral, fighting the death knight blocking the way, but the group gets split at a fork in the path.
# Day 63
### The Watering Shack
[[Lothar (Brewster) Nash|Brewster]] sees through a nearby a blaze of green light and goes to alert the others. He looks out the window and doesn’t see anything. He guesses the energy would come from a magical trap or circle of protection being triggered. He sends [[Puck Nash|Puck]] to check it out, and he sees an opulently dressed human running quickly through the streets with a paper, and a white raven flying out of the cathedral. They decide to just head out and go to the mines.

As they pass through the city, they see the man with the scroll. Brewster tries to trip him but falls over. [[Martin Sootspot|Martin]] casts Detect Thoughts and finds that he was gathering entertainment for [[Bloody Baldwin Haas|Bloody Baldwin's]] birthday.
### Capella Sinistra
[[Moiloc Kodlaksdottër|Moiloc's]] comrades wake up and she tells them that [[Shurima the Corpsemaker|Shurima]] said they have to get a book from the catacombs. [[Reptile]] says Martin might be in the catacombs as well, having seen them leave the main hall together. However, when they return to the great hall, the [[Death Knight]] is blocking the door. Reptile suggests they distract him, but [[Nissa]] rushes past and declares they are going to fight him.

> [!danger] Fight
> [[Ishtar]] uses Twinblade Tornado to slash at the death knight. Nissa runs to the death knight’s side, and [[Holliday]] flanks him. The death knight stabs his sword into the ground, causing all three to be blown back, downing Holliday. Moiloc takes out her fire longsword, but misses twice. Mephistopheles strums his instrument and stays far back. [[Goro Hattori|Goro]] also approaches. Reptile hides, and [[Mephistopheles the Bard|Mephistopheles]] sees him, deciding he will as well.
> 
> Ishtar heals everyone. Nissa attacks twice, hitting the death knight hard the first time, but he blocks the second. Holliday fully misses. The death knight’s sword glows as he swings at Nissa, inflicting intense pain on her. Moiloc backs up, using Eldritch Blast on the death knight. Goro tries to attack, but misses. Mephistopheles tries to hide himself by flattening himself against a pillar. Reptile scurries to the door, seeing a white raven fly to the governor’s palace, holding the gun.
> 
> Nissa pumps herself up a bit. Holliday slices at the death knight. The death knight causes a large explosion, downing Ishtar, Mephistopheles, and Holliday, but Ishtar keeps Holliday up before falling herself. Moiloc heals Ishtar with her healing potion. Goro blesses everyone’s weapons. Reptile leaves the hall to look for another entrance.
> 
> Ishtar heals everyone once again. Nissa slashes at the death knight again, but he uses Hellish Rebuke to hit back. The death knight puts his sword into the ground again, downing Nissa and Holliday, and hurting Goro. Moiloc once again casts Eldritch Blast, scraping against him. Mephistopheles gives Bardic Inspiration to Ishtar. Goro is downed in retribution, and cannot attack.
> 
> Ishtar once again heals everyone, and Nissa, standing up, cuts through and the death knight crumbles.

Reptile had said that he saw the white raven holding the gun during the fight and left, but with the death knight down, the others try to rush into the door before the death knight reforms. They run down the passageway, and soon after, hear the death knight following. They reach a fork in the road, but feel a breeze from one side, and go down the other to find the catacombs. Holliday, Mephistopheles, Goro, and Nissa, arrive later, and go the other way.
### Whitehall Mines
The others arrive at the mining entrance, and [[Tobias Grande|Tobias]] meets them. They send Tobias to save the real Tobias, as [[Branwyn of Coryphea|Branwyn]] refuses to work with him. A procession of slaves are being walked into it. Brewster takes them to a nearby well that connects to a lower level of the mines. They proceed to the slave quarters, and begin to lower a rope into the well. Branwyn goes first, and Martin goes after. Martin loses his grip, and falls. He casts Dimension Door on the floor under the rope and appears at the bottom, right in between three guards. Brewster, seeing her fall, drops a potion of healing down, and it splatters behind him. Martin immediately casts Invisibility.

Martin hides under a bed. The others make their way down the rope as the Inquisitors unchain a hook horror. [[William Archer|William]] stays in the well, removing the rope.

> [!danger] Fight
> Martin casts Fireball and hits most of the inquisitors, and the hook horror. Branwyn shoots the final inquisitor, the third one. That inquisitor then slashes at Martin. The first one flanks Martin and hits as well. The second, fifth and sixth shoot at Branwyn, one hitting. The fourth misses Martin. Brewster casts Heat Metal on the barbed collar on the Hook Horror. Puck shoots at the third one, killing him. [[Tarren Hammerfist|Tarren]] runs out and uses his Fist of Thunder, knocking the second, fourth, and sixth prone. Martin slices into the first inquisitor with Bushido, and [[Lucretia]] finishes him off. [[Vulcan]] runs to the fourth inquisitor, raging, and bites into him.
> 
> Branwyn shoots the fifth one twice, hitting him once. Brewster heats the metal further on the hook horror, then casts Poison Spray on the second inquisitor. Puck shoots at him as well. The second and sixth inquisitors beat Tarren, bringing him into a berserk rage. The hook horror and the fourth inquisitor slice at Vulcan but miss. The fifth one hits Martin. Tarren punches the second one, killing him. Martin sheathes his sword, and kills the fifth Inquisitor while unsheathing it. Lucretia shoots at the fourth inquisitor with a Magic Missile. Vulcan slashes at the hook horror.
> 
> Branwyn shoots the fourth inquisitor, killing him. The hook horror can’t touch Vulcan. The sixth one stabs a sword into Tarren, but doesn’t down him. The hook horror melts at the neck.

Brewster takes out the blinding stone, holding it up to the last Inquisitor as he cowers in fear at the hook horror melting and Tarren casually being impaled by a sword. He questions the inquisitor of [[Daphne|Daphne's]] location, although he seems confused as to who that is. After a description, the inquisitor says she is in the lower levels of the mine, going by the cartographer. Brewster blinds him anyway, punching him to the ground. Martin asks Brewster if he is going to kill the inquisitor, but Brewster offers the kill to Martin. They agree to do it hand-in-hand, impaling the inquisitor with Bushido. Brewster comments that he forgives Martin.
