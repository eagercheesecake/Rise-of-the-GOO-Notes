### Logline
*After the party's death, another group of adventures go after the hydra of Col Man'Dar.*
### Synopsis
Martin, following a divination that his brother is going to die, goes to Col Man'Dar with Arklow Laban, Contigo, Morrigan, and Ororo. They find Kaldrake's corpse, and determine he was a victim used to summon another hydra. They find Goro in the sawmill, and he leaves the town after pointing them to the location of the hydra's egg. They find the egg, but don't see the hydra. Morrigan uses Dominate Person on Martin, having him guide them through the forest to the nearest town, looking for Branwyn. While traveling, Ororo discovers the spell, and breaks it, freeing Martin. Martin confronts Morrigan, who apologizes. Arklow admits to Contigo that he had been poisoning the others' rations. Contigo and Martin sleep in shifts. Morrigan uses Dominate Person on Contigo during his shift, and they leave for the next town. Ororo and Martin wake up and realize what had happened, and follow suit, leaving Arklow behind. They arrive in Edoval, where Morrigan and Contigo destroy the heart of the first hydra, and Ororo takes the heart of the second. Ororo reveals herself as Ouroborus Tikktal, and Morrigan reveals herself as Mora. Brewster, Branwyn, and Moiloc, having been taken as the hydra's thrall, each experience something in death. Brewster finds himself at the gates to Eru's city. Branwyn finds an oil lamp and a haze enveloping Maokai. Moiloc meets a demon prince in hell.
# Day 46
### Col Man'Dar
After seeing a divination that [[Kaldrake Sootspot|Kaldrake]] was going to die, [[Characters/Players/Martin Sootspot|Martin Sootspot]], accompanied by [[Arklow Laban]], [[Contigo Muñoz|Contigo]], [[Mora|Morrigan]], and [[Ouroborus Tikktal|Ororo]], arrive in Col Man’Dar. They arrive to see uniformly spaced craters surrounded by charred corpses, and dilapidated farmland. The scent of magic is in the air, but there doesn’t seem to be any at play at the moment of their arrival. Morrigan and Ororo recognize this as the signed of a recent hydra attack, but don’t share that with anyone. As Martin says he doesn’t want to proceed alone, Arklow says he’s happy to come with, but that he hadn’t been paid yet that day. He says the Sootspot family paid his deposit but that he has a daily fee of 15g. Martin says he can take the gold he wants off corpses and he’ll pay the rest at the end of the day.

Five larger holes have dehydrated, almost mummified, corpses inside. Martin notes that this likely means something was summoned here. He can’t really see the features to identify who they are, but one is clad in armor and a Sootspot signet ring. Martin takes the ring and Kaldrake’s enchanted gauntlets, saddened about the death of his brother. Arklow and Morrigan comfort him, suggesting they look at what did this and seek revenge, an idea which the rest of the group. Ororo tells them that this is likely a hydra reproducing, which requires the entire area to be destroyed, and they are likely moving on to the next city.

They proceed to a nearby, worn-down farmhouse. The front door is smashed to pieces. Martin says he doesn’t want to go first, so Contigo goes in first, and finds the room covered in dried blood and viscera. Martin says the house wasn’t in his vision of Kaldrake dying. Martin finds a pelvis with a coin pouch on it, and loots it for gold. Martin turns around, and Arklow has his hand outstretched, asking for his daily cut.

Contigo opens one of the doors in the house, leading to a kitchen, with untouched, clean cookware and counters. There’s moldy food, but no viscera, mice, or rats. Ororo smells a higher concentration of blood upstairs. Morrigan exits the house, looking for the source of the sawdust smell from Martin’s vision, and sees a lumber mill is nearby with her magic vision. The next door inside is locked. Contigo bounces off against the door, Arklow weakly rams into it, and Contigo finally breaks it down, while Martin watches them in this competition of strength. They see a child’s bedroom, with a cracked window, unmade bed, and ransacked wardrobe and chest. The final room is a storage closet, and a locked trunk. Contigo points this out to Martin, who asks if Contigo wants it open. Contigo points out that Arklow probably won’t allow him to keep anything in it, so it’s more a matter of if the others in the party want it opened. Martin opens it using Knock. Inside is 150 gold, and Martin gives the remaining 10 gold from the pelvic gold pouch to Contigo. He also finds a magical, blue cloak: a cloak of Spell Restoration.

Morrigan re-enters the house as Ororo continues upstairs, and finds one door leading to a master bedroom. It’s a lavish bedroom, with a bed covered in a wet, white substance - the blood of a hydra. She guesses that the heart of the hydra was somewhere in the room, but has been moved, and since hydras can’t move their own hearts, the hydra’s thralls likely took it away. Morrigan and Ororo fill them in on what they saw. Arklow questions how Morrigan knows there’s a sawmill nearby, asking if she’s been here before, and she says she did her research. Martin sees white streak marks going outside the door, and follows the hydra blood trail outside, seeing drag marks going toward a bar with wide-open doors. Martin tells Contigo to lead the way to the doors. Contigo expresses that Arklow may be a better choice to lead, but since he’s on Arklow and Martin’s payrolls, he is shot down.

The barn has wheel marks leading out of it. Morrigan says they’re likely long gone by now. They proceed to the sawmill, passing a fish market and the empty village. The sawmill looks quiet but undamaged, just abandoned. Ororo sees a path going off into the woods, and points it out. The door to the sawmill is locked, and Arklow runs into it, collapsing, and saying it must be barred. Contigo snickers and quickly tries to hide it. Ororo uses Magic Missile against the door, blasting it to splinters as an axe flies through the air, Ororo stepping out of its path just in time. A grizzled Dragonborn is at the door, ready to throw his axe, who Contigo recognizes as [[Goro Hattori|Goro]]. Ororo apologizes to Goro, and introduces themselves as travelers trying to figure out what happened. Contigo re-introduces himself to Goro, asking if he’s seen [[Tarren Hammerfist|Tarren]] recently, which he hasn’t. Goro expresses his anger with [[Gwyneth Sootspot|Gwyneth]] to Martin.

Goro decides to get out of town, and the others follow the path by the sawmill to a circlular outcropping with the outlines of some bodies, but no actual bodies. Morrigan recognizes hair the looks like Branwyn’s, and there are some drag marks nearby leading back to the sawmill. [[Velma|Velma’s]] body is there, torn apart. Morrigan casts Dominate Person on Ororo, but it doesn’t seem to do anything. Morrigan pockets the hairs. Morrigan casts Charm Person on Martin, but fails, and Martin notices. Martin asks the spellcasters to please not do that, and tells Contigo to lead the way to the end of the path, where they all recognize the remnants of a hydra egg.

Martin suggests they continue to the next town to find the hydras. Morrigan casts Dominate Person on Martin, and finally succeeds. She then uses Telepathy to tell him he’s changed his mind, and wants to search the forest, and will bring [[Branwyn of Coryphea|the ranger]] if he finds her. Martin suggests that they cut through the forest. They get lost in the woods. 
### Forest
Ororo figures out that Martin is under the effect of magic, and casts Dispel Magic. Martin confronts Morrigan, saying she could have just said she wanted to find the ranger. Contigo asks if she was looking for Branwyn. Morrigan thinks of a next move, unsure if she could take Ororo in battle. She apologizes, and says a girlhood friend of hers named Branwyn was nearby, and decided to follow them to look for her. Martin clocks this as a lie. Martin says he’s fine to help her look, but he wants to do it on the path, not in the woods. First, the camp for the night. Contigo suggests to Arklow that they sleep in shifts, but he says there’s no need, since they’re among friends. Arklow also tells Contigo that he’s poisoned everyone’s food with a slow-acting poison. Contigo tells Arklow he’s going to keep watch from outside, and Martin asks Contigo if he wants to stay with them. Contigo accepts, and they agree to keep watch. Martin takes the first watch and attunes to his cloak.

On Contigo’s shift, Morrigan casts Dominate Person on Contigo, and tells him to come with and help her find the girl. The pair leaves, and continues north.
# Day 47
Ororo wakes up and immediately realizes what happens. She pulls Martin aside and immediately tells him to get on horseback to look for them. They push the horses to its limit as they dash away. They leave Arklow behind, heading north. Ororo leaves a note telling Arklow to watch the camp. Arklow crumples up the note, and heads south.

Contigo tries to strike a conversation with Morrigan, but she tells him to shut up. She tries to use Branwyn’s hairs to divine where she is, but fails.

At the end of the day, Martin decides they need to camp, which upsets Ororo, but they do so. Martin finds a gemstone on the ground.

Near the end of the day, Morrigan and Contigo see a trampled human corpse on the side of the road, with a viscous white fluid on it. They ride through the night.
### Ringed City
Meanwhile, [[Lothar (Brewster) Nash|Brewster]] wakes up refreshed, staring at a sky full of unfamiliar constellations, stars lighting up the sky like daylight. He feels the sands below him and is reminded of his days with Tarren outside of Whitehall, but is wearing his regular garb. The last thing he remembers is running from a hydra and blacking out. He stands up and looks around, to see a vast, flat plain. Looking at his hands, he notices he’s a little transparent, with a bluish glow. He realizes he might be dead. Brewster angrily curses his friends. He finds his crossbow in the sands and his dagger on his waist. He notices he is still missing a finger, which sucks. He picks a direction and walks. He walks for as long as he can, and collapses. When he gets up, he’s standing in front of a pedestal, with carvings that seem familiar, with events from his life depicted in hieroglyphics.

He sees slavery, his first meeting with [[Daphne]], his escape, his time with Tarren and the Rebellion, and his death. Then he sees an image of him stabbing himself in his good hand with a dagger. He sighs, hoping it isn’t a chosen one situation, but does so. The pain is immediate, and blood spills onto the marble. The stars spin overhead, sand whips around like a tornado. The wound on his hand heals as colors swirl around him, and he finds himself in the middle of a field of wheat. In the distance is a castle floating impossibly in the air, made of black walls, larger than most cities. This is the ringed city, where [[Eru]] is from, in Eru’s plane.

Brewster follows a bridge of light and walks up the bridge to the large wooden doors of the castle, impossibly large. He knocks on the door, which slowly swings open. He steps into an immaculate city of unbroken marble and sandstone.
### Maokai's Prison
Branwyn is on a path in the woods, starving. She isn’t carrying anything on her. She looks for edible food, but can’t find anything except some mushrooms that seem edible. As she’s picking them, she hears a raven, and realizes she is in the same woods from her childhood. She starts running to hide, and hears Mora’s laugh on the wind. She runs for as long as she can, but doesn’t seem to be progressing through the woods.

Branwyn takes a deep breath and clears her head. She looks around, noticing the trees look warped and strange. She pushes into the tree, and it rots and crumbles away. Steeling herself, she continues walking, for a long while. The trees seem to get bigger, and more twisted. The forest finally breaks, and she sees herself in a clearing of dirt, devoid of life, except for a giant tree in the center, some giant branches seemingly falling off. At the base of the tree is a purple mist. She senses the tree is something more, obviously magical in nature, with the fog at the base choking the tree. Something is wrong.

Branwyn goes to investigate, slowly approaching the mist. The mist rises, as if it is alive and reacting to her presence. She takes a mushroom and throws it into the mist, watching as a mouth forms out of the mist and consumes it. In the mist is an oil lamp. She goes back and grabs a branch from the forest. It’s very brittle, but she tries to use it to pull the lamp from the fog. However, the branch crumbles, just knocking over the lamp. She dips a toe into the fog, and her boot disintegrates, but she remains unharmed.

She fully steps in, and all of her clothes evaporate, with the metal of buttons and belts clattering to the ground. She passes the lamp, proceeding to touch the tree. A doorway opens in the tree trunk, taking her to the heart of the tree. Inside the tree is a fleshy red mass, much like a heart, beating very quickly. She hears [[Maokai|a voice]] in her heart begging her not to come closer, saying she, Mora, has already done enough to him.
### Gahenna
[[Moiloc Kodlaksdottër|Moiloc]] wakes up with soot in her throat, laying on hot rocks. She sees a vast plane of fire and rocks, with imps flying around, and the sound of a battle around her. She scales the rocks in front of her. She looks over the apex of the cliffs and sees two armies of devils, one army with red skin and one with blue skin, battling ahead of her. There are two circular pedestals floating above the battlefield, each with four demons surrounding it, looking over the battle, enjoying it. Further off is a few columns.

Moiloc works her way around the battlefield, but as soon as she steps close, a tall [[Mephistopheles the Cold|devil prince]] appears in front of her, twelve-feet tall with twisted horns. He asks what she is doing here, and she also expresses confusion, and introduces herself. She asks where she is, and he confirms it is the infernal plane. She asks why she’s there, and he asks why she wants to be there. He reaches into her head, and finds that she’s interested in the columns, asking if she wants a closer look at his home. She says yes, and he snaps, teleporting her to the tower with him.
# Day 48
### Edoval
Morrigan and Contigo arrive in the city to the north, Edoval, early in the morning. Contigo asks what their goal is, wondering if Branwyn is in the town or the forest, and guessing the hydras had likely already arrived. Morrigan suggests they check the woods first, then the town. Contigo asks if she could take the hydras if they meet them. She says yes, and Contigo says he’s glad they’re on the same size. Mora uses Power Word: Pain on him, and he writhes on the ground. When he gets back up, Mora tells him to be careful how he talks to her, and they continue searching for Branwyn.

They don’t find her in the woods or the town, but they see a farm they could ask around at, based on the hydra’s last activity. They see people sewing in the ground, including Brewster, who nobody recognizes. They are sewing similarly to Col Man’Dar. They proceed into the farmhouse. Morrigan knocks, but nobody answers. Even the people on the field don’t seem to notice them approach. Morrigan tells Contigo to go first. It isn’t locked, but he breaks in. The floorboards inside are shattered, and there’s a deep hole in the center of the hole. Contigo tosses a torch down the hole, and it falls about 25 feet, revealing a tunnel at the bottom, and the torch rolls down the tunnel. Contigo prepares a rope and slides his way down. Mora just jumps down. Contigo leans down and proceeds into the small tunnel, and Mora follows. It’s a very long tunnel, and eventually opens up into a large cavern.

Against the wall is eight figures circling a white, fleshy heart. It can be heard beating across the pattern. The figures are kneading the heart. Contigo recognizes one of the figures as Moiloc. Contigo motions to the heart, and motions for Morrigan to do what she needs to do, and she uses Scorching Ray, causing the heart to pop, exploding white viscera on the surrounding figures. The figures are knocked unconscious as the hydra lets out a loud shriek.

Contigo mentions that Moiloc was in Branwyn’s party. Since Moiloc was taken as the hydra’s thrall, he says Branwyn may be nearby with the newly born hydras. Morrigan tells Contigo to stay there and guard Moiloc, and leaves. Morrigan opens her third and fourth eyes, and sees Branwyn is nearby.

At this point, Ororo and Martin enter the town. They hear a shrill screech and see people collapse onto the ground. When they come to, the people who see Martin and Ororo quickly lock their doors and shutter their windows. Martin checks on one of the girls who collapsed, and feels a pulse. She shakes her, but she doesn’t wake up.

Morrigan removes her disguise, transforming into a terrifying beast, with six lanky arms, eight eyes, and bat wings. Ororo hears faint music, and guesses that there’s one juvenile hydra nearby. She sees Mora, but guesses the music, and thus the heart, is in the church. Ororo proceeds to the church, and Martin goes invisible and follows her. Inside the church is another heart, with a smaller hydra perched above it. Ororo runs for the heart, grabbing it.

Martin wakes up after being knocked out. The church is destroyed. Oroborus Tikktal is flying above him, the hydra in its mouth. It releases the hydra, which pops against the ground, covering the vicinity with white viscera. The dragon flies off. Martin turns to where Mora was, watching as she takes the elf on the ground, Branwyn, and also flies off, heading north.

The Dominate Person spell on Contigo breaks. He leaves the hole, takes his horse, and decides to go north.

Martin decides he’s still hungry, and proceeds to the local inn. He sees Goro and [[Helen]] approaching.
