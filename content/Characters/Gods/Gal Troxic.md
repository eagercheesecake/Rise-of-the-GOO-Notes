## [[Session 23 - Sootspot Intuition]]
While in the Ringed City, [[Lothar (Brewster) Nash|Brewster]] learns that Gal Troxic is the former god of Order, and was popular in Maedros.
## [[Session 32 - I Didn't Ask How Big the Casino Is...]]
Gal Troxic seems to still have his mausoleum staffed by one hadozee after Shurima's takeover of the Ringed City.
## [[Session 39 - Mora]]
Gal Troxic sends one of his angels to prevent the party from collecting the Clotho Lachesis. They are able to defeat her.
## [[Session 40 - Reunited]]
A knight of Gal Troxic attacks Moiloc to prevent Shurima's reunion with Olidamuru. The group is able to defeat him.
