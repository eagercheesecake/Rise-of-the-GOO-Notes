Maokai, also known as the Forgotten One, is a nature god who was taken prisoner in a war between the gods and hasn't been seen since. All records of him are gone.

## [[Session 06 - The Party Has a Feast]]
[[Branwyn of Coryphea|Branwyn]] mentions Maokai when the group goes to the chapel in the castle in Montrissad.
## [[Session 08 - The Party Hates it Here]]
[[Grima]] tells Branwyn that the reason his Grove looks similar to [[Mora|Mora's]] is that the druidic forests were all created through Maokai. Grima also informs Branwyn that Maokai was defeated when Grovewatch was burned, becoming Gravewatch, but this event is still shrouded in mystery. Mora had a goal to find Maokai again.
