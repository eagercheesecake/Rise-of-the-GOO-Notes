## [[Session 11 - The Party Loses a Corpse]]
[[Arklow Laban]] tells [[Kaldrake Sootspot|Kaldrake]] that they fed the souls of the citizens of Brasov to Laogzed, in exchange for Laogzed resurrecting Kaldrake. Arklow says all of this was requested by [[Gwyneth Sootspot|Gwyneth]].
## [[Session 12 - Branwyn's Bad Day]]
Kaldrake tells [[Belnades]] about Laogzed resurrecting him, but Belnades says that resurrection of that kind shouldn't be possible for a djinn.
## [[Session 15 - Gray Boys]]
Kaldrake finds Laogzed's lamp hidden in Gwyneth's manor. Gwyneth gives Kaldrake permission to talk to him, but points out that she has a magic circle trapping him in the room, and asks Kaldrake not to let him out. She says his name is Laogzed, and he can be returned to his lamp by saying his name. He grants Kaldrake's wish to know how he was revived. Laogzed asks Kaldrake to wish for his freedom, but he denies, saying Laogzed's name and returning him to the lamp. After some reality resets, Kaldrake finds that Laogzed is no longer in Gwyneth's mansion.
## [[Session 18 - What Would Branwyn Do]]
Kaldrake finds that Laogzed is no longer in Gwyneth's manor since the reality reset.
## [[Session 22 - The B Team]]
Laogzed's lamp is found at the base of [[Maokai|Maokai's]] tree, creating a mist, trapping Maokai inside.
