## [[Session 06 - Cult Pamphlets]]
[[Moiloc Kodlaksdottër|Moiloc]] finds a book in [[Gwyneth Sootspot|Gwyneth's]] tower discussing Sekolah and the Sahuagin, and their attack on her home.
## [[Session 23 - Sootspot Intuition]]
[[Lothar (Brewster) Nash|Brewster]] finds an area in the Ringed City for Sekollah's worshippers, and learns that Sekollah is the god of war and the hunt. He had taken the domain of war from [[Sha-Sĭme]].
## [[Session 29 - Infighting]]
[[Martin Sootspot|Martin]] confirms in Sha-Sĭme's Crypt that Sha-Sĭme was killed by Sekollah, and may have fought Atalanta, Sha-Sĭme's final avatar.
## [[Session 32 - I Didn't Ask How Big the Casino Is...]]
Sekollah seems to still have his mausoleum staffed by one hadozee after Shurima's takeover of the Ringed City.
