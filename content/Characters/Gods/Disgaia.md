## [[Session 23 - Sootspot Intuition]]
While in the Ringed City, [[Lothar (Brewster) Nash|Brewster]] learns that Disgaia was the goddess of the earth and nature, and was worshipped by druids, rangers, and apothecaries. Disgaia was succeeded by [[Maokai]].
## [[Session 31 - Fourteen Rings]]
[[Hadrian]] gives [[Branwyn of Coryphea|Branwyn]] a book about Disgaia. The book is a fable, which says that a witch and a wizard killed Disgaia. She was consumed by a giant gaping maw, and transformed into a living tree. The only remaining artifact of Disgaia is her wedding ring, which will bring Disgaia back when reformed. The book warns that this should not happen. The party believes this is Withermold, which was recently formed by combining three rings the party had collected.
## [[Session 32 - I Didn't Ask How Big the Casino Is...]]
Disgaia seems to no longer has any hadozee guarding their mausoleum after Shurima's takeover of the Ringed Ciry. There is a dead hadozee inside the mausoleum, a plane stone sarcophagus. Later, Dario's autobiography says he allied himself with Disgaia to fight the empire of Shurima.
