## [[Session 16 - Killing a Mailman]]
[[Kaldrake Sootspot|Kaldrake]] finds Styx in a village after returning from the astral plane. Styx allows him to keep him company on the way to Lothric. When Kaldrake asks if Styx knows if he is alive, Styx presents him with a vision of [[Gwyneth Sootspot|Gwyneth]] holding a knife to his neck. Styx gives him a letter written in Celestial. Styx also gives Kaldrake a vision of [[Monty MacMasters|Monty]] in Michael's in Lothric. Kaldrake shows Styx the white crystal he got from Gwyneth's manor, and Styx throws it into the forest.
## [[Session 17 - Respect These Nuts]]
Styx arrives at Lothric with Kaldrake. He gives Kaldrake a map leading to a house in the gold district, then disappears.
