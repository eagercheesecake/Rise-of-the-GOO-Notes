Eru the Shaper is the main god in Phaeros. She created the Forgotten Ones and the Pantheon of Worship, and is often depicted without a face.

Eru has a chapel in the castle in Montrissad. Her statue is seen when [[Kaldrake Sootspot|Kaldrake]], [[Branwyn of Coryphea|Branwyn]], and [[Gertrude Spugel|Gertrude]] visit the chapel.
## [[Session 07 - The Party Sets Sail]]
[[Belnades]] tells Kaldrake that most people in Wallachia worship Eru. Upon arriving in Brazov, Belnades, Kaldrake, and Lexar head to the Inquisitors' monastery, with many statues of Eru around.
