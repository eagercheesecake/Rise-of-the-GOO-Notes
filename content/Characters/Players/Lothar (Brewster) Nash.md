## [[Session 10 - The Party Gets Bit]]
Brewster meets [[Branwyn of Coryphea|Branwyn]] and [[Moiloc Kodlaksdottër|Moiloc]] when they come to learn about his study on ydril. He mends Moiloc's tattered armor, and she immediately goes into a long-winded story about how she lost her old armor. Brewster says his entire family was enslaved, so he doesn't have much pity for her. He also identifies Branwyn's ring as having some sort of warding magic on it, and identifies Moiloc's behelit as a key that responds to some phrase. He asks where she got the behelit, and she tells him about [[Shurima the Corpsemaker|Shurima]]. Brewster mentions that Shurima used to be a country until it was destroyed 400-500 years ago. Brewster makes her promise to tell him if anything else happens with Shurima, since this piques his interest.

Moiloc, Branwyn, and [[Kaldrake Sootspot|Kaldrake]] come to bring Brewster to the Chateau to investigate a lead on information pertaining to ydril. Brewster tells Kaldrake that he had always wanted to attend the Aethereum. He also identifies the stamp Shurima gave Moiloc as the coat of arms for the Braeton family, and tells them a bit about the history of rule over Wallachia. Brewster brings [[Puck Nash|Puck]] with them to the Chateau.

Brewster is able to recognize a few things in the Chateau: a book about the Braeton family and a circle meant for summoning the undead. The group is attacked by bats and vampires while exploring the upper floor. Brewster uses his knowledge to identify many things in the Chateau. The group is worried entering the cellar, so Brewster sends Puck into the meat locker to see if he triggers any traps, which he does not. The group finds [[Camilla Braeton|Camilla]] and [[Carmine Braeton|Carmine Braeton's]] coffins in the cellars. Camilla's is destroyed, and Carmine's is blocked shut with an immovable rod, which Moiloc deactivates and removes. Carmine explodes out of the coffin in a burst of energy, beginning to fight the others. During the fight, Brewster is downed, but Branwyn is able to kill Carmine and revive him. They collect Kaldrake's body to take it back to Dunn, and Branwyn makes Brewster promise to never let [[Dr. Nubgrub]] touch it.
## [[Session 11 - The Party Loses a Corpse]]
The group notices three-toed footprints leading to the library of the Chateau while they go to leave. Moiloc tells them that Shurima says they shouldn't enter, and goes in alone. Brewster and Branwyn ask her to grab Celestial books for research, but she is unable to do so. The group decides to just leave the Chateau. They decide to go to Dunn to get Kaldrake's body to safety.

On their way, they see [[Queen Cypha|Queen Cypha's]] carriages and soldiers proceeding to Brasov. They question going to Brasov to warn [[Gorm]], but decide they are unable to. They set up camp for the night and take shifts to watch over Kaldrake's corpse. During Brewster's shift, a blue explosion is seen from Brasov, and in the morning, they find that Kaldrake's corpse is gone. They see boot tracks leading toward Brasov, and decide to head there to look for Kaldrake. Brewster finds a chest on the shore, but is unable to open it. He digs through the ruins of [[Jessie Harkness|Jessie's]] bar to find the tunnel leading to Dunn, and they proceed back after finding evidence that the Queen's soldiers may be going there next.

At Dunn, the group sees the Queen's caravans approach, and are surprised to see Shurima exit. He somehow sacrifices some of the soldiers with him to free [[Ouroborus Tikktal]] from her chains. He then magically drains energy from the remaining soldiers, transforming Ouroborus into a black, undead dragon, and disappearing. Brewster rushes to check on the dragon, who wakes up and flies off. Inside the blood-soaked cave, Brewster finds [[King Endrio the Bold|King Endrio's]] cape. He also has Branwyn open the chest from Brasov, and gets a cloudy crystal of teleportation, but doesn't know the catchphrase.

Moiloc is able to use Shurima's name to open the door to the magical carriage, and finds a set of dark, snake armor inside. Brewster Identifies the armor through Puck as being magical, but not harmful. He also recognizes it, but can't place where he knows it from. Moiloc puts it on, and disappears. While she is gone, Brewster Identifies Monty's gauntlets as helping with sleight of hand. When Moiloc returns, they question her, but she won't answer.
## [[Session 12 - The Party Travels]]
Branwyn gets scared unusual changes in the nearby forest, and the group decides to move on to Lothric. Soon after leaving, Branwyn begins to feel immense pain from her bite wound, and they pull over. Brewster notices the wound turning black, commenting that he's seen nothing like it before.

Looking at Gwyneth's book on Mora, she finds that one of Mora's cabins is nearby. Branwyn makes a magic compass to track down the cabin, using Puck as the container for the ritual, and she and Brewster are magically transported to the woods surrounding the cabin. The cabin is burnt, and they find a humanoid with bark skin burnt on the foot of the cabin. Inside is ransacked, and as they approach the cabin, a snoutkai attacks the pair. They defeat it, and inside the bedroom they find the corpse of a child that looks identical to Branwyn, and another journal, with all non-blank pages ripped out. Brewster mentions that [[Daphne]] may be able to help identify what is happening with the duplicate body.

Moiloc, Brewster, and Branwyn reunite in Dunn. They decide to continue to Lothric, and are attacked by yeth hounds on the way. They decide to continue moving through the night, taking shifts with one person driving the carriage at a time. During Brewster's shift, he sees a shirtless and battered Gorm walking the other direction, with purple cracks on his face. Goro refuses to join them, and Brewster carries on. Brewster magically forgets this interaction. Later that night, they arrive at Lothric.
## [[Session 13 - The Party Kills Innocents]]
Brewster wakes the others once they arrive at the slums outside Lothric, and tells them a bit about the city and local government. The next day, the group gives the carriage away, and Brewster uses Disguise Self to appear as a human child in the city. When the group gets to the gate, a guard takes them to a nearby building to get their papers. Branwyn introduces Brewster as Brewster of Coryphea, her brother. Moiloc says the reason for their visit is because Montrissad was destroyed and they were visiting, which seems to make the Inquisitor suspicious. Brewster saves this saying they are looking for asylum from non-humies. They get a six-day visa, after Branwyn and Brewster pay off the Inquisitor.

Brewster suggests the look for the Resistance hideout. However, while looking, they notice shadows watching them from the alley, and follow them into the sewers. They find a detached arm in the sewers and decide to go back to the surface. Brewster recalls Daphne telling him about a serial killer in Lothric whose victims would be found without an arm. They continue to the market district, where they find a shop full of magical trinkets. Brewster is able to buy a magical mask that transforms the wearer into the previous wearer. Upon wearing it, he becomes [[Gwyneth Sootspot|Gwyneth]]. Branwyn magically forgets about the store upon leaving, but Moiloc reminds him about the mask he bought. The group proceeds to the inn, who says they smell awful from the sewers, and they proceed to a bathhouse.

At the bathhouse, Brewster attempts to trick some children into wearing the mask, so he can take on the identity of a human that matches his papers. He accidentally reveals that he hadn't gone to church, which frightened the child and some other nearby citizens. He decides to move on and get new papers if he needs to, and just get the mask on anyone nearby. He attempts to use Mage Hand to place the mask on a nearby man, but he sees the mask, takes it out of the air, puts in on, then puts it in his backpack. Brewster and Branwyn follow him, leaving Moiloc behind because her armor is too loud to sneak, hoping to get the mask back.

Branwyn and Brewster enter the bakery the man works at under the guise of looking for Branwyn's daughter, Charlotte. Branwyn makes a scene, and one of the two bakers leaves to get Inquisitors to help find Charlotte. Brewster uses this as a distraction to try and sneak in and get the mask, but the other baker notices this, and picks him up to stop him. This causes the Disguise Self illusion to be broken, and the baker realizes Brewster is a gnome. Branwyn attempts to shoot the baker so he unhands Brewster, but accidentally kills him. They grab the mask, and proceed to the back room, finding a dead end as the Inquisitors and other baker arrive. They manage to escape into nearby sewers, but are seen during their escape.
## [[Session 14 - The Party Retcons]]
Brewster finds papers and IDs in the pack he took from the bakery, completing his theft of the baker's identity. Brewster and Branwyn continue through the sewers, eventually hearing slapping sounds. Branwyn mentions that anything making that sound in the sewer is something they don't want to deal with, and they run away, finding themselves lost in their panic. They find a magically-dug room in the sewers, full of grates and barrels containing viscera. They push a barrel aside, and find a summoning circle under it. They also find a knife. Puck dumps some bile into the sewers outside the room, with the group hoping this would distract the oncoming monster. This does distract the monster, but as they run away, some rations fall out of Branwyn's pack, causing the monster to chase them again. Branwyn uses Rope Trick allowing them to escape into an extradimensional space. Branwyn is slashed by the monster, and Brewster heals her while they hide away.

They wait for some time, then leave the space and are able to exit the sewers. However, they find themselves in the area of town reserved for the Queen's advisors. They decide they can't leave that area without being noticed, and return to the sewers, where they are immediately chased again by corpse flayers. They are able to fight them off, and have Puck close a portcullis to separate them from the monsters.

After Branwyn and Brewster reunite with Moiloc, they find another shadow man following them. Moiloc uses Command on it, and it leads them to the Resistance hideout.
## [[Session 15 - The Party Has a Meeting]]
Moiloc, Branwyn, and Brewster enter the Resistance's hideout. Brewster and Branwyn find Dr. Nubgrub working on a corpse flayer, and he tells them about his findings. They reunite with Moiloc and go to meet with the heads of the Resistance.

At the meeting, Brewster tells Violet about Kaldrake's passing, but she doesn't seem affected by it. He also tries to warn her that Moiloc shouldn't be present for the meeting, but she doesn't care, since they can take all the help they can get. He fills in everyone on what has happened since they left. When showing King Endrio's cloak, he is asked if Tarren may be related to King Endrio. Brewster says they might, believing he's Endrio's illegitimate son. Branwyn suggests Brewster gives the cloak to Tarren, but he dodges the request and puts the cloak away. The group discusses taking an invitation for the queen's upcoming melee to determine her new champion, and who would be best to be next in line for the role of Pontiff.

Violet tells Brewster he did well on his mission, and gives him a crossbow to regenerate spell slots. She asks him to continue his experimentation on ydril, giving him a crystal they were able to take from Dunn. Brewster begins to analyze the blueprints from the Chateau.
## [[Session 16 - The Party Follows Strangers]]
Brewster begins making the ydril machine. He asks Moiloc and Branwyn to get him some gears while they are at the market.
## [[Session 17 - Respect These Nuts]]
Brewster, Branwyn, and Moiloc help the Resistance defend against corpse eaters. They go to the prisoners, and find one dead, and the other weak. They kill the corpse eaters in the prison, and Moiloc tries to rescue the remaining prisoner, but Brewster stops her. She gives them a map to a fountain, and they let her out. They follow blood trails out of the hideout, into a sewer, and find a mysterious door, which Moiloc breaks down. It leads to a rickety bridge over a bottomless pit, and an underground town. In a tent in the town, Brewster finds a magical ring. Upon putting it on, it begins to tighten, and his finger begins to wither, and he has Moiloc pull the finger and the ring off. Brewster finds flowers which can prevent rot. Brewster notices the entire town is covered in illusion magic.

The group heads down a hallway, and are trapped in a boiling room, although they escape. They reach the end of the tunnel to find [[Olidamuru]], who had taken over Lexar's body, about to sacrifice Sulyvahn. Moiloc uses a Fireball scroll to try and take Olidamuru down, but he shields against it and completes the sacrifice, with all the monsters in the room turning to chase them. The group escapes and finds Kaldrake somehow outside the town, but Olidamuru, now a large fog-like entity, captures Moiloc and holds her over the bottomless pit. Branwyn suggests a trade, saying she's a green dreamer, and Olidamuru says he'll return Moiloc if Branwyn takes off her ring momentarily. She does so, and Mora's voice fills the chamber, saying she has found them. Olidamuru returns Moiloc and disappears as the cave begins to collapse. The group returns to the Resistance hideout to find battered survivors, and catch each other up on what has happened since they were separated.
