## [[Session 40 - Reunited]]
A disheveled Helen finds Galen Laborde at her home in Whitehall. She reports her findings on corpse eaters, and gives an order from the High Architect to track down Gwyneth in the Capella Sinistra. They track her down, following a trail of blood into Gwyneth's secret office. Galen finds an entrance to the Underdark, where she finds Gwyneth near-dead, impaled by Mora's thorns. Galen interrogates Gwyneth, getting her journal. Unable to decode it, and afraid of magic traps in the bindings, she rips the pages out and takes them.

Galen joins Moiloc in fighting her final battle against the corpse eaters. Corpse flayers begin to fall from the ceiling as Olidamuru begins to appear and be contained. Once the ritual is complete, Shurima gains Eru's powers. Moiloc says she will join the others if they choose to fight against Shurima, but asks if they are ready to die trying. Galen takes out a crown with Styx's symbol on it, and Styx becomes visible for a moment. She gives it to her, and Shurima puts it on, saying it is too late to defeat Shurima, but with her powers returned to her, they may be able to stop him. They decide to run, but Moiloc decides to continue to worship Shurima, and kneels down to pray, but is crushed by a falling chunk of the ceiling.
## [[Session 41 - Underage Drinking]]
Galen joins the others while returning to Velbrass.
## [[Session 42 - Allegiances]]
Galen tends to her frostbite before arriving to Velbrass. When they arrive, she catches a pickpocket following them, but is able to scare him off by asking where he thinks he goes when he dies. The group arrive at the Cat's Paw and enter their old room. Branwyn casts Alarm on the door. Galen begins deciphering Gwyneth's book. Soon after, this alarm is tripped by Janos and Moiloc. Galen is suspicious of Janos, due to his Maedrosi armor, and is worried that he was sent to check her true allegiance to Maedros. The group checks out the night market, where Galen and Janos try to get camels from a merchant by promising they will get his necklace, which he lost in the gambling hall, back. They are able to do so, and are briefly ambushed as they leave, although Janos is able to scare them off. Janos asks Galen if her allegiance to Maedros is true, but the risk of being exposed is too high for Galen to say is isn't as solid as Maedros would like it to be.
