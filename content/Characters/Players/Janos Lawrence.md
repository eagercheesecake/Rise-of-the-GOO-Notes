Janos is a human fighter who used to be a high-ranking official in the Maedrosi guard. He wears shining white armor and wields Cato's halberd. Blaming chaos itself for everything he had lost in his childhood, he wins a game against Olidamuru and earns his boon, becoming immune to the effects of chaos on the world.

- **First Seen:** Session 42
- **Status:** Alive

## [[Session 42 - Allegiances]]
Janos enters Velbrass, looking for Galen after receiving a lead that she may be able to lead him to Olidamuru. He instead finds Moiloc, drunk, and they have a lively conversation, where she fills him in on Shurima, Olidamuru, and the other members of her party from Montrissad who Janos says King Davis was concerned about. Janos tries to return Moiloc to the Cat's Paw, but sets off Branwyn's alarm, causing a brief confrontation. While introducing himself, Janos shows his resistance to chaos with a coin flip, although it doesn't go ass well as he had hoped. The group checks out the night market, where Galen and Janos try to get camels from a merchant by promising they will get his necklace, which he lost in the gambling hall, back. They are able to do so, and are briefly ambushed as they leave, although Janos is able to scare them off. Janos asks Galen if her allegiance to Maedros is true, hinting that his isn't, but Galen doesn't confirm the same.
