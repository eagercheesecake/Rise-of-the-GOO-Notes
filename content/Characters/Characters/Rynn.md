## [[Session 05 - The Party Explores a Castle]]
Rynn is seen being kicked out of an inn. She steals gold from [[Moiloc Kodlaksdottër|Moiloc]], and is chased after and cornered by [[Lexar Bellos|Lexar]], [[Kaldrake Sootspot|Kaldrake]], and [[Branwyn of Coryphea|Branwyn]], but she is able to hide the gold she stole. Later, Rynn is seen at the castle having joined the Games.
## [[Session 06 - The Party Has a Feast]]
Rynn is seen at the pre-game feast. The dessert of the feast turns out to be poisoned, causing the group to become sick, and the guards attack the contestants.
