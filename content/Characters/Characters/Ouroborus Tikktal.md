## [[Session 09 - The Man or the Bears]]
[[Tarren Hammerfist|Tarren]] tells [[Moiloc Kodlaksdottër|Moiloc]] about Ouroborus, and Moiloc makes plans to visit once they get settled.
## [[Session 11 - The Party Loses a Corpse]]
[[Shurima the Corpsemaker|Shurima]] is seen exiting carriages bearing [[Queen Cypha|Queen Cypha's]] markings. He somehow sacrifices some of the soldiers with him to free Ouroborus from her chains. He then magically drains energy from the remaining soldiers, transforming Ouroborus into a black, undead dragon, and disappearing. [[Lothar (Brewster) Nash|Brewster]] rushes to check if the collapsed Ouroborus is okay, and she rises, commenting that she no longer needs to breathe, before flying off.
## [[Session 15 - Gray Boys]]
Brewster tells the other Resistance members about what they witnessed with Ouroborus.
## [[Session 22 - The B Team]]
[[Martin Sootspot|Martin]], Ouroborus (in a dragonborn form, going as Ororo), [[Mora|Morrigan]], [[Contigo Muñoz|Contigo]], and [[Arklow Laban|Arklow]] arrive in Col Man'Dar after Martin sees a vision of [[Kaldrake Sootspot|Kaldrake's]] death, and find his corpse. The group finds Goro in the lumber mill, and [[Velma|Velma's]] corpse in the forest. While leaving through the forest, Ouroborus discovers that Martin is under the effects of magic, and dispels it, causing Martin to fight with Morrigan about the Dominate Person spell she used. Morrigan explains she is looking for a childhood friend, and Martin agrees to help look, but on the path instead of in the middle of the forest. During Ouroborus's shift, she realizes that Morrigan and Contigo are gone. Thinking Morrigan has used Dominate Person on Contigo, Ouroborus wakes up Martin, and they give chase.

In Edoval, Ouroborus tells Martin that she detects a heart in the chapel. Inside the chapel is the heart, with a small hydra above it. Once entering, Ouroborus darts for the heart, causing a pulse of energy that knocks Martin out. Martin wakes up to see Ouroborus, back in dragon form, flying away with the hydra. She drops it on the ground, and it explodes in a pool of viscera.
