## [[Session 08 - Religious Conversion]]
[[Olidamuru]] assigns Jiren to assist [[Lexar Bellos|Lexar]] after he nearly dies during his initiation with the Inquisitors. Jiren is able to help Lexar save [[Goro Hattori|Goro]] and [[Belnades]] from the Inquisitors after they are kidnapped in their siege on Brasov.
## [[Session 09 - The Man or the Bears]]
Jiren helps Lexar fight when the Wavecrasher is boarded by the Inquisitors. Lexar is killed on the Wavecrasher, and Jiren likely returns to Olidamuru.
