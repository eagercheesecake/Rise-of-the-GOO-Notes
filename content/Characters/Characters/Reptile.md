## [[Session 29 - Infighting]]
[[Moiloc Kodlaksdottër|Moiloc]], [[Martin Sootspot|Martin]], [[Helen Berestova|Helen]], and [[Tarren Hammerfist|Tarren]] see Reptile stealing a gem from a jewel salesman.
## [[Session 30 - Discretion]]
Moiloc meets up with Reptile in an attempt to recruit him. She learns that Reptile was a former slave at Whitehall. His father died during the escape, after which [[Shurima the Corpsemaker|Shurima]] offered help for him and his young siblings. Reptile devotes himself to Shurima, who tells him to help Moiloc when she arrives at Velbrass. He offers to join the party to Whitehall if he can get his siblings enrolled at the Lyceum so he knows they're safe. Reptile also reveals that he knows [[Tarren Hammerfist|Tarren]] from their time at Whitehall.
## [[Session 31 - Fourteen Rings]]
[[Branwyn of Coryphea|Branwyn]] pays Reptile, who goes and gets his siblings set up at the Lyceum.
## [[Session 32 - I Didn't Ask How Big the Casino Is...]]
The group proceeds to Whitehall, and buy a small house to make into a base.
