## [[Session 10 - The Party Gets Bit]]
[[Lothar (Brewster) Nash|Brewster]] tells the party about the Braeton family's rule, which ended about 25 years ago. Around this time, [[King Endrio the Bold|King Endrio]] was assassinated and [[Queen Cypha]] rose to power. He says King Endrio used to fight for equal rights, and freed Wallachia from the Orkus Badlands and Amaris. A statue of King Endrio and his horse, Boltz, is seen outside the Chateau.
