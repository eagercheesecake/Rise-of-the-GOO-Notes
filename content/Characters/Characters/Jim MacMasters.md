### [[Session 01 - The Party Fights Sewer Rats]]
[[Kaldrake Sootspot|Kaldrake]] purchases a room from Jim. [[Branwyn of Coryphea|Branwyn]] gives him a note from Monty asking for a room, and he provides her with one as well. He also gives [[Moiloc Kodlaksdottër|Moiloc]] a room in exchange for her working in the pub. He gives directions to Branwyn when she asks where Moiloc is so she can join her team for the Games.
## [[Session 04 - The Party Puts Out a Fire]]
A group of cloaked figures set fire to the Hatched Pig Inn and attack Jim. Besides these thieves and the adventurers, Jim sees nobody leave the inn. [[Gertrude Spugel|Gertrude]] heals him, and he reveals a secret passage under the bar that allows for escape. He is joined by Moiloc and Branwyn, and takes them to Monty's office through the secret tunnel. Jim takes a potion from Monty's desk and heals himself, and tells the others that the attackers were likely enemies that he and his brother Monty had made during their business ventures.

Jim sees Branwyn has become charmed by a gem on display in the room, and throws a paperweight at her to free her.
