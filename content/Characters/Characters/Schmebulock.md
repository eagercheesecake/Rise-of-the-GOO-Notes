## [[Session 25 - I Know an Archer]]
[[Lothar (Brewster) Nash|Brewster]] asks Schmebulock if he saw [[Jessie Harkness|Jessie]] leave after her disappearance. Schmebulock says he has not seen the eye symbol on Jessie's sheets. Schmebulock tries to give viscous sludge, which he calls coffee, to the guests. Brewster and [[Moiloc Kodlaksdottër|Moiloc]] accept. Brewster grows an extra eye that gives him true sight, and Moiloc begins to float.
