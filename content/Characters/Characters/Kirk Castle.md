## [[Session 33 - I Want To See a Mushroom]]
Kirk Castle is the Minister of Production in Whitehall, and is in charge of the craftsman district, and the quicksilver industry. He is given a tour of the Underdark from the Hammerfist monks, which [[Moiloc Kodlaksdottër|Moiloc]] accompanies him on. After some time in the caves, Moiloc has Kirk lean over a deep pit, chops him in the neck with her axe, and pushes his body into the pit.
