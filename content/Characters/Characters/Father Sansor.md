Father Sansor is a priest in Eru's chapel in the castle in Montrissad. He disguises himself as a worshipper of Eru, but is actually a disciple of Olidamuru.

- **First Seen:** Session 6
- **Status:** Unknown

## [[Session 06 - The Party Has a Feast]]
[[Kaldrake Sootspot|Kaldrake]], [[Branwyn of Coryphea|Branwyn]], and [[Gwyneth Sootspot|Gwyneth]] come to Father Sansor to ask about [[Gwyneth Sootspot|Gwyneth's]] whereabouts. He says she left a week ago, and points them to her friend [[Goro Hattori|Goro]] for more information. Soon after they leave, [[Lexar Bellos|Lexar]] enters. Father Sansor gives him a sacrificial dagger and leads him to a prisoner they have tied up below the chapel, revealing he is actually a disciple of Olidamuru as well. He tells Lexar to sacrifice the prisoner, and he does so, turning the prisoner into a corpse eater.
