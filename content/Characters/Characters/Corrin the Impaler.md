## [[Session 18 - What Would Branwyn Do]]
[[Shurima the Corpsemaker|Shurima]] asks [[Moiloc Kodlaksdottër|Moiloc]] to collect Corrin's eye for him. He tells her Corrin is heading to take back the castle from [[Olidamuru]]. [[Kaldrake Sootspot|Kaldrake]], [[Lothar (Brewster) Nash|Brewster]], [[Branwyn of Coryphea|Branwyn]], and [[Moiloc Kodlaksdottër|Moiloc]] find him in the castle, and Kaldrake decapitates him. Moiloc takes his eye and gives it to Shurima, who inserts it into his empty eye socket.
