## [[Session 09 - The Party Downsizes]]
[[Branwyn of Coryphea|Branwyn]] stops by Gorm's tent to take extra bow and arrows they aren't using.
## [[Session 10 - The Party Gets Bit]]
Branwyn is able to get what she was looking for at Gorm's tent, but Moiloc can't find replacement armor. The next day, Gorm is seen rallying a group of raiders before an upcoming raid.
## [[Session 11 - The Party Loses a Corpse]]
Seeing [[Queen Cypha|Queen Cypha's]] carriages going to Dunn, the group considers going to Brasov to warn Gorm about the incoming soldiers, but decide they wouldn't be able to beat them there, or fight in their condition. After Brasov is destroyed, no evidence is found that Gorm's raiders escaped.
