## [[Session 07 - The Party Sets Sail]]
[[Belnades]] brings [[Lexar Bellos|Lexar]] and [[Kaldrake Sootspot|Kaldrake]] to Father Gregor. She informs him that the Inquisitors failed their mission in Montrissad, but Lexar and Kaldrake may be looking to join. Kaldrake introduces himself as a Sootspot, which seems to upset Father Gregor. He is pleased, however, when Lexar tells him he's a former member of the Cult of Miasma, but that [[Father Sansor]] had convinced him to convert. Father Gregor asks the pair to leave him and Belnades to talk for a bit, and points them to the barracks, where [[Ponce]] will meet them. After they leave, Father Gregor punishes her for failing their mission, causing her to need an eyepatch.
## [[Session 08 - The Party Hates it Here]]
Father Gregor sees Kaldrake and Lexar for their initiations. Kaldrake passes his initiation, and Father Gregor presents him with the badge of the Inquisitors, saying he'll get his first mission soon. Lexar fails his initiation, and Father Gregor has him tossed into the pit the Inquisitors throw their corpses in. He then provides Belnades and Kaldrake with an assignment to investigate a caravan that was attacked by the Resistance.

After Kaldrake and Belnades return from the caravan, Kaldrake frees the Inquisitors' prisoner, [[Violet]]. Kaldrake then enters Gregor's office as a distraction while an invisible Violet sneaks behind him, slits his throat, and bashes him with nearby books.
