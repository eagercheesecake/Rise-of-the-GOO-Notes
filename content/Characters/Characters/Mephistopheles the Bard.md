## [[Session 29 - Infighting]]
Mephistopheles is seen performing in the Cat's Paw.
## [[Session 31 - Fourteen Rings]]
[[Martin Sootspot|Martin]] notices Mephistopheles watching as he makes a deal with [[Holliday]], and goes to question him. Mephistopheles says he is under contract with the Bagworm, and asks Martin to free him. Martin reluctantly agrees. He decides to join the party on their journey to Whitehall after he learns they have killed the Bagworm. The party has him question the Bagworm using Speak to Dead, since he is a trusted face.
## [[Session 32 - I Didn't Ask How Big the Casino Is...]]
Mephistopheles tells Martin and Moiloc that he owes 100g in gambling debts. Together, they enter the gambling den and pay it off. They then Fireball the gambling den, killing everyone inside, and looting the 100g and more. The group proceeds to Whitehall, and buy a small house to make into a base.
