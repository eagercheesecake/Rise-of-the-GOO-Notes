## [[Session 30 - Discretion]]
[[Branwyn of Coryphea|Branwyn]] meets Hadrian when she enters the mausoleum of [[Dario the Twin]]. She follows him down a hall to take a tour and learn more about Dario, ending with the final resting place of Dario. He also shows her his pendant, called the Everheart, which contains a part of Dario's soul. The Valentines hope they can use it to resurrect Dario one day. After the tour, [[Lothar (Brewster) Nash|Brewster]] asks Hadrian if [[Ishtar]] can join them on their journey to Whitehall, which he approves. Brewster also asks him to restore [[Helen|Helen's]] tongue.
## [[Session 31 - Fourteen Rings]]
Branwyn and Brewster return to Helen in the mausoleum, with Hadrian, who heals her. Branwyn asks if he knows anything about gods of nature, and he gives her a book about [[Disgaia]]. Later, he also regenerates Brewster's finger.
