## [[Session 15 - Gray Boys]]
[[Gwyneth Sootspot|Gwyneth]] tells [[Kaldrake Sootspot|Kaldrake]] that [[Martin the Great||Martin the Great]] made a deal with [[Baedos|Bados]] to sell Martin's soul to [[Abaddon]]. Bados cursed the Sootspot family so that they die at the age of 100.
## [[Session 16 - Killing a Mailman]]
Bados is mentioned as part of [[Gulvayas Hakon|Gulvayas's]] plan to become Archwizard, although his role is not clear. Gulvayas offers [[Mora|Morrigan]] a vial, saying it will protect her from Bados's control.
## [[Session 35]]
Gwyneth gives [[Martin Sootspot|Martin]] a book about Bados, saying she was the god of fate.
