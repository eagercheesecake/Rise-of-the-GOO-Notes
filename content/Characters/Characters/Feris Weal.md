## [[Session 16 - The Party Follows Strangers]]
Feris asks [[Moiloc Kodlaksdottër|Moiloc]] and [[Branwyn of Coryphea|Branwyn]] to try and get information from prisoners the Rebels have taken, as well as asking if they could get some materials from the market. He provides some gold for them to do so. They return to him with the items, and an invitation to the Inquisitor tournament, and he sends them to get Inquisitor garb from the prisoners and buy horses.
