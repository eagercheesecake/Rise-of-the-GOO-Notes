Feris Weal is the head of the Lothric branch of the Resistance. He is a tiefling veteran with a metallic prosthetic leg.

## [[Session 15 - Gray Boys]]
Feris is present at the Resistance's planning meeting.
## [[Session 16 - Killing a Mailman]]
Feris asks [[Moiloc Kodlaksdottër|Moiloc]] and [[Branwyn of Coryphea|Branwyn]] to try and get information from prisoners the Resistance have taken, as well as asking if they could get some materials from the market. He provides some gold for them to do so. They return to him with the items, and an invitation to the Inquisitor tournament, and he sends them to get Inquisitor garb from the prisoners and buy horses.
## [[Session 17 - Respect These Nuts]]
Feris is seen battling corpse eaters as they invade the Resistance hideout, having lost a few fingers. Moiloc heals him as she heads out. Feris is also seen tending to the wounds of Resistance members after the group returns from their confrontation with Olidamuru.
## [[Session 18 - What Would Branwyn Do]]
Feris is seen while the Resistance is attempting to move their hideout to Domnhall Keep.
