## [[Session 22 - The B Team]]
Mephistopheles appears to [[Moiloc Kodlaksdottër|Moiloc]] when she wakes up in Gahenna, asking if she wants a closer look at the city. Moiloc agrees, and he teleports her to his tower.
## [[Session 23 - Sootspot Intuition]]
Moiloc talks with Mephistopheles, who says he's the rules of two-fifths of Gahenna, having killed the previous ruler. Mephistopheles says [[Moona]] was his follower, doing anything he asked her. He says she died in the stress of childbirth and he collected. He shows a vial of writhing black liquid that he keeps on a pendant, implying it's Moona's soul. Moiloc tells him that she is a follower of [[Shurima the Corpsemaker|Shurima]], which seems to scare Mephistopheles. He tells her to give a ring to a Sootspot, promising a favor to be exchanged at any time. Moiloc signs a contract agreeing to this.
