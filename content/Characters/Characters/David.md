## [[Session 05 - Can't Be Worse Than Here]]
Lexar and [[Moiloc Kodlaksdottër|Moiloc]] talk with David in an attempt to convince him and a team of farmhands to join the Games. David reluctantly agrees, and rides horseback with his team to the castle. David rides on his favorite horse, Dash Man. David enrolls with the team name David and the Horse Men.
## [[Session 06 - Cult Pamphlets]]
David and the Horse Men are seen at the pre-game feast. He mentions to Lexar that he saw [[Boofoon]] die, and is shaken up. The dessert of the feast turns out to be poisoned, causing him to become sick. He pulls out his hammer as the guards attack the contestants. However, he freezes up, and doesn't escape with Teddy & the Roosevelts.
## [[Session 07 - Shuffleboard]]
At some point, [[Goro Hattori|Goro]] rescues David and [[King Aslan]] from the castle, and takes them to his ship. They reunite with Teddy & The Roosevelts outside the ship, but David is scarred and unresponsive. Goro uses David's hammer to break through a sewer grate and onto Goro's ship, the Wavecrasher. After setting sail, the group hears something from under the decks, and descends to find [[Skalazar]], a stowaway on the ship. The group decides to head to Brasov, in Wallachia.

At the end of the journey, Lexar and Moiloc throw a party to get David out of his funk. Lexar uses his Unseen Servant to have David win a game of shuffleboard. However, David stays unresponsive. Despite this, Skalazar befriends David and Lexar, bonding over the party.
## [[Session 09 - The Man or the Bears]]
David is seen fighting onboard the Wavecrasher as it is taken over by the Inquisitors. Gertrude later sees David imprisoned on an Inquisitor ship at the Brasov docks.
## [[Session 11 - The Party Loses a Corpse]]
Kaldrake finds David on the High Pontate, and gets [[Arklow Laban]] to set him free. The group returns to Wallachia, and sets up camp after beginning to walk to Fort Bosco for horses.
## [[Session 12 - Branwyn's Bad Day]]
In the morning, David and Goro go to hunt a deer for food for the day. David talks with Goro, telling him he had decided to leave the group to find his husband.
## [[Session 29 - Infighting]]
Around a hundred mercenaries from Velbrass are sent to assist David in taking back Montrissad.
