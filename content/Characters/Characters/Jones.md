Jones is a human Inquisitor who formerly worked under Arklow Laban. He is very racist, and not a very accomplished fighter. He dreams of killing all non-humans as revenge for an orc killing his uncle during an invasion of the Orkus Badlands.

- **First Seen:** Session 11
- **Status:** Alive

## [[Session 11 - The Party Loses a Corpse]]
After being revived, [[Kaldrake Sootspot|Kaldrake]] questions Jones on what happened. Jones shows immense respect and fear for Kaldrake and the Sootspot family. He informs Kaldrake that he is on the High Pontate, and that [[Gwyneth Sootspot|Gwyneth]] took him there, also noting that she is the new Grand Vizier. He also says Brasov has been destroyed, and they have declared war on Amaris. He then directs Kaldrake to [[Arklow Laban]], saying he is in charge. Kaldrake soon returns, saying he sees himself in Jones, and takes him with him as they leave the ship. Kaldrake also takes Jones's armor and shield, not having his own.

The group returns to Wallachia, and when Kaldrake mentions Dunn, Jones says Dunn should already be destroyed. Kaldrake takes Jones's map to try to find Lothric or Dunn. Jones mentions that Fort Bosco might be a good place to get horses. Kaldrake gets annoyed with Jones's idolization of him, and tells Jones to return to the High Pontate, and he'll be relieved of duty after that. Jones promises to fulfill his dreams once he is free from duty.

Jones returns to Kaldrake after leaving the High Pontate again, saying with his newfound freedom he has decided to help Kaldrake with his mission. He also says he dreams of killing all non-humans, because a non-human killed his uncle while he was invading the Orkus Badlands. He admits to killing his adopted goblin brother, but says his brother deserved it for trying to kill his mom. The group sets up camp for the night.
## [[Session 12 - The Party Travels]]
Kaldrake wakes up to Jones cooking beans in the morning. Once Jones notices Kaldrake is awake, he excitedly pulls him into a conversation, revealing that he's vegan. He says Fort Bosco is about two days away, before returning to talking about how non-humans are only good to be slaves. Kaldrake mentions that anyone can be a slave, trying that approach for racial equality, but this only confuses Jones into thinking Kaldrake is saying he is Kaldrake's slave, a situation he seems to be fine with. On their way to Fort Bosco, the group is attacked by treants. Jones tends to [[Belnades|Belnades's]] wounds after the fight. At Fort Bosco, Kaldrake and Jones decide to enter alone, since the others are not human. Jones says Kaldrake should be received more kindly since he is a sootspot.
