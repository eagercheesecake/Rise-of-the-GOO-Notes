## [[Session 06 - The Party Has a Feast]]
King Aslan hosts a feast before the second round of the Games begins. While staring at the king, [[Gertrude Spugel|Gertrude]] notices that he has two right hands. During the feast, the desserts turn out to be poisoned, and his guards attack him and the contestants. Aslan is badly hurt during the fight.
