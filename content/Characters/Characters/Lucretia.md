## [[Session 30 - Discretion]]
[[Martin Sootspot|Martin]] finds Lucretia at an archaeological dig outside of the Lyceum. They return to the Lyceum to catch up. When Martin mentions he is going to Whitehall, Lucrecia asks to join them, hoping to talk to [[Daphne]]. They proceed to the headmaster, and get his permission to let Lucretia leave the school for a time to join them.
## [[Session 31 - Fourteen Rings]]
Martin, [[Goro Hattori|Goro]], and Lucretia return to the mercenary guild to meet up with the others. They join Martin and [[Tarren Hammerfist|Tarren]] to negotiate with [[Holliday]]. Lucretia leaves for a moment to grab what she needs from the Lyceum, and joins Martin at the Cat's Paw. Once Martin tells the group about the assassins the Bagworm is preparing to kill them, they make a plan to take out the Bagworm. However, Lucretia quickly realizes that she is not prepared for this battle, and runs off.
