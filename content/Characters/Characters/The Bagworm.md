## [[Session 29 - Infighting]]
[[Lothar (Brewster) Nash|Brewster]], [[Branwyn of Coryphea|Branwyn]], and [[Vulcan]] meet with the Bagworm. He offers to help them with whatever they need if they can retrieve something from the Lyceum. Branwyn listens in on partygoers in the bar, and learns of someone who had an accident after longstanding debt to the Bagworm.
## [[Session 31 - Fourteen Rings]]
[[Mephistopheles the Bard]] asks [[Martin Sootspot|Martin]] to free him from the Bagworm. Martin finds a hooded figure meeting with the Bagworm and follows him to some barracks, filled with assassins. In the barracks, he finds a letter from [[Gwyneth Sootspot|Gwyneth]], who had told the Bagworm to kill four adventurers, but save Martin's body if he dies.

Once Martin tells the group about the assassins the Bagworm is preparing to kill them, they make a plan to take out the Bagworm. They find that the Bagworm has taken control over a death knight, who they cannot hope to defeat, so they find the Bagworm in the basement and kill him. Branwyn disguises herself as the Bagworm, and uses his rings to break into his stash of gold in his office. The party has Mephistopheles question the Bagworm using Speak to Dead, since he is a trusted face. The Bagworm confirms that Gwyneth is not his master, but did give him the death knight.
