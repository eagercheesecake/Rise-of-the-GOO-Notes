## [[Session 15 - Gray Boys]]
[[Edward Tallow]] mentions that Sister Marie is the most likely successor to [[Pontiff Sulyvahn]].
## [[Session 25 - I Know an Archer]]
[[Moiloc Kodlaksdottër|Moiloc]] has a vision of [[Gwyneth Sootspot|Gwyneth]], [[Mora]], [[Daphne]], and Sister Marie the Confessor in Whitehall. They each appear, one after another, then it repeats.
## [[Session 33 - I Want To See a Mushroom]]
The party learns that Sister Marie has become Queen of Wallachia.
## [[Session 37 - For the People]]
Moiloc and Ishtar down the hall and find a sacrificial altar, where Sister Marie is about to kill Ulysses. The pair are able to defeat her, with the help of the real Tobias, who shows up last-minute. Moiloc is able to convince Tobias and Ishtar to leave and go to safety. Once they are gone, Moiloc sacrifices Ulysses and Marie.
