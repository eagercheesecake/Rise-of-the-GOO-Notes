## [[Session 15 - The Party Has a Meeting]]
Edward is present at the Resistance's planning meeting. [[Moiloc Kodlaksdottër|Moiloc]] is suspicious of him, but can't place why. Edward tells the Resistance that the Cardinal Sphere is planning on electing [[Sister Marie the Confessor]], but they may be able to swing getting [[Isaac Domhnall]] elected, who is more accepting of non-humans.

[[Gwyneth Sootspot|Gwyneth]] lets slip to [[Kaldrake Sootspot|Kaldrake]] that Edward is a mole for Wallachia, placed in the Resistance to take it down.
