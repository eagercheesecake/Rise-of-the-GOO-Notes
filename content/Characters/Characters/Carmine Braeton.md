## [[Session 10 - The Party Gets Bit]]
[[Lothar (Brewster) Nash|Brewster]] tells the party about the Braeton family's rule, which ended about 25 years ago. Around this time, [[King Endrio the Bold|King Endrio]] was assassinated and [[Queen Cypha]] rose to power. Inside the Chateau, Brewster also finds a book saying that in the year of blood, [[Camilla Braeton]] offered house Braeton to a force, and everyone in the Braeton house was sacrificed, except Carmine. The name of the force it was offered to is illegible.

In the cellar of the Chateau, the group finds Camilla and Carmine's coffins. Camilla's is destroyed, and Carmine's is blocked shut with an immovable rod. Carmine is able to kill [[Kaldrake Sootspot|Kaldrake]] and [[Moiloc Kodlaksdottër|Moiloc]], down Brewster, and charm [[Branwyn of Coryphea|Branwyn]] into thinking of him as a friend. However, when going to finish off Branwyn, the charm is broken, and Branwyn kills Carmine by shooting him at close range through the neck.
