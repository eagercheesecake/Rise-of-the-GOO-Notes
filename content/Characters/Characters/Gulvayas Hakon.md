Gulvayas is a drow who seems to be linked to Kaldrake's family and powers. He is repeatedly seen in visions where he kills Martin Sootspot and takes over his form.

- **First Seen:** Session 14
- **Status:** Unknown, likely alive

## [[Session 14 - Gwyneth's Personal Diary]]
[[Kaldrake Sootspot|Kaldrake]] has a vision, taking him into the past where he takes the form of Gulvayas. Gulvayas kills a child [[Martin the Great|Martin the Great]] and his father. He takes a magical mask, a tome belonging to Martin, and bejeweled dagger donning the sigil of the Sootspot family off of Martin's body. Kaldrake gains some of Gulvayas's memories, realizing that he's going to the Aether to study magic, and also realizing that to Gulvayas, the Sootspot family sigil actually belongs to the Hakon family. Thinking of his family, Gulvayas recalls pain, suffering, and the feeling of cold stone.

Later, when Kaldrake mentions this to Belnades, she insists these events never happened. She mentions that the Hakon family is a group of drow known for their cruelty.
## [[Session 15 - Gray Boys]]
[[Gwyneth Sootspot|Gwyneth]] tells Kaldrake that Gulvayas and the Hakon family invaded the Sootspot family castle to get Gulvayas's dagger back. After Kaldrake resets reality and enters one of Gwyneth's portals, he passes out, and dreams he is Gulvayas again, in an ornate office.
## [[Session 16 - Killing a Mailman]]
Kaldrake has another vision of Gulvayas disguising himself as Martin the Great. He meets [[Mora|Morrigan]], and gives her a vial of blue liquid, telling her to drink it when the time comes to prevent [[Baedos|Baedos's]] control. Gulvayas meets with the High Mages and announces his plan to become Archwizard and the Maester of the Aetherium. The High Mages protest, and Morrigan traps them in black vines, and drinks the vial. The Mages coalesce into Gulvayas, and Morrigan falls onto the ground weakly, saying she loved Gulvayas. Gulvayas removes the mask and reveals his true identity.
## [[Session 24 - Charlotte's Back!]]
[[Shurima the Corpsemaker|Shurima]] reveals that he has Gulvayas's blade.
