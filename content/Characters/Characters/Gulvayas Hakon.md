Gulvayas is a drow who seems to be linked to Kaldrake's family and powers. He is repeatedly seen in visions where he kills Martin Sootspot and takes over his form.

- **Status:** Unknown, likely alive

## [[Session 14 - The Party Retcons]]
[[Kaldrake Sootspot|Kaldrake]] has a vision, taking him into the past where he takes the form of Gulvayas. Gulvayas kills a child [[Martin Sootspot]] and his father. He takes a magical mask, a tome belonging to Martin, and bejeweled dagger donning the sigil of the Sootspot family off of Martin's body. Kaldrake gains some of Gulvayas's memories, realizing that he's going to the Aether to study magic, and also realizing that to Gulvayas, the Sootspot family sigil actually belongs to the Hakon family. Thinking of his family, Gulvayas recalls pain, suffering, and the feeling of cold stone.

Later, when Kaldrake mentions this to Belnades, she insists these events never happened. She mentions that the Hakon family is a group of drow known for their cruelty.
