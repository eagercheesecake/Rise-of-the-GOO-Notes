### [[Session 06 - Cult Pamphlets]]
He is seen by Branwyn standing at the castle gate before the feast, emanating an aura of dread. Later, [[Boofoon]] yells that he disrespected them, and spits on his armor. He reacts by immediately killing Boofoon. Later, the man is seen at the pre-game feast. The poisoned pie knocks him out as the guards attack the contestants.
## [[Session 31 - Fourteen Rings]]
The death knight appears again under control of [[The Bagworm]]. After the group kills the Bagworm, he leaves. Using Speak with Dead, the Bagworm reveals that Gwyneth gave him the Death Knight.
## [[Session 34 - The Party Splits]]
The death knight appears to attack the group when Gwyneth leaves them in the main hall of the Capella Sinistra. He kills Puck, but doesn't participate much in the battle besides that.
