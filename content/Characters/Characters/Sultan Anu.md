## [[Session 30 - Discretion]]
[[Hadrian]] shows [[Branwyn of Coryphea|Branwyn]] a tapestry of the sultan, who looks exactly like [[King Aslan]]. He also says that the sultan's rule was won from a military battle, where he partnered with the Lyceum. Since the sultan doesn't have any heirs, the next in line would be his niece.
## [[Session 31 - Fourteen Rings]]
[[Ishtar]] reveals that she and [[Nissa]] are the sultan's adopted daughters, and that Nissa serves as the sultan's bodyguard. When meeting with the sultan, Moiloc tells him that King Aslan is alive, and the sultan remarks that he hopes to kill Aslan himself. He pawns Nissa off on the group to aide them on their adventure, saying she is in need of experience. The sultan gives Brewster a wooden, fleshy ring which he got from the previous sultan. Brewster attempts to get him to promise that he will not instate slavery after taking over Whitehall, and the sultan says slavery tends to make a ruler's reign end quickly.
