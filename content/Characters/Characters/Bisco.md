## [[Session 14 - Gwyneth's Personal Diary]]
Upon entering [[Gwyneth Sootspot|Gwyneth's]] manor, [[Kaldrake Sootspot|Kaldrake]] meets Bisco. He claims to be able to cast Fireball. After Kaldrake explores the house, Bisco answers his questions about the automata.
## [[Session 15 - Gray Boys]]
Gwyneth says that Bisco used to be a wizard, but she transformed him into a demon. She says he can't cast Fireball.
## [[Session 18 - What Would Branwyn Do]]
Bisco is found impaled on the wall of Gwyneth's empty manor, stabbed by a knife from the blacksmith of Kodlak Islands.
