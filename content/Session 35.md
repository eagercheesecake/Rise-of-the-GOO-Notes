### Logline

### Synposis
# Day 63

Brewster and Lucretia leave the sewer. They exit into a cell, with dried blood, bones, and viscera, strewn across the floor. There are two emaciated tigers in the corners of the cell, chewing on bones. Brewster casts Gaseous Form and slips through the cracks of the bars, into a store room full of crates. Brewster peeks into the crates and finds foodstuffs, as well as art. This incudes a gem of brightness, and a necklace of adaptation. Brewster peeks out of the trapdoor and sees a dark kitchen, with one stove, and a lone human chef.

Brewster casts Poison Spray on the chef, immediately killing him. Brewster pats him down, finding a key ring of eight keys, and a bloody locket. Brewster remembers making a locket for his mother when he first started training to be an Artificer. This is the same locket. In the pan the chef was cooking is a hand, wearing Brewster’s mother’s wedding ring. Brewster, upset, takes the ring off the hand, and throws it to the tigers. He unlocks the door, and throws more food in, trying to tame the tigers, but it sets them off, and they attack.

Brewster casts Sanctuary on himself, and runs up into the kitchen. He looks out of a door, and sees a portrait of Vernan Grande. He sees a light coming from a nearby room, and throws the pan against the wall of that room. He then casts Blink as he hears people approaching. Meanwhile, Lucretia hides in a crate.

One of the Inquisitors finds Brewster, who immediately blinds him with, spits on him, dodges his sword attack, Poison Sprays him, and Blinks away. The tiger begins to attack the Inquisitors as Brewster blinks to the other door, which leads to a hallway with a window. The tigers break through the door and starts attacking the Inquisitors. Brewster calls out to Lucretia, saying the coast is clear. She comes up and joins him by the window. They break the window and look out, realizing one of the hallway doors lead outside. They begin to head back to the forge.

Martin and Gwyneth bicker a bit. Gwyneth says she needs Martin’s help to get in contact with Shurima. Martin says neither of them are trustworthy, so he has no reason to do so. Martins asks her to kill Mora, and while she likes the idea, she turns it down. Martin asks to learn more information about their family, but Gwyneth says he isn’t ready for the responsibility of knowing.

Gwyneth mentions the curse that causes the Sootspots to die at 100, and Gulvayas’s knife. She says it was one of Hortensia’s relics. She says Martin, or Gulvayas, cut his soul into pieces to pursue immortality. He put each piece into a homunculus: the Sootspots. They die every hundred years, since after that, the piece of soul returns to Martin. She gives him a book entitled Bados, saying she was the god of fate. Martin wanted the power to shape fate to his desire. However, a body without an entire soul isn’t able to receive divine power and handle it, and he was banished to the astral plane. She wants to be able to get to Martin’s prison on the moon, so she can break the “curse” causing them to die at 100.

Martin still doesn’t see how he can help, so Gwyneth asks for help in Whitehall. She’s looking for a book called the Clotho Lachesis. It lets its wielder detect divine artifacts. Gwyneth says in return, she can help him find Daphne. Martin is shocked that Gwyneth is implying that she doesn’t know where Daphne is. Martin reluctantly agrees, and Gwyneth agrees to give him a Tobias. Gwyneth makes a Tobias. Martin asks about why they took Tobias, and she says that he had a daughter taken by Mora who was killed.

A boom of thunder is heard from the catacombs, and Gwyneth says she has a meeting. She gives Martin an Inquisitor’s medal, saying he can show it to Inquisitors to stop them from bothering him. Martin leaves as Gwyneth says she’ll check in with him later.

Martin grabs Tarren and Puck, and returns to the forge, where he meets back up with Brewster and Lucretia. Brewster grills Martin, who is joking around much too much for his liking after everything that had happened. He receives Puck, and heals Tarren.

Brewster finds a note under the table in the forge, left by Branwyn, saying they were attacked by Tobias, to make sure none of them follow them, and that they’re going to the Watering Shack. Brewster comes back out and says the letter says specifically not to bring any Tobias’s, and Martin says she won’t know. Brewster, fed up with how nonchalant Martin is being, tells him that he left them for dead, clearly made some sort of deal with his sister since he has a Tobias, and only came back with information that Daphne was in the mines, something which Brewster already could have guessed. On their way to the red light district, Brewster tosses Martin’s signet ring into the abyss, saying he’s lost respect for the Sootspot family. Martin loses Brewster and the others soon after arriving in the red light district.

Brewster, Lucretia, and Tarren arrive at the Watering Shack. They fill each other in. Soon after, Martin and Tobias knock on the door. Branwyn looks out the window and sees them. She pokes her head out of door, and asks how Martin has the gall to ignore her instructions and bring a Tobias. They fight for a second, and Martin agrees to send Tobias to the quicksilver mines to wait for them.

Moiloc returns to the monastery to find it burnt and dilapidated. The trees are chopped down, and the monks are dead. A few Tobiases are also dead. Moiloc goes to a couple of living monks tending to the head monk’s corpse, chastising them for waiting for the fight to come to them, and trying to get them to join her in their fight. Mephistopheles offers a song, and Moiloc agrees, saying they need a funeral song. Mephistopheles fails at this miserably. They return to the forge to find it blown up. Moiloc wonders if the others have died, and everyone else seems to agree that they had.

Moiloc uses her amulet to page Reptile, who says he found what they were looking for. Moiloc announces that they should go meet up with Reptile. They proceed to the cathedral, and find the door open. As they arrive, Reptile directs them on how to find them via Moiloc’s amulet.

They go into the main entryway to find blood everywhere, solidifying Moiloc’s belief that her entire party is dead. They proceed to a hallway to their right, following Reptile’s directions. They arrive in a room with a giant magical circle, surrounded by five hooded figures. She sees Reptile hanging in the rafters in the corner. The sigil is in the shape of Shurima’s eye, which Moiloc recognizes as a circle of protection against Shurima and his powers.
